### Key steps

1. Check who can apply: confirm the grant accepts your type of applicant (individual, business or nonprofit).
2. Check what the funds can pay for, and whether the award is cash or in-kind.
3. Note the deadline. Some grants are ongoing and others have fixed windows.
4. Gather documents: ID or residency proof, financial info, project description, budget.
5. Answer every question, attach all materials and keep a copy.
6. Avoid scams: never pay upfront fees for a 'guaranteed' grant.
7. Expect reviews to take weeks or months, and keep applying after a rejection.
