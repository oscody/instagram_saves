# Five free business grant websites to check weekly

The post names five websites where business owners can look for free, non-repayable business grants. It says the funding exists and that the difference comes down to knowing where to look and being ready to apply.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Check grant platforms every week for non-repayable capital, new opportunities and funding alerts.
2. Start with national databases such as grants.gov.
3. Also check local programs, since access exists at both national and local levels.
4. Look at USFundingApplications.org, grantporto.com, helloallis.com and grantwatch.com alongside grants.gov.
5. Build awareness of where grants are listed and readiness to prepare before you apply.
