# ChatGPT Prompts for Birth-Date Self-Reflection

A six-slide carousel that shows four ChatGPT prompt templates. Each one asks ChatGPT to analyze the user's birth date (DD/MM/YYYY) for a personality map, shadow work, a life purpose path, or a self-care plan.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Personalized life map: start with "Using my date of birth [DD/MM/YYYY], create an in-depth psychological and personality map." Ask for strengths, challenges, values, decision-making style, emotional needs and potential internal contradictions. Ask it to connect the patterns and explain how they affect life, relationships and decisions. End by asking for the 5 strengths to build on most, the 5 challenges to work on, and 3 questions to ask yourself.
2. Shadow work: use only the birth date to explore potential blind spots. Ask it to identify internal conflicts, defense mechanisms and patterns that could sabotage relationships or decisions. For each pattern, ask it to explain what triggers the cycle, how you react, what need is behind the reaction, and how to break it. Ask it to show what you might not want to admit, not to label you.
3. Life purpose path: ask for an analysis of the birth date from a personality and self-knowledge angle, not a job recommendation. Ask what kinds of problems you might enjoy solving, what environments could help you grow, what needs to satisfy, and what might leave you feeling empty even when things look fine. Then ask for 3 possible life paths, each with what it would bring, what you would sacrifice, its biggest risk, and how to try it in real life.
4. Real self-care guide: ask for a personalized self-care plan from your birth date, explicitly without generic advice. Ask what helps you feel grounded, regain energy, manage emotions, keep healthy boundaries and feel like you are moving forward. Ask it to separate what recharges you, what stabilizes you and what makes you grow. Then ask for a daily, weekly and monthly plan, plus a protocol for when you feel overwhelmed or losing your way.
