# Questions to ask your child about trust

A six-slide carousel with photos of parents and children, built around the idea that children gradually stop trusting parents when they feel unheard or judged. It lists five questions to ask a child tonight and ends with an offer for a question deck.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Don't assume your child trusts you; ask them directly, tonight.
2. Ask if there is something you said that they have never forgotten, even if you moved on from it.
3. Ask if they feel they have to hide parts of themselves so you won't be disappointed.
4. Ask if, when they make mistakes, they feel safe coming to you or prepare themselves to be judged.
5. Ask what they would say you need to change as a parent if they were completely honest with you.
6. If you wonder what your child isn't saying, don't ignore that feeling; ask the right question, at the right time, in the right tone.
