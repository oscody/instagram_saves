# Hoverboard rotor: 12 magnets cut loading 40%

The creator shows a hoverboard prototype that lifts using eddy currents from spinning magnet rotors, and compares a 16-magnet rotor (R28 mm) with a 12-magnet rotor (R22.5 mm). A chart gives calculated summed magnet loads at 8,000 RPM of about 271 lb and 164 lb, roughly 40% less.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Reduce the rotor from 16 to 12 magnets and place them closer to the center to lower the calculated combined outward loading.
2. At 8,000 RPM, the calculated summed load drops from about 271 lb (16 magnets, R28 mm) to about 164 lb (12 magnets, R22.5 mm), about 40% less.
3. Build sequence shown: print the carrier, fit the magnets, close it up, bolt it on, then repeat for the second rotor.

## Caption

My hoverboard prototype now uses 12 magnets per rotor instead of 16. Smaller rotors, with about 40% less calculated outward loading at the same RPM.
