# Why African families keep starting from zero

A five-slide carousel arguing that families rebuild from scratch because their skills, money and connections are never pooled and nobody organizes the family. It places the responsibility on eldest children and warns that pride and avoiding family problems cause the loss.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. List the family's assets: skills, knowledge, connections, property and experience.
2. Bring those assets together instead of operating like strangers who share a surname.
3. Someone must take responsibility for organizing the family, and eldest children are the ones it usually falls to.
4. Leadership means admitting when you don't know something.
5. Let a younger sibling lead the discussion in an area where they understand better, such as business or finance.
6. Set aside pride, which the post says has destroyed more family wealth than poverty.
7. Do not treat walking away from family problems as independence; the post calls it selfishness.
8. Aim to leave children a family that already knows how to build together.
