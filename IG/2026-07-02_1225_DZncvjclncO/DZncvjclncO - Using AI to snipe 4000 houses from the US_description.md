# Using AI to snipe $4,000 government houses

The carousel claims a five-step way to buy government-seized houses for about $4,000 and rent them to government-subsidized tenants, aiming for $240K a year. Nearly every step is tied to the author's AI tool, Tranchi, which the post offers to send by comment.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Choose a market: the post names Cleveland, Ohio; Detroit, Michigan; and Birmingham, Alabama, citing seized houses at $2K-$5K, low price-to-rent ratios, landlord-friendly laws, low property tax rates, and Section 8 rent levels.
2. Buy a government-seized house through auction listings (the post claims about $4,000 per house).
3. Take a $10K personal loan to cover the purchase and light rehab, then check the property's estimated value (the post says usually around $80K) and find contractors.
4. Refinance with a DSCR loan, which the post says needs no tax returns or W-2, pulling 75% of the new value (its example is $60,000 tax-free cash).
5. Rent the house to a government-subsidized tenant, which the post says covers the rent monthly and yields $500-$800/month cash flow from day one.
6. Reuse the cash to buy more houses and keep stacking until reaching $240K per year (the post's claimed target).
