# AI to snipe $4,000 government houses

A carousel pitching a multi-step plan to buy government-seized houses for about $4,000 through an AI tool, naming Cleveland, Detroit and Birmingham as target cities. It walks through financing with a personal loan, a refinance based on a higher value, and renting to Section 8 tenants.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Use an AI tool to scan auction sites for government-seized houses priced around $2K to $5K.
2. Pick markets with low price-to-rent ratios, landlord-friendly laws, low property taxes and little retail competition: Cleveland, Ohio; Detroit, Michigan; and Birmingham, Alabama.
3. Take a $10K personal loan to cover the purchase and light rehab.
4. Use the tool to estimate what the house is actually worth (the post says around $80K) and find vetted contractors for repairs.
5. Refinance based on the new appraised value to pull out cash.
6. Rent the house to Section 8 tenants, whose rent the post says is above market.
7. Use the cash to buy more houses and repeat.
