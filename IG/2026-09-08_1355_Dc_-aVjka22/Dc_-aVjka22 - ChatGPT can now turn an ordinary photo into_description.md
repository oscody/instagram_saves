## What it teaches

1. Upload your photo to ChatGPT and paste one of the prompts, replacing the bracketed placeholders such as [location], [scene], [outfit] or [destination].
2. Always ask to keep your face, facial structure, skin tone, hairstyle and identity recognizable.
3. Specify lighting, composition, shallow depth of field, background separation, lens perspective and realistic skin texture.
4. DSLR photographer mode: ask for a professional DSLR portrait placed in [location] that looks like a real photographer took it, not an AI image.
5. Cinematic photoshoot: place yourself in [scene] with dramatic but realistic lighting, natural shadows, cinematic composition and professional colour grading.
6. Luxury Instagram photoshoot: put yourself in [luxury location] wearing [outfit], with natural pose and an expensive, editorial look that does not seem overly edited.
7. Travel influencer look: integrate yourself into [destination], matching the lighting, shadows, perspective, weather and clothing to the location.
8. Magazine cover look: ask for a high-end fashion editorial photo with a sophisticated pose, [outfit/style], studio or editorial lighting and believable colour grading.
9. Restore old photos: restore and improve an uploaded photo into a modern portrait while preserving your actual facial identity, as if captured today with a portrait lens.
10. Change your location: keep yourself recognizable but replace the environment with [location], matching lighting, reflections and clothing interaction, as if photographed there with a DSLR.
11. Professional headshot: turn the photo into a headshot suitable for LinkedIn, a company website or a personal brand, with clean lighting and subtle background separation.
12. Golden hour photoshoot: place yourself in [location] with warm natural sunlight, soft highlights and realistic shadows, styled as a genuine golden-hour photograph.
