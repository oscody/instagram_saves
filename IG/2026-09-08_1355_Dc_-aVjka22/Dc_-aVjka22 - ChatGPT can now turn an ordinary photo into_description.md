# ChatGPT Prompts for Professional-Style Photo Edits

A carousel of ChatGPT prompts for turning an uploaded photo into a professional-looking shoot while keeping the subject's face recognizable. The cover promises nine prompts, but this carousel shows five, each with a title and a full prompt, and the last slide repeats the fifth.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Upload a photo to ChatGPT and paste one of the prompts.
2. Replace the bracketed placeholders such as [location], [scene], [destination], [luxury location], [outfit] or [outfit/style] with your own details.
3. Ask ChatGPT to keep your face, facial structure, skin tone, hairstyle and identity recognizable and natural.
4. Be specific about lighting, composition, depth of field, environment, skin texture and clothing, since the caption says that specificity is the key.
5. Ask for a result that looks like a real photograph rather than an AI-generated image.
