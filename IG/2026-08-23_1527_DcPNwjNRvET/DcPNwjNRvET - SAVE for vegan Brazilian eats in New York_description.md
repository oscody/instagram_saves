# Vegan Brazilian eats at Berimbau, New York

A restaurant review of Berimbau's midtown location in New York, listing the vegan dishes and cocktails the author tried, including feijoada, moqueca, and bolo de mandioca. Photos show the dishes, drinks, and a dessert of cake in cream.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## Caption

SAVE for vegan Brazilian eats in New York!✨🇧🇷

Tried Brazilian food for the first time at Berimbau recently and I enjoyed their vegan dishes!🌱

~ everything we tried ~
✨ salada tropicalia 
✨ broccolini w tofu
✨ moqueca vegana w plantains & okra
✨ feijoada vegana (black bean stew & tofu) - this is the national dish in Brazil🇧🇷
✨ bolo de mandioca (most delicious caramelized yuca cake w coconut)
🍸 passion fruit & mezcal cocktails 

The two vegan mains come with rice and farofa to dust on top.. that was interesting! the feijoada was my favorite of them all - the seasonings and smoked tofu combo was so good🔥

Also their midtown location has a a cozy listening bar upstairs and we caught a jazz show before dinner🎶 def recommend for fun vibes and a lil taste of Brazil!💃🏻

📍Berimbau (midtown location)
3 W 36th St., New York, NY
