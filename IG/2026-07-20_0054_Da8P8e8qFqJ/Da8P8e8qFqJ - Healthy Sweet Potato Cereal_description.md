# Healthy Sweet Potato Cereal

A recipe reel for homemade cereal clusters made from roasted sweet potato, rolled oats, butter, honey and vanilla. The caption lists nutrient highlights and per-serving numbers (316 calories, 9g protein, 54g carbs, 7g healthy fats, makes 4).

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Roast the sweet potato at 200°C for 45–60 minutes until completely soft, then scoop out 260g of flesh and mash or blend until smooth.
2. Chill the purée overnight, or for at least 30 minutes, before mixing to reduce moisture for crunchier cereal.
3. Preheat the oven to 180°C and mix the purée with 180g rolled oats, 20g melted butter, 40g raw honey, 1 tablespoon vanilla extract and a pinch of sea salt until thoroughly combined.
4. Press the mixture firmly onto a lined baking tray into a 1–1.5cm thick rectangle.
5. Bake for 20–25 minutes until set and lightly golden.
6. While still warm, break into clusters, drizzle over the remaining 20g honey, toss lightly and spread back onto the tray.
7. Bake for another 15–25 minutes, turning every 5–10 minutes, until deep golden and crisp.
8. Cool completely before storing, since most of the crunch develops as it cools. Serve with milk.

INGREDIENTS:
1 Medium-Large Sweet Potato (260g cooked puree)
180g Rolled Oats (organic,glyphosate-free)
20g Butter, melted
60g Raw Honey (@vilgain - Code MAX)
1 Tbsp Vanilla Extract
Pinch of Sea Salt

METHOD:
1. Roast the sweet potato at 200°C for 45–60 mins until completely soft. Scoop out 260g flesh and mash/blend until smooth.

Pro Tip: Chill the sweet potato purée overnight (or for at least 30 minutes) before mixing. This reduces moisture, meaning you’ll end up with crunchier cereal

2. Preheat oven to 180C. Mix together the sweet potato, oats, butter, 40g honey, vanilla and sea salt until thoroughly combined.
3. Press the mixture firmly onto a lined baking tray into a 1–1.5cm thick rectangle.
4. Bake for 20–25 mins until set and lightly golden.
5. While still warm, break into clusters. Drizzle over the remaining 20g honey, toss lightly and spread back onto the tray.
6. Bake for another 15–25 mins, turning every 5–10 mins, until deep golden and crisp.
7. Cool completely, serve with raw milk and enjoy!

Crunch-Maxing Tip: Roast the sweet potato, press the mixture firmly, and let the cereal cool completely before storing. Most of the crunch develops as it cools.
