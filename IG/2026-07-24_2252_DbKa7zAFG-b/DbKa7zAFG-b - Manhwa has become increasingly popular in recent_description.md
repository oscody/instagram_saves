# Best Manhwa From Each Generation

A carousel recommending manhwa grouped into "Old Gen," "Current Gen," and "New Gen," with each group shown as a grid of titled cover art. The caption also explains how manhwa, manhua, and manga differ by country of origin and print style.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Manhwa are comics from South Korea, usually published in full color, though some are black and white.
2. Manhua are comics from China and often follow a similar format to manhwa.
3. Manga are comics from Japan and are traditionally published in black and white.
4. Identify a work's origin by its country, since calling a manhua a manhwa is incorrect.

## Caption

Manhwa has become increasingly popular in recent years. Many well-known adaptations, such as the K-drama Weak Hero and the anime Tower of God, originated from manhwa.

Manhwa refers to comics from South Korea. They are usually published in full color, though some may be black and white.

There are also other types of Asian comics, such as manhua and manga. Manhua are comics from China and often follow a similar format to manhwa. Manga are comics from Japan and are traditionally published in black and white.

𝗪𝗵𝘆 𝗶𝘀 𝗶𝘁 𝗶𝗺𝗽𝗼𝗿𝘁𝗮𝗻𝘁 𝘁𝗼 𝗱𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴𝘂𝗶𝘀𝗵 𝗯𝗲𝘁𝘄𝗲𝗲𝗻 𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗺?
Knowing the differences helps identify the origin of each work. For example, calling Magic Emperor a manhwa would be incorrect because it is a manhua. While manhwa, manhua, and manga are all Asian comics, they come from different countries and cultures.
