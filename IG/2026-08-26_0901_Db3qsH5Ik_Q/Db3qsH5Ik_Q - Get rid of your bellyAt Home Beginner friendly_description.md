# Beginner At-Home Belly and Core Workout

A short beginner-friendly ab workout reel in which four exercises are matched to different belly concerns. Three people are shown doing the moves on plyo boxes in a gym, with "4x20" on screen.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. If you have a big belly, do bent-over mountain climbers using a bench, 20 reps for 4 sets.
2. If you have fat around your waist, do bent-over side steps, 20 reps for 4 sets.
3. If your lower belly sticks out, do bent-over tilt jumps, 20 reps for 4 sets.
4. To work your whole core, do plank jacks, 20 reps for 4 sets.
