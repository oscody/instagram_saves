# 7 Lines That Start Real Connection

A carousel of seven open-ended questions meant to start deeper conversations, each paired with a short note on what it does. Each slide shows a photo of a young woman in a different setting.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Ask: "What's something you've never told anyone because you thought they wouldn't understand?" It instantly builds emotional trust.
2. Ask: "When you're having a bad day, what do you actually need from someone - advice or company?" It shows maturity and understanding.
3. Ask: "What do you crave most - peace, love, or understanding?" It is thought-provoking and emotionally magnetic.
4. Ask: "What's a small thing that always brings you comfort, no matter what?" It shows you care about what soothes their soul.
5. Ask: "What's one memory that changed you forever." It opens a personal door without feeling forced.
6. Ask about something you wish people noticed about you more. It makes them feel seen and valued instantly.
7. Ask: "When was the last time you truly felt at peace?" It is a deep calm question that invites honesty.
