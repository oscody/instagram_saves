# Five money tips from a 30-year-old creator

A man filmed beside a car shares five money tips he says helped him find financial freedom. He also asks viewers which obstacles they face in building financial stability.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Put your money to work: don't leave it sitting in a savings account. Open an investment account at Vanguard, Charles Schwab, or Ameritrade. He cites an average savings return of 0.24% versus about 10% for an investment account.
2. Follow the 70-30 rule: focus on how much you keep, not how much you earn. Live on 30% of your income and put the other 70% into an account you won't touch until around age 50 or 60. His example: on $1,000, live on $300 and invest $700.
3. Make more money by monetizing social media: pick a topic, study it, and teach it, and brands will find you. He says it won't happen overnight.
4. Change your mindset: don't go to school and work 40 to 50 years for someone else, and avoid being broke.
5. Pay attention to your money: treat it like a partner you need to study, watch over, and nurture, because neglect costs you.
