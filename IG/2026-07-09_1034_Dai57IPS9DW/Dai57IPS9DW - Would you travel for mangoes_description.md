### Key steps

1. The 2026 festival ran July 2–5. The next one is reported for July 1–4, 2027.
2. Mango Mania is a daytime event on the Friday at CHASKA in Cades Bay. Admission was about EC$10 for adults, and children under 12 were free.
3. The Passport Food Tour runs on the Saturday. Guests collect stamps at bars and restaurants, either by guided party bus or on their own. It needs a ticket.
4. Book through the Nevis Tourism Authority or the @nevismangofest account. I couldn't find an official website or ticketing details.
