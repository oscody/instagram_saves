# Seven ChatGPT prompts for jobs and funding

The carousel suggests uploading your CV to ChatGPT and using seven prompts to find matching jobs, scholarships, grants, and fellowships, check your CV against job descriptions, and plan applications. The caption adds that AI can't guarantee results, so deadlines, eligibility, and application details should be checked on official sources.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Upload your CV to ChatGPT before using the prompts.
2. Find matching jobs: ask for 15 roles you're qualified for, with required skills, your matching strengths, and the gaps to fix.
3. Find scholarships: based on your CV, education, location, field, and achievements, ask for scholarships you may qualify for, prioritizing those with no application fee, and an explanation of eligibility criteria.
4. Find grants: ask for grants, fellowships, or funding programs relevant to your background, including eligibility, application deadlines, funding amount, and the official application source.
5. Find hidden opportunities: ask for job boards, scholarship databases, grant directories, fellowships, and organizations where someone with your background could find less commonly advertised opportunities.
6. ATS CV match: paste a job description and ask the AI to act as an ATS recruiter, identify missing keywords and skills, and show how to improve your CV without adding anything that isn't true.
7. Personalized application plan: ask for a 30-day strategy listing which types of jobs, scholarships, grants, and fellowships to target each week and how many applications to submit.
8. Opportunity scout: ask the AI to search the web for currently open jobs, scholarships, grants, and fellowships matching your CV, include only those whose deadlines have not passed, and provide the official application link for each.
9. Verify deadlines, eligibility requirements, and application details through official sources, since AI won't guarantee a job or scholarship.
