# Building a restaurant website with Wegic AI

A creator demos the Wegic AI website builder making a Nigerian restaurant website in minutes from a prompt and a few questions. On screen, the finished site shows a hero banner, signature dish cards with prices and Order Now buttons, and a team section.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Describe your business in a simple prompt to the AI.
2. Answer the AI's questions about your business, such as your menu and customer reviews.
3. Click generate to create the full website with pages, graphics and clickable buttons.
4. To change something, click the section you want to edit and tell the AI what to change, such as your menu.
5. Add a payment system in one click so customers can order.
