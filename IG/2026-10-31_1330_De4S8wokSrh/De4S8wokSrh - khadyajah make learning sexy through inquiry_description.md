# Flirting through inquiry: open-ended questions

A carousel that frames flirting as an inquiry practice, defining inquiry and explaining what makes a good question. It closes with three sample questions to ask someone you're getting to know.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Treat inquiry as the intentional process of asking questions to explore ideas, deepen understanding, and construct meaning, moving past surface-level information.
2. Ask questions that do more than invite an answer: aim for one that gives both people a chance to discover something about themselves and each other.
3. Ask the other person what they imagine for the connection, for example: "when you imagine us continuing to get closer, what does that look like to you?"
4. Ask what kind of place they see you having in each other's lives.
5. Ask what kind of commitment, if any, they are interested in exploring with you.
