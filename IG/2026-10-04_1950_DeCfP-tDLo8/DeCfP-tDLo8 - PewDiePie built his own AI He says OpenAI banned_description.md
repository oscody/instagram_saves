# PewDiePie's self-built Ajax AI and OpenAI ban claims

The carousel reports PewDiePie's claim that his home AI, Ajax (a fine-tuned Qwen 3.5 9B), was deactivated by OpenAI for distillation, restored on appeal, then banned again. It quotes his criticism of OpenAI's data use and notes that OpenAI has not commented.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## Caption

PewDiePie built his own AI. He says OpenAI banned him twice along the way.

His model is called Ajax. It’s a fine-tuned version of Qwen 3.5 9B that runs privately on a home PC and can handle search, browsing, email and your calendar.

In his new video he shows OpenAI’s deactivation email. He says the account was restored on appeal and then banned again. The dispute is over distillation, meaning training an AI on another AI’s answers, which OpenAI’s terms don’t allow for competing models.

His view of OpenAI is pretty clear: ”They worked hard stealing all of our data to make their AI machines.”

OpenAI hasn’t commented.
