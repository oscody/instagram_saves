# Five Stages From Reactive to Self-Authored Identity

The post outlines a five-stage model of identity, moving from a reactive self shaped by the past to a self-authored self that chooses its own beliefs and behaviors. It says most people stay stuck in the first three stages. A closing line offers 1:1 coaching via DM, which is not part of the model.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Stage 1, the reactive self: the past drives your decisions, emotions dictate behavior, and the unfamiliar feels dangerous.
2. Stage 2, the defended self: you build an identity that cannot be hurt, with walls and personas around what you fear to feel.
3. Stage 3, the conditioned self: you shape yourself around approval, expectations and rewards to belong.
4. Stage 4, the observing self: you notice that the voice in your head isn't always you, and observe thoughts, emotions and patterns instead of obeying them.
5. Stage 5, the self-authored self: you choose your own beliefs, standards and behaviors, so your history explains who you became but no longer decides who you become next.
