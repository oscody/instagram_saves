# ChatGPT resume hack for job applications

A man in an orange jacket shares a quick HR tip for job seekers, showing an on-screen "ChatGPT Resume Hack" label. The caption ends with a line promoting an AI automation eBook.

## What it teaches

1. Copy your entire current resume into ChatGPT.
2. Give ChatGPT a prompt asking it to rewrite the resume using talent acquisition AI bot keywords.
3. Include the specific job title you are looking for in the prompt.
4. Add the keywords the bots use into your resume so it is better matched to applicant tracking screening.
