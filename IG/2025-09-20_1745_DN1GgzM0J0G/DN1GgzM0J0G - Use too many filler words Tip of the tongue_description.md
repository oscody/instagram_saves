# Reverse conversations game for verbal fluency

A man in a room with a laptop explains weak word retrieval and verbal fluency, then introduces a one-minute word game called reverse conversations. The on-screen text and captions cover the game's rules and its benefits.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Think of a random sentence.
2. Make the last word of that sentence the first word of the next sentence.
3. Keep chaining sentences this way, with each new sentence starting on the previous one's last word.
4. Do this for one minute straight.

## Caption

Use too many filler words? Tip of the tongue moments? Struggle to articulate your ideas? All can be eased by improving verbal fluency.
