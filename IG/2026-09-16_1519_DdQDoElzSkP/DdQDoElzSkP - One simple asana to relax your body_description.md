# Reclining seated pose for relaxation and digestion

A short clip shows a reclining seated pose done on a bed with pillows, while the narration says it helps gastritis, flatulence and indigestion. On-screen text makes a claim about getting 8 hours of sleep in 10 minutes, and the caption calls it a simple asana to relax the body.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Sit with the hips resting on the heels, with stacked pillows behind you on the bed.
2. Lean back slowly onto the pillows.
3. Let the arms rest overhead or extended, with the head on the pillow.
4. Stay in the pose for about five minutes, as the narrator suggests.
