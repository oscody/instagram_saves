# 15 Content Formats for Game Wishlists

A game developer presents 15 content formats meant to grow game wishlists, with on-screen examples of behind-the-scenes footage, a sound design showcase, and character creation. The caption invites viewers to borrow the ideas for their own posts.

## What it teaches

1. Post developer diary content
2. Show gameplay mechanics
3. Share behind-the-scenes footage
4. Showcase the game engine
5. Post progress updates
6. Share time-lapse development clips
7. Show sound design, e.g. weapon swings
8. Show character creation
9. Share how the team works (our process)
10. Post gameplay illustrations
