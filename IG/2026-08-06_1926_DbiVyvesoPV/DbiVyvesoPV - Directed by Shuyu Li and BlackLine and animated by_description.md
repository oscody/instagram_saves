# First three episodes of False Memory now available

An anime news graphic announcing that the first three episodes of the donghua False Memory are streaming on Bilibili, released August 2, 2026. It lists the studios, genres, original creator, director, chief animator and a synopsis of Guan Chao's quest to alter the past.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## Caption

Directed by Shuyu Li and BlackLine, and animated by Flint Sugar and Studio Tumble, the series follows Guan Chao on his desperate quest to alter the past. His search for the powerful "Burning Stone" to activate his Time Gun goes awry when he accidentally plunges into the Memory Management Bureau. To escape and return to reality, Guan Chao must team up with memory administrators Fang Tang and Bai Chong to track down and recover "bugged" individuals trapped across different layers of memory, facing deep mysteries with every battle he fights.
