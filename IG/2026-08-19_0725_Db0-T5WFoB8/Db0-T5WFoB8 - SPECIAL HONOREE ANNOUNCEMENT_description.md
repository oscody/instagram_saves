✨ SPECIAL HONOREE ANNOUNCEMENT ✨

We’re excited to celebrate Mike and Nicole Nicholas, Co-Founders of @auntsetuncles as Special Honorees at the Legacy 100™ Brunch!

This husband-and-wife duo built one of Brooklyn’s most celebrated plant-based spaces: a cafe, bar, and concept shop that opened in Flatbush at the height of the pandemic. Blending their 2 decade plus experience and and backgrounds in streetwear design, music, and hospitality, Mike (a Flatbush native) and Nicole (originally from Toronto) created a true cultural oasis: a “living room” for their community, stocked with indie magazines, curated books, and clothing alongside a soulful, Caribbean-inflected vegan menu.

Through consistent community-forward programming, Mike and Nicole have turned Aunts et Uncles into a vital third space, one that champions Black culture, clean living, and economic revitalization right in their own neighborhood.

Join us as we honor their legacy of community-building and creative entrepreneurship during Black Business Month!
