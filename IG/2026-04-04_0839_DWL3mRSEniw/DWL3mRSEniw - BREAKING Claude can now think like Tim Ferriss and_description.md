## What it teaches

1. Prompt 1, Find your unfair advantage: First give Claude your skills, interests, work experience and personality. Apply the 80/20 rule to find the 20% of skills producing 80% of your results. Find skill combinations few others can replicate. Explain the market premium each combination could command. Rank them by income potential and lifestyle compatibility. Rules: combinations beat single skills; each advantage must connect to a real market willing to pay; lifestyle compatibility is a filter.
2. Prompt 2, Find your leverage gaps: First give Claude what you currently do and how you earn. Apply the DEAL framework (Define, Eliminate, Automate, Liberate). Separate tasks only you can do from tasks that can be outsourced or automated. Map where a muse business or scalable income stream fits your skills. Build an action plan to reclaim time. Rules: busy work is flagged directly; every automation or delegation step must be executable now; the plan should free at least 10 hours per week.
3. Prompt 3, See where your career leads: First give Claude your current role, income structure and weekly time commitment. Classify each major activity as freedom-building, skill-building or time-for-money trap. Calculate a freedom ratio showing how much of your work compounds versus consumes your time. Apply fear-setting: the realistic worst case of changing versus staying the same. Compare the next 5 years on the current path versus a redesigned path. Rules: be direct about a time-for-money trap; freedom-building counts only if it reduces required hours over time; fear-setting must include a realistic downside.
4. Prompt 4, Build a wealth strategy: First give Claude your background, skills and ideal lifestyle. Identify your highest-leverage skill set and the muse business models that fit it. Map scalable income streams such as productized services, digital products, licensing or equity. Build a 10-year roadmap: years 1-2 muse launch, years 3-5 automation, years 6-10 ownership. Flag the single most important move for the next 90 days. Rules: traditional career advancement is excluded unless it builds toward freedom; every income stream must scale without proportional time input; the 90-day move must be executable with current resources and zero permission.
