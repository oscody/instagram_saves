# 人活着是为了什么

A man in a black puffer jacket and green tie talks to the camera about life, with on-screen text saying people who died in the morning may still have been planning for the evening and "let me tell you a little secret." The caption calls it the best answer to the question of life's purpose.

## What it teaches

1. Don't keep postponing your life until later, whether that means waiting for free time, waiting for money, or waiting for the future.
2. Remember that nobody knows what happens next, since people can die at any moment.
3. Don't let too much love and too many chances pass you by while you wait.
