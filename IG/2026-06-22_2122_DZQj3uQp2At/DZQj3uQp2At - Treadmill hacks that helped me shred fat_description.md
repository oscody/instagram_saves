# Treadmill hacks that helped me shred fat

Shows a before and after physique comparison, then an on-screen Tier 2 treadmill workout with timed speed and incline changes. The caption explains the goal of burning as many calories as possible while in a calorie deficit.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Stay in a caloric deficit, since the goal is to burn as many calories as possible within it.
2. If you are already in a calorie deficit, burning an extra 300-600 calories on the treadmill on top of that should help you shred fat faster.
3. Start with Tier 1, which the caption calls more beginner friendly, and move up to Tier 2 once Tier 1 is achievable.
4. Tier 2 (45 minutes total): 5 minutes at 3 speed with 8 incline.
5. Tier 2, continued: 10 minutes at 3.5 speed with 12.5 incline.
6. Tier 2, continued: 10 minutes at 3 speed with 15 incline.
7. Tier 2, finish: 20 minutes at 3 speed with 12.5 incline.
