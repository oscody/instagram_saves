# Using Claude to find and score job listings

A short reel showing Claude (Sonnet 4.6) building a job dashboard from an uploaded resume, with listings for companies such as Razorpay, Zepto and Google, each given a fit score out of 100 and a probability label.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Open Claude and upload your resume.
2. Turn on the web search option.
3. Add the job-search prompt and send it (the prompt text is not shown in the post).
4. Claude builds a dashboard of matching jobs, each with a fit score out of 100.
5. Apply only to jobs scoring 75 or higher; skip those below 75%.
