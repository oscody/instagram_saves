## What it teaches

1. Look for jobs on LinkedIn, and for remote jobs also use Remote Jobs (Remote.co), FlexJobs and WeWork Remotely.
2. If your resume keeps getting no replies, use ChatGPT to refine it and LinkedIn to reach out to the hiring manager.
3. To prepare for an interview, use Google's free interview warm-up tool.
4. To build a portfolio for an interview, use ReadyMag, Canva or Adobe Portfolio.
5. After getting an offer, use levels.fyi to negotiate it.
