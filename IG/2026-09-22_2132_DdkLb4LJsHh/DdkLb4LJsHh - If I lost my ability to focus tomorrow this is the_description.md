# Eight-step system to rebuild deep focus

A reel presenting an eight-step routine for getting into focused work sessions, with the creator on screen holding a countdown timer and headphones. It is also a paid partnership with a headphone brand.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Scan your workspace and clear every visible distraction.
2. Write one simple objective on paper before starting.
3. Set a time limit for the session, using Parkinson's law (work expands to fill the time given).
4. Close any browser tabs you are not using.
5. Turn your phone on Do Not Disturb and keep it out of the room.
6. Remove background noise, for example with brown noise.
7. Begin the session.
