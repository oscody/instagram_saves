# Michael Jackson, Qigong and mastery through sincerity

A talking-head reel arguing that Michael Jackson's dance drew on qigong-style breath, visualization and intention, with a clip of the creator's young son doing tai chi as a training example. The reel also refers to a training link offered by comment.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Qigong is the coordination of breath, visualization and body mechanics.
2. Give the sensation and the breath a color to make it easier to feel.
3. Give your attention to a part of the body, such as the fingertips, and move it through the body with intent.
4. Watch where the mind extends or gets stuck in the body, and notice how it shows up in movement.
5. Practice breathing and visualizing through each movement, rather than copying the external form alone.
6. Program yourself deliberately with affirmations and personal mantras, since everyone programs themselves either way.
