# Quantum jumping as brain rehearsal and predictive processing

The post describes "quantum jumping" as a neuroscience idea rather than travel between parallel universes. It says that mentally rehearsing a different future, combined with real action, can update the brain's expectations through neuroplasticity.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Vividly imagine performing an action; motor imagery can activate some of the same neural networks as doing it.
2. Repeatedly rehearse a different response to something you normally avoid, before you encounter it.
3. Combine that mental rehearsal with real action so your brain receives new information.
4. Let the new information update your brain's expectations; repeated learning can change neural connections (neuroplasticity).
