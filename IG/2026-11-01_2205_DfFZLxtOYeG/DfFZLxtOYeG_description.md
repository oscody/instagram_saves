# Copy-paste Claude prompt for a faceless digital business

The post shares a full prompt to paste into Claude AI for planning a faceless digital product business. It asks for niches, a product built with Claude and Canva, a 30-day launch plan, a faceless social content strategy, a sales funnel, free automation tools, and a roadmap to £10K/month.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Tell Claude you are a complete beginner building a faceless digital product business with AI.
2. State your limits: a small budget and 1–2 hours of work per day.
3. Ask Claude to act as an experienced digital business strategist.
4. Ask for 5 profitable niches where people are actively trying to solve a problem.
5. Ask Claude to pick one digital product you can make with Claude and Canva.
6. Ask for a 30-day action plan to launch your first product.
7. Ask for a faceless Instagram and TikTok content strategy to attract buyers organically.
8. Ask for a simple sales funnel that turns viewers into customers.
9. Ask how to automate repetitive tasks with free tools.
10. Ask for a realistic roadmap to £10K/month, including sales targets, conversion rates and potential challenges.
11. End the prompt by asking Claude to question you about your skills and interests before it builds the plan.
