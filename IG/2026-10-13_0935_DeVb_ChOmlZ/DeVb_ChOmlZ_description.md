# Six defense-sensing engineering builds for your desk

A reel listing six DIY sensing projects that mimic "senses" used in defense engineering, such as a laser warning receiver and an underwater acoustic modem, with rough parts costs. Frames show a laser warning receiver's six-photodiode ring layout and an acoustic modem's two piezo transducers with tones for 1 and 0.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Laser warning receiver: arrange six photodiodes around a ring, each feeding a fast AC-coupled amplifier so sunlight is ignored while laser pulses are caught; the sector hit by a pointer lights up.
2. Underwater acoustic modem: use two potted piezo transducers, one emitting a tone for 1 and one for 0, and a detector that reads the bits from the echoes through water.
