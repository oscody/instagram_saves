# Google Career Dreamer walkthrough for career planning

A screen-recorded walkthrough of Google's free Career Dreamer tool, showing how to enter a role, pick tasks and skills, and explore career paths. The narrator uses web developer as the example path, covering its salary, education, daily routine, growth areas and upskilling resources.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Open Career Dreamer and click start.
2. Describe a current or dream role, and choose a field (the narrator typed computer engineer and picked AI).
3. Select the tasks you have done in that role.
4. Select the skills you are good at.
5. Read the career identity statement the tool creates.
6. Review the suggested career paths and click explore paths.
7. Open a path to see its average salary, education needed, and sweet spot section on what to focus on.
8. Scroll down to see a day in the life of the role and areas of growth.
9. Follow the link to Google AI skills and click learn more on a resource for your domain.
