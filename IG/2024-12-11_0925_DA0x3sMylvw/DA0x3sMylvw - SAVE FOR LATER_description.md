# ETF cheat sheet for major stock indices

A table listing ETF tickers from Vanguard, iShares by BlackRock, State Street, and Charles Schwab for categories such as global, US, ex-US, S&P 500, growth, dividend, value, small-cap, and mid-cap stocks. The caption says it is meant for finding ETFs that cover specific areas of the market; Schwab has no listed global stocks ETF.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Find the market category you want in the left-hand column, such as US Stocks or Dividend.
2. Read across that row to the column for the provider you use: Vanguard, iShares, State Street, or Charles Schwab.
3. The ticker in that cell is the ETF listed for that category by that provider.
