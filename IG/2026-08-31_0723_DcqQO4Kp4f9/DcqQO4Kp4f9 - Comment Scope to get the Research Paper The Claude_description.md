# Turning an AI-fiction research paper into a Claude skill

The reel describes how the creator turned a research paper on AI-written fiction (61,000 stories, a detector from Maryland and Google DeepMind) into a reusable Claude skill called "human scope," shown in clips of a grant proposal chat and a slash-command skill picker.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Paste the full research paper into a Claude chat.
2. Ask Claude to treat every finding as a rule it must never break while writing.
3. Open the skill creator in the same chat and turn those rules into a permanent skill.
4. Name the skill (the post uses "human scope").
5. Before any writing task, invoke the skill with a slash command so Claude applies the rules.
6. Rules to follow from the findings: avoid over-explaining the story's meaning (the post says AI over-explained stories 77% of the time), avoid stories that are too clean, and leave some tension unresolved rather than spelling everything out.
7. Watch model-specific habits as the post describes them: Claude rarely lets tension build, GPT reaches for gossip, and Gemini leans toward dark settings.
