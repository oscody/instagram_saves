# Nine steps from a billion-pound entrepreneur book

A street interview in which a man presents the nine steps of a book he calls "How to Make a Billion in Nine Steps." The on-screen text reads "online, you," "for revolution," and "this is about."

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Copy and pivot.
2. Get an investor to help your business scale.
3. Get a coach and a mentor, and learn from others.
4. Use an omni-channel model: don't rely only on being online; have stores or pop-up stands too.
5. Hire your replacement: hire someone better than you to run the day-to-day business.
6. Go global with locals: hire a local person to head up your business in each country.
7. Evolve, don't revolt: revolution is for the start of a business, after that keep evolving.
8. Keep a not-to-do list, which matters more than a to-do list.
9. Hone your character: stop doing what you're bad at, focus on what you're good at, and hire others for the rest.
