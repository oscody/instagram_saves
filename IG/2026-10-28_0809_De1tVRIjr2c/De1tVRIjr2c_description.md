## What it teaches

1. Use a different visual style for each AI video or motion graphics project instead of one look for everything.
2. Open the prompt by naming the style and medium, as in 'Neo-expressionist graffiti painting'.
3. Describe the subject in concrete detail: shape, colors, facial features, and clothing or parts.
4. Name the materials and brushwork, such as oil stick, spray paint, and scratchy doodles.
5. Specify the background and palette, such as bright yellow, pale pink, and sky blue with paint drips.
6. End the prompt with the aspect ratio, such as vertical 3:4.
