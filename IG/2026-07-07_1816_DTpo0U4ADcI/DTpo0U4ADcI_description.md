### Key steps

1. In OBS, right-click the webcam source and open Properties, then Configure Video.
2. Turn off auto exposure, then lower exposure (reel says about -7; the value varies by camera and lighting).
3. Apply the change.
4. Raise gain until your face is clearly visible.
5. Turn off auto white balance and set it manually while watching your skin tone in the preview.
6. The reel names one more setting that the audio doesn't make clear. Raise it slightly.
7. Raise saturation a little, and compare against your original image.
