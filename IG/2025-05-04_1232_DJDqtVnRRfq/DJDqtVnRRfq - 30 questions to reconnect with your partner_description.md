# 30 questions to reconnect with your partner

Three frames show a couple sharing a picnic table outdoors, with on-screen text promising 30 questions that connect partners deeper. The caption lists the 30 prompts, from "What made you feel loved this week?" to "What's one moment with me you'll never forget?"

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Pick one of the 30 questions to reconnect with your partner.
2. Say "Let's do this one tonight" to start the conversation.
3. Slow down and go deeper with the question, rather than keeping it light.

## Caption

30 questions to reconnect with your partner:

1. What made you feel loved this week?

2. What do you need more of from me lately?

3. When did you feel closest to me recently?

4. What’s something I used to do that you miss?

5. Is there anything you’re scared to tell me?

6. What’s one thing you’re proud of us for?

7. What’s your favorite way I show you love?

8. What kind of connection do you want to deepen?

11. What’s one thing you’re afraid of in relationships?

12. How do you feel about how we handle conflict?

13. When do you feel most supported by me?

14. What are you hoping our next chapter looks like?

15. What’s a memory you go back to when you miss me?

16. What’s something small I do that you love?

17. What kind of intimacy do you crave more of?

18. What do you think makes us a good team?

19. What’s something about me that surprised you?

20. How has your view of love changed with me?

21. What’s your favorite part of our relationship?

22. What does safety in a relationship mean to you?

23. What’s something we’ve grown through together?

24. What do you want to create together this year?

25. When did you last feel truly heard by me?

26. What do you think is our love language as a couple?

27. How can I help you feel more secure with us?

28. What do you want us to be better at as a team?

29. What do you admire about me most?

30. What’s one moment with me you’ll never forget?

You don’t have to wait until things feel off to reconnect.

These 30 questions aren’t just for “deep talks”—they’re for reminding each other why you’re still choosing this love, even in the hard moments.

Whether you’re long-distance or living together, pick one and say: “Let’s do this one tonight.”

Slow down. Go deeper. Stay rooted in love that grows.
