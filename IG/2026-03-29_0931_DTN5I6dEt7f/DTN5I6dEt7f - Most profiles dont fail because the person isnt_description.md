# Six ChatGPT prompts to optimize a LinkedIn profile

A man sits with a laptop on a stand, and on-screen text promises to optimize a LinkedIn profile in 30 minutes using six ChatGPT prompts. The caption says recruiters spend 6–10 seconds on a profile and lists the six prompts.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Use each prompt exactly as written.
2. Headline: "Rewrite my LinkedIn headline to clearly show what I do, who I help, and the outcome without buzzwords."
3. About section: "Rewrite my About section as a short story that shows my impact, not my responsibilities."
4. Work experience: "Convert my work experience into achievement-based bullets with numbers, outcomes, and tools used."
5. Keywords: "Optimize my profile keywords so I appear in recruiter searches for my target role."
6. Featured section: "Rewrite my Featured section to position me as someone who solves problems, not someone who's job-hunting."
7. Audit: "Audit my profile like a recruiter and tell me why I would or wouldn't get shortlisted."
