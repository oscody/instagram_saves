# Running an AI advisory council in one chat

A short reel presenting a single-chat prompt that makes Claude answer a decision from five named advisor roles, who review each other's answers anonymously, before a chairman gives a final call and next steps. The on-screen slides list the contrarian ("what could fail") and chairman ("weigh the perspectives, make the call") roles.

## What it teaches

1. Ask Claude to answer your question from five advisor perspectives: a contrarian who looks at what could fail, a first-principles thinker who breaks down assumptions, an expansionist who finds the upside you're missing, an outsider who knows nothing about your industry, and an executor focused on what gets done on Monday morning.
2. Have each advisor review the other advisors' answers without knowing which advisor wrote which one.
3. Have a chairman weigh all the perspectives and give a final call with clear next steps.
