# New MoMI LAB opens in Astoria, Queens

The Museum of the Moving Image in Astoria, Queens, has opened MoMI LAB, a free 2,400-square-foot artist-led studio with hands-on technology displays, including the robot dog Sparky. The post also lists hours, ticket prices and the museum's first Artist Residency Program.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## Caption

NEW EXPERIENCE IN NYC: MoMI LAB! 🤖✨

One of NYC’s coolest new educational spaces has officially opened! #momipartner

Museum of the Moving Image has unveiled MoMI LAB—a FREE, 2,400-square-foot artist-led co-creation and educational studio where visitors of all ages can explore the technologies shaping our everyday lives through hands-on, interactive experiences.

🎨 Turn AI prompts into images and videos
🥽 Step into immersive virtual reality worlds
🖨️ Watch digital designs come to life through 3D printing
⚙️ Experiment with sensors to discover how robots navigate the world
🌈 See how projection mapping transforms ordinary surfaces into works of art
🤖 Plus, meet Sparky—the museum’s adorable robot dog!

The opening also marks the launch of MoMI’s first-ever Artist Residency Program, where visitors can watch artists create in real time, join workshops, and get a behind-the-scenes look at how art and technology come together.

And the fun doesn’t stop there! Museum of the Moving Image is already one of NYC’s most unique museums, featuring the beloved Jim Henson Exhibition, iconic movie props and costumes, vintage arcade games, immersive exhibits, and hands-on experiences celebrating film, television, video games, and digital media.

Whether you’re raising a future filmmaker, gamer, animator, or simply looking for a fun day out, this Queens gem deserves a spot on your NYC bucket list. 🎬✨

WHERE: 📍36-01 35 Ave, Astoria, NY. (MoMI LAB is located on the Museum’s Lower Level).
WHEN: ⏰Thurs 2 pm – 6 pm, Fri 2–8 pm, Sat & Sun: 11 – 6 pm. MoMI Lab is free.
TICKETS: 🎟️Adult $20, Youth (age 3-17) $10. Free general admission is offered every Thursday from 2–6 pm.
GOOD TO KNOW: Be sure to check the calendar! MoMI offers a wide range of amazing programming - including movie screenings and events for families.
