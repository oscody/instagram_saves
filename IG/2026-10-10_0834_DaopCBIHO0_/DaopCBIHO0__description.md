# Distribution beats product quality in business

A podcast clip tells the Western Union story, where a competitor was bought out after it kept competing in one city. It argues that market availability and distribution matter more than having the best product, with marketing following after.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Distribution and availability matter more than product quality; the most available product wins, not the best one.
2. Marketing comes after distribution, but it still matters, because people have to know about a product for it to count.
3. If a competitor already holds a market you want, compete with them hard until they buy you out or you grow bigger than them, instead of building something new.
