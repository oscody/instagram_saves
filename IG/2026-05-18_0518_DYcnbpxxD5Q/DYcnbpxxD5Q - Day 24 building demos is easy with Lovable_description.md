### Key steps

1. Go to lovable.dev and sign up.
2. Paste a prompt describing the business site, such as a dark-themed barber shop site with services, pricing, gallery and booking. The creator's exact prompt is only available by commenting 'prompt' on the reel.
3. Upload the business's own images.
4. Publish the site to preview it, and share the demo link or show it on a call.
5. Custom domains need a paid plan. Connect one in the publish settings, then add the DNS records Lovable shows.
6. Pitch the demo to local businesses and offer to hand over the site.
