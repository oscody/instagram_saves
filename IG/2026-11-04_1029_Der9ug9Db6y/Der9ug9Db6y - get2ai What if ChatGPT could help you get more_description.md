# Six ADHD-friendly ChatGPT prompts for focus

A carousel of six ChatGPT prompt templates for task overload, low focus, and task-switching. The caption says there is no official "ADHD Focus Mode" in ChatGPT, so the prompts are meant to build your own version.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Don't ask ChatGPT just to 'help me be productive'. Say how you want the task broken down, how long you can focus, what usually distracts you, and what needs to be finished.
2. Task paralysis breaker: replace [TASK] in 'I'm stuck staring at [TASK]...' and ask for tiny steps under one minute each, only the first step, an explanation of how to begin, and waiting until you finish before giving the next one.
3. Dopamine menu: ask for a personalized menu of 5-minute appetizers (quick, energizing activities), 20-minute main courses (focused work sessions), and 10-minute side dishes (creative or enjoyable breaks), kept realistic and easy to follow.
4. Virtual body double: ask ChatGPT to act as an accountability partner for 30 minutes, define a clear goal for the session, guide you through short work intervals, and assess your progress and pick the next action each time you return with an update.
5. Context-switching reset: when moving from [TASK A] to [TASK B], ask for a simple 3-minute reset routine that closes the first task, clears distractions, and starts the second, giving one action at a time.
6. Boring-task gamifier: describe the boring [TASK] and your [INTERESTS], then ask for a simple game with a mission, small milestones, a points system, and a meaningful reward, without making the task more complicated.
7. Time-blindness check: estimate how long [PROJECT] will take, then ask ChatGPT to break it into subtasks, give a realistic time for each, name three steps you might overlook (setup, interruptions, review time), and suggest a practical deadline.
