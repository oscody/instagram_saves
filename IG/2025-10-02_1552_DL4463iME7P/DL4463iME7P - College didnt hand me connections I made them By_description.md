# Three networking steps from a college internship story

A creator describes how college conversations, a trip with a billionaire mentor, and a side project led to an internship. The post is organized as three numbered steps on networking.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Step 1: Start the conversation. Put yourself in the right rooms and interact with people you want to be like and learn from. Stay in places with connected people, and keep listening, learning, and showing up.
2. Step 2: Be someone worth respecting. Be a person of your word, carry yourself with purpose, and treat people with respect. Give a genuine compliment, ask a good question, make eye contact, and be genuinely curious. Listen.
3. Step 3: Lead with value. Bring something useful, such as ideas, a platform, or execution, instead of only asking for mentorship. Show what you can build.
4. Believe in yourself enough to put yourself out there, even when it is uncomfortable.
