# Learning Robotics Basics in 30 days

A reel laying out a four-stage plan for learning robotics from zero in 30 days. Shown with a circuit diagram for the electronics stage and footage of the author's robot arm setup.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Spend a few days on electronics basics used in robotics: voltage, GPIO, PWM, motors, and sensors. Start with Paul McWaters' Arduino Series.
2. Get hands on with a real robot arm (the post names the WaveShare robot arm). Follow its tutorials, move each joint yourself, and start changing the example code.
3. Work through the beginner ROS2 tutorials. Learn nodes, topics, services, and actions, then connect ROS back to the arm.
4. Make the robot complete one simple task entirely on your own. The author's example is picking up small styrofoam cubes.
