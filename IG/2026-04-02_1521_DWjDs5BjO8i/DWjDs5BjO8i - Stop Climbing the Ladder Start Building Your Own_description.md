# Four Claude prompts for redesigning your career

A four-slide set of Claude prompt templates, each with #ROLE, #TASK, #STEPS, #RULES and #OUTPUT sections, for finding a career's unfair advantage, leverage gaps, freedom path and 10-year wealth strategy. The last slide is an ad for a paid AI masterclass.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Prompt 1, Find your unfair advantage: Give Claude your skills, interests, work experience and personality first. Find the 20% of skills producing 80% of your results. Find the rare skill intersection few others can replicate. Explain the market premium each combination could command. Rank by income potential and lifestyle fit. Rules: skill combinations beat single skills; every advantage must connect to a real market willing to pay; lifestyle fit is a filter.
2. Prompt 2, Find your leverage gaps: Give Claude what you currently do and how you earn. Apply the DEAL framework (Define, Eliminate, Automate, Liberate). Separate tasks only you can do from tasks that can be outsourced or automated. Map where a muse business or scalable income stream fits your skills. Build an action plan to reclaim time. Rules: flag busywork directly; every automation or delegation step must be executable now; the plan should free at least 10 hours a week.
3. Prompt 3, See where your career leads: Give Claude your current role, income structure and weekly time commitment. Classify each major activity as freedom-building, skill-building or time-for-money trap. Calculate your freedom ratio, meaning how much work compounds versus consumes your time. Apply fear-setting by comparing the worst case of changing with the worst case of staying the same. Compare the next 5 years on the current path versus a redesigned path. Rules: be direct about a time-for-money trap; freedom-building counts only if it reduces required hours over time; fear-setting must use realistic downside.
4. Prompt 4, Build a wealth strategy: Give Claude your background, skills and ideal lifestyle. Identify your highest-leverage skill set and the muse business models that fit it. Map scalable income streams such as productized services, digital products, licensing or equity. Build a 10-year roadmap: years 1-2 muse launch, years 3-5 automation, years 6-10 ownership. Flag the single most important move for the next 90 days. Rules: traditional career advancement is excluded unless it builds toward freedom; every income stream must scale without proportional time input; the 90-day move must be executable with current resources and zero permission.
