# Target job search with Claude using your CV

A walkthrough of using Claude with a CV attached to work out which company types and role titles best match your skills. It shows an example output with transferable selling points, strongest-match company types, and suggested job titles to search for.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Open a new chat in Claude and attach your CV.
2. In the prompt, state your target field, whether you are switching careers or staying in your field, and your location or preferred market (e.g. London, remote, UK-wide).
3. Ask Claude to be honest about where you have realistic chances and where you are stretching.
4. Ask for 4-6 transferable skills or experiences from your CV, with why each is valuable to employers in your target field.
5. Ask for the specific types of companies (industry, size) where you have the highest chance, and for each one explain why you are a strong fit, which role to lead with in applications, and which role titles to search actively.
6. Use the breakdown to build a priority list of companies and roles to target, rather than applying broadly.
