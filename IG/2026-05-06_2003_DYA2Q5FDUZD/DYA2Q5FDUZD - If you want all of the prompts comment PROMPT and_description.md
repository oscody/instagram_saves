# Four Claude prompts for resume and job search

A carousel telling how a resume made with Claude scored 90% but got about a 10% interview rate, then showing four prompts for analyzing a resume, checking fit for a role, finding people to contact on LinkedIn, and writing a resume and cover letter.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Prompt 1: Ask Claude to act as a senior resume writer, analyze your resume top to bottom, identify your top skills and experiences, and recommend jobs to apply for.
2. Prompt 1 (continued): For each recommended job, have Claude give an interview rate percentage and 5 real job links posted in the last 24 hours.
3. Prompt 2: Ask Claude to act as a recruiter for a role you want, with the job description attached as a PDF, and say whether you would get an interview and why or why not.
4. Prompt 2 (continued): Have Claude evaluate skills and qualifications match, relevant experience and impact, keywords and ATS alignment, and overall fit for the role and company, then give how to get an interview and key resume tweaks.
5. Prompt 3: Ask Claude to act as a networking strategist and find 5 recruiters, hiring managers, or hiring team members to connect with on LinkedIn, using the job description PDF.
6. Prompt 3 (continued): For each person, have Claude write a LinkedIn message asking about the job and explaining how your skills and experiences fit the role.
7. Prompt 4: Ask Claude to act as a top 1% job seeker and create a resume and cover letter for your target role using your listed skills.
8. Prompt 4 (continued): Build the resume with a professional summary, core skills, experience bullets with quantified achievements, education, and certifications and tools; build the cover letter with a header, a body that names your skills and results, and a closing.
