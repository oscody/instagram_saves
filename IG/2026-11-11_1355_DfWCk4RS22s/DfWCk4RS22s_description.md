# How to focus when you have no focus

A tongue-in-cheek reel with meme-style visuals giving tips for focusing when distracted and overwhelmed. It covers a wall-staring reset, a stripped-down desk setup with one task on one sticky note, and going to bed earlier with fewer screens and less caffeine.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Feeling unable to start with too many tasks is not a character flaw; the brain is used to scrolling and wants dopamine.
2. Sit facing a wall for 10 minutes with no music, no phone and nothing else, even though it feels painful.
3. Set up the desk like for a school test: no to-do list and no unnecessary browser tabs.
4. Choose one task and write it on one sticky note, then cross it off when done.
5. For endurance, go to bed 30 minutes earlier, use screens less, and cut caffeine before bed.
6. Avoid an all-or-nothing mindset: start where you can, show yourself compassion instead of self-judgment, and focus on progress over time.
