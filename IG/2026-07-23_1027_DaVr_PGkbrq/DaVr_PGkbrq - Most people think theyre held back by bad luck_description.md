# Carl Jung's Method for Making Wishes Come True

The carousel presents a Carl Jung-attributed approach to wishes, framed as a 10-day practice that works even for skeptics. It argues that desires take hold through a clearly stated wish and a present-tense identity, not through positive thinking.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Practice the method for 10 days.
2. Name the wish clearly by asking: "What would I want if I stopped pretending I didn't?" Choose what is true, not what sounds realistic or what impresses others.
3. Write the wish in the present tense as an identity statement, for example "I live calmly. My body feels grounded. My mind feels clear." rather than "I want peace."

## Caption

Most people think they’re held back by bad luck.

Carl Jung believed something far more unsettling.

The biggest obstacle isn’t the world around you.

It’s the invisible part of you that’s still protecting an old identity.

You say you want success.

Yet you procrastinate.

You say you want confidence.

Yet you avoid opportunities that could change your life.

You say you want freedom.

Yet you keep choosing what’s familiar over what’s possible.

Jung called this unconscious loyalty.

Hidden beliefs, old fears, and deeply rooted identities quietly shape your decisions long before you’re aware of them.

You don’t rise by forcing yourself to think more positively.

You rise by exposing the patterns that have been running your life in the background.

Because once your conscious and unconscious mind stop pulling in opposite directions...

Everything begins to change.
