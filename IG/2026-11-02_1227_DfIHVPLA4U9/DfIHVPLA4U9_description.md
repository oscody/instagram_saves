# Three-Round No-Equipment Floor Core Circuit

A man in a pink hoodie demonstrates a bodyweight floor circuit outdoors on a mat, with on-screen labels for 10 dead bugs and the caption issuing a three-round challenge. The post frames it as a challenge for viewers to finish and share their time in the comments.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Get on the floor (any floor); no gym or equipment is needed.
2. Round 1 of 3: 10 dead bugs, then 15 glute bridges, then 20 mountain climbers.
3. Finish the round with a 30-second side plank, holding 15 seconds per side.
4. Repeat the full sequence for three rounds in total.
5. If three rounds isn't possible, do one round.
