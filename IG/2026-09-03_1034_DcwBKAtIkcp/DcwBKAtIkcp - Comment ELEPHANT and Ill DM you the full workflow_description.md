# AI road-trip film built from grey Blender blocking

A reel showing a driving scene with a man and elephants on a desert road, shown next to the grey Blender blocking and phone camera setup used to make it. The caption describes the pipeline: a grey 3D blockout, a hand-shot camera move, and a photoreal AI render finished with a 70s film look.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Block out the scene in Blender as grey boxes, using Claude to help build it through the Higgsfield plugin.
2. Shoot the camera move by hand on a phone with VirtuCamera.
3. Feed the grey render to Seedance 2.5 as a motion reference.
4. Use reference images to wrap a photoreal look around the motion: a character sheet with your own face (made in Higgsfield Soul), the car, and the elephants.
5. Finish the footage in DaVinci Resolve with halation, grain, bloom and a 70s film look.
