# Felix Rieseberg's prompt for deeper AI results

The post shares a prompt attributed to Felix Rieseberg, described as a lead engineer at Anthropic, meant to get deeper, more professional output from AI rather than a bigger one. It is meant for research, writing, coding, business strategy, planning, and other complex tasks where quality matters.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Before rewriting your prompt, say what a top professional in the field would do that you are not asking for.
2. Name the rawest material that professional would start from, instead of the already-digested version you were given.
3. Name the specific methods and tools that professional would use.
4. Say what, other than you or the AI, would show the result is right.
5. Then rewrite your original prompt to ask for exactly those things.
6. Use the prompt: "I want a deeper version of this task, not a bigger one. Before rewriting it: (1) say what a top professional in this field would do here that I'm not asking for; (2) name the rawest material they would start from instead of what has already been digested for me; (3) name the specific methods and tools they would use; (4) say what, other than you or me, would show the result is right. Then rewrite my prompt to ask for exactly that."

## Caption

dominatingtech One of Anthropic’s lead engineers has shared a prompt that could change the way you use AI. 👀

Felix Rieseberg’s advice is simple: instead of asking AI to do more, ask it to go deeper. The goal is to approach tasks like a top professional by identifying what an expert would do differently, which raw materials they would use, what tools and methods they would apply, and how they would verify the final result.

Here’s the prompt:
“I want a deeper version of this task, not a bigger one. Before rewriting it: (1) say what a top professional in this field would do here that I’m not asking for; (2) name the rawest material they would start from instead of what has already been digested for me; (3) name the specific methods and tools they would use; (4) say what, other than you or me, would show the result is right. Then rewrite my prompt to ask for exactly that.”

You can apply this approach to research, writing, coding, business strategy, planning, and almost any complex task where quality matters. 

Save this prompt for the next time you want AI to deliver deeper, more professional results.
