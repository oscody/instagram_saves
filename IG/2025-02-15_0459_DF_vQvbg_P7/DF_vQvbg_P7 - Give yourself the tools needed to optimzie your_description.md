# Claude prompts for resume matching and interview prep

A short reel that walks through using Claude.ai to tailor a resume to a job description and to prepare for an interview. The prompts are shown in the caption and on screen.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Go to Claude.ai and upload your resume together with the job description.
2. Ask Claude to identify the top 3-5 MUST-HAVE skills from the job description.
3. Ask Claude to pinpoint the specific skill each resume bullet point demonstrates, and to flag any bullet that isn't relevant to the job.
4. For any MUST-HAVE skill the resume doesn't strongly reflect, ask for specific bullet rewrites that use keywords and phrases from the job description.
5. For interview prep, paste the research prompt, which on screen asks for 5-7 insightful questions the candidate can ask the interviewer, with an employee review from Glassdoor or a similar platform incorporated and the source cited.
