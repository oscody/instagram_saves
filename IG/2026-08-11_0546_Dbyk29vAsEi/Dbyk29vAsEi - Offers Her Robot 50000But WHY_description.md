# Robot at Invest Fest: $50,000 prompt joke

A stage bit at Invest Fest shows the robot Offers Her Robot $50,000 for a marketing email, and it returns a much bigger package: HTML email, conversion checklist, action guide and a six-email series. The skit's on-screen slide lists the six emails (The Hook, The Story, The Proof, The Offer, The Urgency, The Last Chance).

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Let the AI interview you first so it works from real context instead of guessing.
2. Brief the AI as an expert before the work begins.
3. State the stakes and value of the job, since a higher-stakes brief gets deeper strategy and more deliverables.
