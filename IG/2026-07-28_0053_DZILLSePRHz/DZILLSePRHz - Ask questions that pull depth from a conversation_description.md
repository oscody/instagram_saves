# Open-ended questions for deeper conversations

A man speaking on a podcast-style set argues that questions like "how was your day?" get auto-responses, and that open-ended questions about favorites, least favorites, and new learning draw real answers in relationships, friendships, and with kids. The caption promotes a podcast episode and books, which are not included here.

## What it teaches

1. Avoid closed questions like "how was your day?", which tend to get short auto-responses like "good" or "same old stuff".
2. Ask "Anything interesting happen today?" to open the conversation.
3. Ask "What was your favorite part of the day?"
4. Ask "What was your least favorite part of the day?"
5. Ask "What's something you learned today that you didn't know yesterday?"
6. Ask about something that sparked your interest and that you want to explore further.
7. Use these questions in relationships, friendships, and with kids to get real responses.
