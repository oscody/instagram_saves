## Caption

Wake Forest Water Adventure Park 🌊🌴 This is THE spot to visit this summer and it is right here in our area! @fantasylakeadventurepark is a 65 acre crystal lake, it is now open and there is SO much to do here! This was an old rock quarry that would eventually fill with water after an aquifer was dug into!

As we were pulling up to their location, we instantly became excited at what we were seeing. A beautiful lake with high rock walls surrounding it, a massive water obstacle course, floating cabanas, kayaking, paddle boarding, a 20 foot CLIFF JUMP, scuba section with submerged vessels like at 53 ft boat, 2 planes and more… This place seriously has it all! 

This will be your go to summer spot as they have a sandy area with tables and lounge chairs so you can relax and catch a tan while watching your family enjoy the fun in the water! And, they even have palm trees which gives it even more of a tropical vibe! 🏝️😎 A detail that we noticed, the landscaping was phenomenal which makes the overall location feel so special!

This will be a must visit spot for you, your friends and your family this summer, so make sure you plan your visit soon! 🙌🏻 

*Tickets are not available on-site so make sure you purchase them online ahead of time!*

*Life jackets are required at all times. A safety team was on site with us during the filming of this video.*

📍 3601 Quarry Rd, Wake Forest, NC 27587
