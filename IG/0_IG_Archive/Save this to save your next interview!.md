## Caption

Save this to save your next interview!

DO NOT say “I don’t know” or “I haven’t done that” 🥲

The key here is showing you are prepared (you’ve reviewed the job description and know the required skills) and that, even if you haven’t had an exact experience, you still have the skills to do the job!

Step 1: review the job description and list the required skills before your interview

Step 2: come up with several stories you can tell to demonstrate your proficiency in those skills or closely aligned skills. You can use the STAR or PAR framework to help with this.

Step 3: sell your skills with confidence!

You got this! ✨
