*If you love a little escape from the city, consider going on this $20 2-hour cruise!

Not only will you get as close as you possibly can to the Statue of Liberty without landing on Ellis Island, but you can also enjoy the NYC skyline including views of Hudson Yards, the Empire State Building, the One World Observatory, and Lower Manhattan all during SUNSET 🌇

And no cruise can be without liquor 🥂 so happy to say this cruise offers both liquor and food. There’s more than 30+ seafood dishes to choose from as well as frozen drinks, signature cocktails, beer, cider, wine and shots to pair with your food.

They also offer season passes for those who love cruises for $45. If you’re planning to go on the Statue of Liberty Cruise more than 2x this year, it’ll pay itself off immediately on the 3rd visit.

*🚨 The 2-hour $20 cruise ONLY happens at 7pm so if you want to go on the same cruise shown in the video, make sure to book those tickets only.
