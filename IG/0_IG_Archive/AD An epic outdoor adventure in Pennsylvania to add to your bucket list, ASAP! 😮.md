AD | An epic outdoor adventure in Pennsylvania to add to your bucket list, ASAP! 😮🤩⬇️

Did you know you can go ATV-ing underground?! Whaaaat!!!? 

This just may be one of the coolest things to do in Pennsylvania!

📍Where? Mines & Meadows ATV/RV Resort in Lawrence Country, PA | @visitlawrencecounty partner #visitpa #PAGetaway

📌  𝘚𝘢𝘷𝘦 𝘧𝘰𝘳 𝘺𝘰𝘶𝘳 𝘧𝘶𝘵𝘶𝘳𝘦 𝘵𝘳𝘢𝘷𝘦𝘭𝘴 𝘢𝘯𝘥 𝘴𝘩𝘢𝘳𝘦 𝘸𝘪𝘵𝘩 𝘺𝘰𝘶𝘳 𝘣𝘦𝘴𝘵 𝘢𝘥𝘷𝘦𝘯𝘵𝘶𝘳𝘦 𝘱𝘢𝘭𝘴!

This is the only place that offers underground riding in the Northeast - it’s truly a unique experience! 

You’ll ride through caverns in total darkness (with only your headlights to guide you); past underground lakes (one that’s even 3 acres in size) and through twisting tunnels and more!

The fun even continues above ground with close to 85 miles of trails on 877 acres!

We of course got dirty (I’m talking covered from head to toe in mud) and had an ABSOLUTE blast the whole time!! 

⛑️ Helmet and protective eyewear provided (if you don’t have your own)

👤 Knowledgeable and fun tour guide will lead the way and show you all you need to know!

💲 Price varies depending on vehicle rental type (ie. ATV, side by side etc); length of tour / tour choice etc! Find out more via Visit Lawrence County and/or @minesandmeadows 

🏕️Camping Options: From primitive tent camping to RV sites with electric hookups!
•Costs:
-Electric Sites: $35-$40 per night.
-Non-Electric Sites: $25-$30 per night.
-Long-Term Stays: Available with annual RV park leases and weekly, monthly, and seasonal rates 

I simply cannot wait to share more of the amazing adventures to be had in this incredible & underrated Pennsylvania destination! 🙌🏻
