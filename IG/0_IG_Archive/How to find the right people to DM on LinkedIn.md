How to find the right people to DM on LinkedIn:

[first, make sure you save this!]

1️⃣ Look through the “People” tab of a company’s LinkedIn page

Go to the “People” tab and search “[target position] manager” to find the right hiring managers. To find the right recruiters, you may have to manually sift through a few profiles to find the right ones.

2️⃣ Tap into the “secret” job market on LinkedIn

You’ve probably heard of this trick. Search “Hiring [target position]” in the search bar of LinkedIn. 

However, the search results from this may not be the most helpful, since you’re only able to adjust the filters for recency and the connection level of the poster (ie 1st, 2nd or 3rd+ connection). A better option is…

3️⃣ Searching through your network

Go to the “My Network” tab, search your network for “[target position] manager”, filter for 1st or 2nd connections, your target geographic location, and optional: enter your past employers and school to find people who have those similarities.

You’ll get a list of potential hiring managers that you already may know of share commonalities with that you can reach out to!
