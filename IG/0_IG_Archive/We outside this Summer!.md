We outside this Summer! 
After work though, and a nap 😂

Summer is approaching, make sure to add @thewilliamvale rooftop movie series to your summer bucket list. 

They offer two seating options: general admission lawn seating (picnic style) or lounge seating. 

Doors open 1 hour before the movie begins. I suggest coming early to watch the beautiful sunset and get a good seat. 

Cocktails and food are available for purchase. We saw Mean Girls and the drink names were based on the movie which we loved. They also handed out a drinking game you could play while you watch the movie. 

Their rooftop movie is once a month and a great date night activity. 

Make sure to tell them Table For Two sent you 🎬🍿
