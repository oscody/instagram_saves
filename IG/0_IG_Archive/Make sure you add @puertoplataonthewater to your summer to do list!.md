## Caption

Make sure you add @puertoplataonthewater to your summer to do list! 

They’re located in Freeport, LI along the Nautical Mile. They offer a variety of great dishes including delicious seafood platters. My faves were the Smash Burger & the Picadera platter. We tried the Fulton St platter which was really tasty but I do wish the sauce had more of a kick to it, nonetheless it was still really good and tasted super fresh. We were able to share the platter between 5 people. Their frozen drinks are also super yummy. We tried the Mega Mezcla & the Fish God. 

Once you’re done dining you can head over to their outdoor bar & lounge for some vibes. They have a full bar, a few fried dishes and hookah is also available for purchase. That hookah was bussin okay, it lasted a long time. The DJ had us dancing, he did play a majority of Latin music, a lot of típico & bachata but also mixed it up w some reggae and Reggaeton while I was there. 

Thanks to the owners Rudy & Lance for being amazing hosts, I will be coming back! This is definitely a go to spot this summer. Check them out & tag @foodiegirltour if you do ✨
