## Caption

I want to be lit in the passenger seat singing love songs to my man 💕

You can never go wrong with dinner and movie for date night and @lookcinemas allows you to have both in one place. 

It’s a new dine-in movie theater that recently opened its 57th Street location. 
You can choose between enjoying your cocktails and food at the restaurant in the lobby or enjoy them while watching the movie. 

Our favorite cocktails were the GoldDigger & and W57th. There’s a variety of cocktails ranging from sweet to bold, but there’s something for everyone. We tried the queso and quesadillas off the food menu which were great, next time we’ll try more entrees. The staff is very friendly and ensures you enjoy your time. 

The drinks and food are on the pricier side so that is something I would keep in mind. It’s great for a date night or even a family day out. 

If you go, make sure to tell them Table For Two sent you 🍿
