Here’s 10 things to do in NYC this weekend (May 13 - May 15):

- Friday:
💃 Dance the night away at a FREE K-Pop dance party at @LincolnCenter with DJ @KDOTLIM

💐 Get a FREE flower bouquet at the @mmlafleur x @eastolivia pop-up at @_mamannyc_ in Soho📍239 Centre St, 3:30-6pm while supplies last

- Saturday:
🇯🇵 Attend the FIRST ever Japan Parade in NYC! Find all the deets on my previous post.

😍 Get some supercute treats and merch at the @HelloKittyCafeTruck. Check yesterday’s post for all the deets.

🤤 Starting this Saturday, admission is FREE at @QueensNightMarket. Enjoy a plethora of delicious food from all over the world for only $5 - $6 each. Open from 5pm-12am.

💃 Head to Governor’s Island for @SwampInTheCity, a Cajun & Creole music festival. Enjoy dancing, Cajun and Creole food, cooking demonstrations, dance lessons, music from world-class Louisiana dancehall bands and much more! Admission is FREE, must RSVP.

- Sunday:
🎉 Attend the FIRST annual Asian American and Pacific Islander Cultural Heritage Parade📍6th Avenue from W 44th St to W 55th St, starting at 10:45am

- Saturday + Sunday: 
🧗 Climb for FREE at the Grand Opening Weekend of @thecliffsgowanusbk. See post from 2 days ago for all the deets.

🤤 Eat your way around the world at the 9th Avenue International Food Festival (@9th_ave.food_fest), one of NYC's most popular street fairs. You’ll find dishes ranging from Italian, French, Taiwanese, Brazilian to Greek, Portuguese, Latin American, Indian and many more!📍9th Avenue between 42nd and 57th Streets, 10am-6pm

- Now through Sunday: 
🛍 Shop up to 70 - 80% off @ragandbone, @MaraHoffman and @stoneyclover items at their Sample Sales.
