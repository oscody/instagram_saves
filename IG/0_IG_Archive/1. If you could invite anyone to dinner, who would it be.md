## Caption

1. If you could invite anyone to dinner, who would it be?

2. What has been the highlight of your life?

3. What trait in others do you admire most?

4. If you wrote a book, what would it be about?

5. If you could write a letter to your younger self, what advice would you include?

6. What makes you feel pure joy?

7. If you had more than enough money, what kind of life would you live?

8. What would your dream day look like?

9. If you could master any skill overnight, what would it be?

10. What’s the most challenging decision you’ve ever made?

Most importantly, remember to listen to understand, and ask curious questions when the person stops talking!
