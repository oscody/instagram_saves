Save this! You need to be prepared to ask questions at the end of your next interview.

Here’s part 1 (of infinity) of “The Best Questions to Ask at the End of Your Interview!”

You need to learn more about the role, the company culture, determine if the role/company are a good fit for you AND get a contact for next steps!

Save these questions:

1. When was the last time someone got promoted on this team?
2. How do you evaluate and define success in this role?
3. I do have a few questions but before I ask, can I say one thing?
(Drive home why you’re a good fit.)
4. What are the next steps in the process and who can I contact if I have questions?
