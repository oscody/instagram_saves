If you’re applying for jobs in this market you NEED to be networking. And this is not because networking is a buzzword, but because you need to be finding ways to stand out in the middle of 5,000 applicants. 

But when people think of networking they think of hiring managers and recruiters…

And a lot of people don’t know this: one of the most powerful people you can be networking with are peers (people in a similar level than you working in the same department of the company you’re applying for). 

This is why want to network with peers:

➡️ They’re more reachable and they can easily become your advocates in the company

➡️ They can give you A LOT of tips on the interview process (that recruiters wouldn’t give you). 

Here is the step by step you need to follow when networking with them:

1️⃣ Go into linkedin and open the company page.

2️⃣ Go to people and look for the people in the same department you would be getting into. 

2️⃣ Send them a message to connect!
