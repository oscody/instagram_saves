## Caption

15 deep questions that will bring you & your partner closer together:

(all of these are taken from our free questions app Agapé for couples, families, & friends!)

1. What’s a mistake you’ve made that, looking back, you wouldn’t change because of what it taught you?
2. What’s one thing you’d tell your younger self to give them peace of mind?
3. Describe a time when you truly stepped out of your comfort zone.
4. When you envision our future, what’s one thing that excites you the most?
5. If you could take a year off to pursue something with no financial constraints, what would it be?
6. Is there a skill or hobby you’ve always admired from a distance but never tried?
7. If you could relive one year of your life, which one would it be and why?
8. If you could have one conversation with someone from your past, who would it be and what would you discuss?
9. If you had the power to change one law, policy, or rule in the world, what would it be and why?
10. What’s something you’ve never done but feel you’d be good at?
11. If a movie were made about your life so far, what would be its most unexpected twist?
12. If you could immerse yourself in a new culture or way of life for a year, where and how would you choose to live?
13. What’s a story from your childhood that you’ve never shared with me, but think about often?
14. If you could teleport to any place in the world at this very moment, where would you go and why?
15. If you could send a message to the entire world and have them listen, what would you say?
