I wanted to share some job boards that have actually gotten me real responses. 🙌 

I know it can feel hopeless blasting apps into the void. But I’ve had some wins by mixing up where I apply.

Here are 3 sites that have worked for me:

1. hiring.cafe - Great for startup and tech roles. I connected with a few cool companies here. 

2. Wellfound - Jobs at startups. Quality over quantity.

3. stillhiring.today - Lower competition since it’s focused on who IS still hiring, not just aggregating listings.

My advice? Spread those apps around! Apply to a mix of high volume boards + more niche ones like these. 

It increases your chances of getting real traction instead of disappearing into the abyss. You got this! Sending positive vibes your way.

Let me know if you check any of these out and find them useful. Always happy to share resources for the job hunt hustle. We’re in this together!
