Try them with your partner✨❤️

15 questions that will make you fall in love with each other all over again:

1. What’s your favorite thing about doing life with your partner every day?⏳

2. If you could relive any day we’ve spent together, which would you choose? 👩‍❤️‍👨

3. What about our relationship makes you feel the most grateful? 🧿

4. What’s a shared memory of ours that always brings a smile to your face?🫠

5. How has our love challenged you to become a better person? 💪

6. What was the exact moment you realized you were falling in love with me? ☺️

7. What’s something I do that reassures you, even on your toughest days, that we’re in this together? ❤️

8. Describe a time I did something relatively small and simple that made you feel extremely happy and appreciated.🫰

9. Describe a moment when you looked at me and thought, “This is why I love them.”💋

10. How do you feel our love is different or special compared to other love stories you know?👩‍❤️‍👨

11. When you close your eyes and think of a moment filled with pure love between us, what do you see?🧿

12. If you could have one wish about our future come true, what would you want it to be?👩😻👨

13. What trait or habit of mine have you come to cherish over time?🙈

14. Is there a song, scent, or place that instantly reminds you of a special moment between us?🎵💌🚌

15. What’s one way I’ve been there for you in a way that you didn’t even know you needed? ✨
