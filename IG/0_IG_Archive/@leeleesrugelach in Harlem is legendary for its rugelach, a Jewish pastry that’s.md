## Caption

@leeleesrugelach in Harlem is legendary for its rugelach, a Jewish pastry that’s been updated and—some would say—perfected by Alvin “Lee” Smalls. Originally hailing from South Carolina, Mr. Lee spent years working as a baker in New York-Presbyterian hospital. He opened his first bakery in Harlem in his 50s, bringing carrot cake, walnut cookies, and of course his unique rugelach to the masses. These are rich, moist, and absolutely essential. 

Mr. Lee just celebrated his 80th, but he still gets busy in the kitchen. Make sure you pay him a visit, and let him know #RighteousEats sent you.
