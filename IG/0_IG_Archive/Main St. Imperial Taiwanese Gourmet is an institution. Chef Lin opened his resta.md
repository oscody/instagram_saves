Main St. Imperial Taiwanese Gourmet is an institution. Chef Lin opened his restaurant in 2000 and established himself as a Flushing icon, masterfully conducting the wok to turn out Taiwanese classics like Fly's Head and Tofu Casserole. But his menu holds other gems, like a fried whole fish with garlic, clams with basil, and braised ribs. Let me know if you want to see that in part 2. Here are my essentials:

∙ Taiwanese Meatball 肉圆 - A gelatinous dough stuffed with pork, highly flavorful. 🔥

 ∙ Tofu Casserole 豆腐鍋 - When the locals recommend you something, make sure you try it. You will be rewarded. 

 ∙ Flys' Head 蒼蠅頭 - One of the most well-known Taiwanese dishes. Minced pork, chive and black bean harmoniously mix with rice. Righteous.

Restaurants define the cultural fabric of communities. Main St. Imperial is one of those "if you know you know" spots, highly respected, heartfelt, and harmonious. #RighteousEats
