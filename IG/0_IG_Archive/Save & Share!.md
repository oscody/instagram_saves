Bucket list Adventure in Upstate New York!
Take a hot air balloon ride over the Letchworth State Park! Considered one of the best Hot Air Balloon rides in the USA.

Mass balloon launch happens during the Memorial Day weekend balloon festival.
Provided by a family-owned company @balloonsoverletchworth !
Rides are available, morning and evening,  Monday through Saturday, year-round.
The launch site is located adjacent to the Middle Falls area.
Advance booking is required.

Plan your adventure to Letchworth State Park and stay at @dellcollective , just a few miles from the Park. A great barn-style Airbnb stay where you can interact with cute farm animals.
