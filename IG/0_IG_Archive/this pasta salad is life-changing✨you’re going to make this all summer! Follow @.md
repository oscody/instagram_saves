## Caption

this pasta salad is life-changing✨you’re going to make this all summer! Follow @healthygirlkitchen for more vegan recipes! 

Ingredients (8 servings): 
16 oz rotini 
2 cups bell pepper
1 cup diced celery
1.5 cups chickpeas
1 cup cucumber
1 cup red onion
2 cups cherry tomatoes
1 cup black olives
1/2 cup dill

Dressing: 
1/2 cup plant-based plain yogurt
1/3 cup olive oil
1 tbsp lemon
1 tsp salt
1 tsp garlic
1 tsp dried dill
1/4 tsp onion powder
1 tsp dried parsley
1/4 tsp pepper
1 tsp dijon mustard

How to: 
1. Cook pasta and chop the veggies.
2. Mix dressing ingredients in a bowl. 
3. Strain pasta and run cold water over it. Add into large bowl with veggies and pour dressing on top. Mix! 
4. Keep in fridge for up to 5 days! Eat it for lunches all week long.
