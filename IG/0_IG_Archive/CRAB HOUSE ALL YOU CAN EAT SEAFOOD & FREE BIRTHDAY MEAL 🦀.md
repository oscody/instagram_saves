CRAB HOUSE: ALL YOU CAN EAT SEAFOOD & FREE BIRTHDAY MEAL 🦀 
•
@CrabHouseNY is a NYC restaurant offering all you can eat lobster & seafood!

You can pick an unlimited number of items
and everything is made fresh to order (crab, lobster, scallop, shrimp and much more)! 

The best time to visit Crab House is on your birthday. If you visit within 1 day of your birthday with 3 paying adults, you will eat for FREE!

Offer valid at both Midtown (135 E 55th Street) & Coney Island @crabhousebrooklyn (1223 Surf Avenue) locations!

Pricing for Adults:
Lobster: $115/person
Seafood: $80/person
*kids are reduced

I visited last week and it was truly delicious! We had about 10 dishes to split - mainly seafood but steak and some veggies as well. My favorites were the lobster and old bay shrimp! Thanks to @lindanguyenlee for the tip on this spot! 

Come to Crab House HUNGRY - you will leave full of delicious seafood!
