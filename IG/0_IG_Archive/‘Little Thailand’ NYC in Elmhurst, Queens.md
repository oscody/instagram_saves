‘Little Thailand’ NYC in Elmhurst, Queens. 

It’s to officially become known as this soon and it’s one of the neighbourhoods we’ve eaten in most, having lived there for the past 3 & a half years! 

Here’s a little guide on where to eat in the area between 76th - 79th streets on Woodside Ave, which will be renamed once it gets the Mayor’s signature. 

📍 @ayada_thai_queens
📍 Khao Kang
📍 @zaabzaabnyc
📍 P.Noi Thaithai Grocery
📍 @hugesannyc
📍 @patabarnyc

It was unofficially called NYC’s Thai Town, so I wonder why they went with Little Thailand instead but either way it’s great recognition for the neighbourhood.
