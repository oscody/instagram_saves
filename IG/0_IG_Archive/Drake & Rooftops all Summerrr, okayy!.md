## Caption

Drake & Rooftops all Summerrr, okayy! 

If you’re looking for a cute rooftop with IMMACULATE VIEWS , like the TikTok girls say 😅, definitely checkout @monarchnyc. 

It’s the perfect after work spot to grab a drink, soak in the weather and enjoy breathtaking views of the Empire State Building. They have great cocktails and small bites. Loved their Buffalo Chicken Pizza & Tiger Shrimp Dumplings. Mocktails are also available if you don’t want to drink, the Lookin Like a Snack was so good, tasted like a Piña Colada, perfect summer vibes. 

It is a rooftop in the middle of the city so it is a tad bit pricey but what spot isn’t in NYC nowadays 😅. Food and drinks range from $18-25, Mocktails are $13. The view is worth it though. Perfect spot to start off a date or hang w friends. Check it out ✨
