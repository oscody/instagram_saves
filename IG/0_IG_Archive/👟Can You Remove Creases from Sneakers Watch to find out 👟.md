👟Can You Remove Creases from Sneakers? Watch to find out 👟

To remove creases from sneakers, you can try several methods:

	1.	Stuffing: Fill the sneakers with socks, towels, or shoe trees to reshape them. Leave them stuffed for a day or two to allow the material to regain its shape.
	2.	Heat and Pressure: Use a hairdryer on low heat to warm up the creased area, then press down on the creases with your fingers or a smooth, flat object like a spoon. Be careful not to overheat the material.
	3.	Steaming: Use a handheld steamer to gently steam the creased area for 20-30 seconds, then use your fingers or a cloth to smooth out the creases while the sneakers are still warm.
	4.	Ironing (with caution): Place a towel over the creased area and iron on low heat for a few seconds, constantly checking to avoid overheating or damaging the material.
	5.	Commercial products: There are also specialized products available, like sneaker shields or crease protectors, that can help prevent and reduce creases in sneakers.

Always test any method on a small, inconspicuous area first, and be cautious to avoid damaging the material of your sneakers.
