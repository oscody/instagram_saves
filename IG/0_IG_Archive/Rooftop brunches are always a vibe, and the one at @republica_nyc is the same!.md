## Caption

Rooftop brunches are always a vibe, and the one at @republica_nyc is the same!

Republic is a Latin restaurant in Dykman with aesthetic for dayssss but good food and service as well.

They offer a bottomless brunch on the rooftop, with 2 courses, mimosas and sangria. 

For dinner they have the most well presented and creative cocktails! The food and drinks come swinging, in a bird cage, and other crazy ways

A great place for a birthday or just because. Definitely check it out!
