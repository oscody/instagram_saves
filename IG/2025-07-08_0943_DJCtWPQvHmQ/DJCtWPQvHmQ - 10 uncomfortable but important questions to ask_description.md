# 10 uncomfortable questions to ask early in dating

A short video of a woman driving, listing questions to ask a partner when you first start dating. The on-screen text repeats several of the questions.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Ask how they typically handle conflict or disagreements in the relationship.
2. Ask whether they have non-negotiables or boundaries the partner has to respect.
3. Ask how they define loyalty and where they think most people mess it up.
4. Ask how important it is to have their own space and time in a relationship.
5. Ask whether marriage is something they would eventually want.
6. Ask what the way they wish people loved them better is.
7. Ask what one thing about relationships they are still figuring out.
8. Ask about faith: whether they are a woman or man of God, whether they go to church, and whether they believe in the Lord Jesus Christ.
9. Ask what one thing about themselves is hard to accept but they are not willing to change.
10. Ask how they manage finances and what their views on money in a relationship are.
11. Bonus: ask what one thing they appreciate about being single that they want to keep in a relationship.
