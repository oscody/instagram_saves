## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. Solo is a real app and its mileage tracking, Smart Schedule, Pay Guarantee and 7-day trial all exist. The "20% more on average" and "up to 40% more" claims are Solo's own marketing, and the guarantee has conditions.
* Checked: 2026-11-10 by claude-sonnet-5-5

### Resources

* [Solo: Your Gig Business App (worksolo.com) (worksolo.com)](https://worksolo.com/): product, found by search, verified, high confidence. The FAQ page on worksolo.com confirms mileage tracking, Smart Schedule, Pay Guarantee and the 7-day trial described in the reel.
* [Solo FAQ (worksolo.com)](https://worksolo.com/faq): page, found by search, verified, high confidence. Opened it. It lists pricing tiers, Pay Guarantee conditions, and 'up to 40% more per hour' in Smart Schedule cities. It does not mention 20%.
* [Solo app on Google Play (play.google.com)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.paveapp): product, found by search, not opened, medium confidence. Search result titled 'Solo: Your Gig Business App'. I did not open it.

### Key steps

1. Download Solo from the App Store or Google Play and start the 7-day trial. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends to avoid a charge.
2. Link your gig accounts (Uber, DoorDash, Instacart and others).
3. Opt into mileage tracking and leave the app running in the background so it logs work trips automatically. You can edit miles by hand.
4. Use Smart Schedule (Pro plan, eligible cities only) to see the best hours to work.
5. For Pay Guarantee (Pro Plus plan, select metros only), save a Pay Prediction schedule, keep location on, average at least one job per scheduled hour, and link Venmo or PayPal. Any shortfall is paid at the end of the week.

### Not found

I found no source for the '20% more on average' figure. Solo's own FAQ says 'up to 40% more per hour', which is a maximum. I did not check independent reviews in detail, and the search summary noted that sources disagree on which plan includes which feature.
