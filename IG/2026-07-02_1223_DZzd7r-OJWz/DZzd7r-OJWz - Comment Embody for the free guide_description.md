# Five-step embodiment method for becoming who you want

A talking-head reel over moody painted and black-and-white images about shedding your old self to reach what you want. It ends by offering a breakdown of the method to anyone who comments.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Design your environment: curate the accounts you follow, the conversations you join, and the spaces you spend time in so they reflect who you are becoming.
2. Do an evening rehearsal: before sleep, instead of scrolling, replay your day as the new version of yourself and how that person would carry themselves.
3. Name the old self: give the procrastinating, shrinking version a name so that when its patterns show up, you see them as separate from you.
4. Apply the cost rule: make old behavior cost something each time it slips through, such as a set of push-ups or a cold shower.
5. Set standards over goals: stop asking what you want to achieve and decide what you will no longer accept from yourself.
