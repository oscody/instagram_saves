# Four Claude prompts for LLC business funding

A six-slide carousel presenting four Claude prompts meant to help LLC owners get business funding, covering entity structure, funding product types, business credit, and industry setup. The full prompts are only sent after commenting a keyword.

## What it teaches

1. Structure: ask Claude to act as a business funding strategist, review your entity type, time in business and current setup, and list 3-5 structural gaps likely causing loan denials before you apply.
2. Funding options: ask Claude to act as a business banking expert, explain revenue-based financing, business lines of credit and 0% business credit cards, then identify 3-5 signs of which product fits your structure, time in business and revenue.
3. Owner profile: ask Claude for 3-5 steps to build business credit, including trade lines, reporting agencies and business banking, so the business looks fundable to underwriters.
4. Industry setup: ask Claude for 3-5 adjustments to your entity type, NAICS code and business licensing so they match what underwriters expect for your specific industry before you apply.
