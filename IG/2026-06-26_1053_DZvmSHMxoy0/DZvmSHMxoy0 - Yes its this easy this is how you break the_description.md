# Breaking the stranger barrier at a bar

A dating reel showing a bar approach: a man stands near a woman while she orders, asks about her drink, and starts a friendly conversation. The reel ends with a masterclass promotion for a Sunday session.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Approach a woman at a bar or club while she is ordering, and stay next to her.
2. Ask her what she is ordering and start a friendly conversation.
3. If she is friendly, offer to buy the next round.
4. Ask whether she is there with friends and where they are.
5. Introduce your own friends to her friends.
