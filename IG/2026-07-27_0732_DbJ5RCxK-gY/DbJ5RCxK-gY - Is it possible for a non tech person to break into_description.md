# 8-week roadmap for non-tech people entering AI

The reel asks whether a non-tech person can break into AI in 8 weeks and walks through a week-by-week plan. The free resources for it are sent by DM.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Week 1: learn how LLMs are used by watching an introductory YouTube video on the topic.
2. Week 2: take a free AI fluency course, such as Anthropic's prompt engineering tutorial shown on screen.
3. Week 3: learn prompting, which is how you talk to LLMs, through a free course.
4. Week 4: take Google's free Introduction to Generative AI course.
5. Week 5: earn the Google Cloud Generative AI Leader certification, which needs no coding and which recruiters value.
6. Week 6: build an AI tool for your own field, such as a contract-review tool for a lawyer or an art tool for an artist, using drag-and-drop automation tools that need no coding.
7. Week 7: combine your prompting skills, certifications and tool into one working system, then test it, find where it fails and debug it.
8. Week 8: post about your tool online; these posts become your resume.
