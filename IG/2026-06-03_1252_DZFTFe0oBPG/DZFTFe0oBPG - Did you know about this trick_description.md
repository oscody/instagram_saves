# Splashing water to train eyes to stay open

The reel claims fighters use a water-splashing drill to stop flinching when something flies at their face. Clips of boxing matches are mixed with a man splashing water from a basin outdoors.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Grab a bowl of water.
2. Aggressively splash the water at your own face.
3. Repeat the splashing over and over while forcing your eyes to stay open.
4. Aim to retrain the nervous system so your eyes stay locked on an incoming object instead of closing and turning away.
5. Once you can see the punch coming, you can avoid it.
