# Psycho-cybernetics ideas in 60 seconds

A talking-head reel with on-screen text that summarizes ideas from Maxwell Maltz's Psycho-Cybernetics. The points are numbered on screen (#3, #8, #12) and cover self-image, goal-seeking, visualization, mistakes, and self-forgiveness.

## What it teaches

1. Your results cannot outperform your self-image, so change how you see yourself.
2. Your brain is a goal-seeking mechanism: give it a clear target and it will find a way.
3. Avoid negative targets, since the brain will pursue them too.
4. Your subconscious cannot tell a vividly imagined experience from a real one.
5. Visualize the end result or rule, not the hard work.
6. Act as if you are already the person you want to be.
7. Treat mistakes as data to correct your course. You make mistakes, but you are not a mistake.
8. Spend 15 minutes every day mentally rehearsing your biggest wins.
9. Think of happiness as a habit you practice, not an endpoint you reach.
10. Dehypnotize yourself from the limits society gave you.
11. Forgive yourself instantly so you can move forward.
12. Change your self-image and your reality will follow.
