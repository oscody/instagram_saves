# Hide ChatGPT Tells in Your Resume

Greg Langstaff walks through a sample resume marked up with phrases and em dashes that signal ChatGPT was used. He says using AI for a resume is fine, as long as those tells are removed.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Swap out words that people rarely use but ChatGPT uses often for plainer alternatives.
2. Delete em dashes or replace them with a comma.
3. Check for repetitive sentence structure, such as 'did something, resulting in outcome' repeated across bullets, and vary it.

## Caption

Submitting a resume with these ChatGPT tells comes across as lazy. They want to know that you have a command of the written word AND that you can use AI to do more than just the bare minimum. #ChatGPT #AI #resume #jobsearch
