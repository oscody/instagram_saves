# Cinco preguntas de Jung para conocerte

A carousel presenting five questions that the post says Carl Jung asked new patients, each with a short explanation of what it is meant to uncover. It ends with an invitation to comment "MAP" to receive material on discipline and productivity.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Answer the five questions one at a time, not all at once, with pen and paper and without rushing.
2. Look for honest answers rather than perfect or final ones, even if they are incomplete or uncomfortable.
3. Q1: What are you avoiding? Give a name to what you avoid, because what is not named cannot be examined.
4. Q2: What do you fear others will discover about you? Name the hidden belief (such as 'I am not enough') and say it out loud.
5. Q3: What would you do if you knew for sure you would not fail? First recognize that this desire exists; you do not need to act on it immediately.
6. Q4: Who would you be if you stopped seeking approval from others? Distinguish who you really are from who you have learned to pretend to be, without necessarily stopping caring about others.
7. Q5: What gives meaning to your life today? Focus on this day rather than your whole life, and accept a partial answer.
8. Treat the suffering that comes from this self-examination as a way to grow rather than to remain stuck.
