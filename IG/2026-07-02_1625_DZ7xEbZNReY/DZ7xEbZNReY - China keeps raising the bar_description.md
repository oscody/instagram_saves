# 念凡尘 (Nian Fanchen) donghua preview from Tencent

The post promotes the upcoming donghua 念凡尘 (Nian Fanchen / I'm Actually a Cultivation Bigshot), made by 崇卓动画 and distributed on Tencent Video, about a man whose ordinary actions are seen as profound cultivation techniques. ## Caption

China keeps raising the bar.
I will not lie, I never heard of this studio but I will clearly pay more attention now.
« 念凡尘 » is produced by 崇卓动画 (Chong Zhuo Animation Studio) and distributed via Tencent Video as part of their 2026 lineup.

The premise flips the typical overpowered protagonist trope: Li Nianfan just wants a peaceful life, but every mundane action he takes is interpreted as a profound cultivation technique by everyone around him—except himself.

The series leans into comedic misunderstandings while keeping the cultivation world’s traditional stakes intact. Tencent continues to diversify its donghua lineup beyond pure action xianxia, proving they can handle genre-bending narratives with the same production quality.
