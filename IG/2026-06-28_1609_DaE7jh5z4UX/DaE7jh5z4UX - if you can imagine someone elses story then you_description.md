# Imagining your future life and reading fiction

The creator argues that imagining detailed scenes of the life you want, with the feelings involved, can shift your real circumstances. Fantasy and fiction books are shown on screen, including The Eye of the World, The Name of the Wind, Oathbringer and The Hobbit.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Imagine specific scenes from the life you want, including how it feels, what you are thinking and feeling, and everything about the moment.
2. Daydream with direction and intention rather than only writing down goals.
3. Make the imagined scene emotionally real, so your subconscious mind believes it.
4. Read fantasy and fiction to train your imagination, treating it as a muscle that gets stronger with practice.
