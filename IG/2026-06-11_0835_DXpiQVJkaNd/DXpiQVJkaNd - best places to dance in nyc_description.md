### Key steps

1. Go to Garibaldi Plaza in Washington Square Park on Wednesday.
2. Arrive by 6pm for the free beginner class (6–6:30pm).
3. Open social dancing runs until 9pm. No cover charge or registration.
4. Check the organizers' Instagram for weather cancellations and the season end date (the 2025 season ended Oct 29).
