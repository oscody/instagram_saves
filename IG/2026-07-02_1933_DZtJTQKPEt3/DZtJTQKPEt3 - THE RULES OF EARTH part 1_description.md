# The Rules of Earth (part 1?)

A spiritual monologue in front of a lit candle, presenting a narrative in which people are infinite beings who forgot their origins in a simulation called Earth. It frames inner awakening, fear and finding other awakened people as the way out.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Get quiet and listen for the excitement or aliveness you feel, which the post calls the code written inside you.
2. Stop living in fear, since the post says fear keeps you in survival mode and focused outward.
3. Meditate, go within and listen to your heart to activate that inner code.
4. Pay attention to intuitive nudges and synchronicities, and live in alignment with them.
5. Choose love over fear as a daily act.
6. Find other awakened people and build outside the systems that cage you.
