# 6 AI prompts to build a LinkedIn profile

A carousel of fill-in-the-blank AI prompts for optimising a LinkedIn profile for UK and European recruiter searches. The full set is offered by comment.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Headline: fill in your role, years of experience, top strengths, target company type, one concrete result, and target market, then ask an AI for 7 headline options under 220 characters, each with at least 2 recruiter keywords, mixing role-led, outcome-led and one aimed at international professionals.
2. About section: give the AI your current role, years of experience, hard skills, proudest measurable achievement, what makes your background different and your target role, then ask for a first-person About section under 320 words that opens with a hook not starting with 'I', ends with an invitation to connect, and avoids the words 'passionate', 'hardworking', 'results-driven', 'dynamic' and 'motivated'.
3. Experience: paste a role's duties, tools and approximate results, then ask for 5 bullet points, each starting with a different strong action verb, following action + context + result, including at least one number, no longer than 2 lines, plus the top 3 keywords to repeat elsewhere in the profile.
4. Keywords: paste a full UK or European job description and ask the AI to extract the top 20 keywords, group them into hard skills, soft skills, tools and industry terms, rank them high/medium/low, say where the top 10 should go (headline, About, experience or skills), flag UK/EU-specific terms and suggest 3 extra terms UK recruiters search for.
5. Skills: state your target job title, market, seniority and existing skills, then ask for the top 25 LinkedIn skills labelled hard or soft, with how often they appear in UK and European postings and whether peers or recruiters tend to endorse them, then pick the 10 to pin at the top of your Skills section.
6. Rule for all prompts: AI should support your voice, not replace it. Structure and clarify with AI, but always add your own context, achievements and numbers.
