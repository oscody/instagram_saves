# Six AI prompts for a UK-focused LinkedIn profile

A six-slide carousel presenting AI prompts for building a LinkedIn profile that appears in recruiter searches for UK and European roles. The prompts are written out on the slides, and the post offers clickable versions by comment.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Use the headline prompt: fill in your role, years of experience, top strengths, one measurable achievement, target UK or European location and whether you want visa-sponsored roles, then ask for 7 headlines under 220 characters with at least 2 recruiter keywords each.
2. Use the About prompt: give your current role, experience, hard skills, proudest measurable achievement and what sets your background apart, then ask for a first-person About section under 320 words that opens with a hook not starting with 'I', states what you are looking for and ends with an invitation to connect.
3. Rewrite each experience entry with the experience prompt: describe the role, duties, tools and approximate results, then ask for 5 bullet points that each start with a different action verb, follow action, context and result, include a number, and stay under 2 lines, plus the top 3 keywords to repeat elsewhere in the profile.
4. Find keywords with the job-description prompt: paste a target UK or European job description and ask for the top 20 keywords ranked high, medium or low, where to place the top 10 on your profile, UK-specific terms to watch for, and 3 extra terms UK recruiters commonly search.
5. Build the Skills section with the skills prompt: list the skills you already have, then ask for 25 LinkedIn skills labelled hard or soft, how often they appear in UK and European postings, whether they are usually endorsed, and which 10 to pin to the top.
6. Run an audit with the final prompt: paste your headline, About section and 1 to 2 experience entries (with profile screenshots) and ask for a score out of 10, whether the headline and opening lines make a recruiter click, missing keywords, and the 3 highest-impact changes with rewritten examples.
7. Keep your own voice: AI structures and sharpens the wording, but add your personal context, achievements and numbers so the profile stands out.
