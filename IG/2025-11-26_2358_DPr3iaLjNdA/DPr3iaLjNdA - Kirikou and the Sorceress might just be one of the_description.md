# Kirikou and the Sorceress: an overlooked animated film

A reel praising Michel Ocelot's Kirikou and the Sorceress as an overlooked animated film set in a West African village. It follows a tiny boy born able to walk and talk, who investigates why the sorceress Karaba cursed his village, and highlights its African folklore roots and its focus on empathy over typical good-versus-evil tropes.
