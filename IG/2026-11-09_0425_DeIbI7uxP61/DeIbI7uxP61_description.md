# Find recent hiring posts on LinkedIn by city

A creator shows a LinkedIn search that surfaces recent posts from recruiters and hiring managers about open roles in a given city, rather than using the jobs tab. The example is a "seattle hiring" search sorted by latest.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Open LinkedIn search and type your city plus the word "hiring" (for example, "seattle hiring").
2. Tap the "Posts" filter.
3. Tap "Sort by" and choose "Latest", then show results.
4. Check these results every day for recruiters and hiring managers posting about open roles.
