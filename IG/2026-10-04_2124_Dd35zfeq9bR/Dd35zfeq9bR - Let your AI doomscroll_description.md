# Let your AI doomscroll your saved reels

A creator describes handing about 70 saved reels to Claude, which reviewed their transcripts, frames and captions and turned the worth-repeating workflows into skills. The on-screen text lists this as a "Try it" step.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Go through your saved folder and gather the saved posts you never used.
2. Give the saved material (transcripts, frames and captions) to Claude.
3. Have Claude pull out anything worth doing more than once.
4. Have Claude turn those workflows into skills, which are saved sets of instructions it follows the next time it does that job.

### Key steps

1. Turn on code execution in Claude settings. For automatic grabbing, use the Claude desktop app and the Claude in Chrome extension, logged into Instagram.
2. Put 5–15 useful reels on one topic into a new Instagram collection. Skip memes and motivational content.
3. Give Claude the reel links so it can download low-quality video, transcribe the audio and review the frames. Or paste captions and transcripts as text, which uses less usage but gives words only.
4. Send the prompt: 'I saved some reels about [topic] in an Instagram collection. Here are the links. For each one, read the caption, transcribe the audio, and look at the frames. Then group what they teach into a few skills I can reuse for [the job], instead of one skill per reel. Keep them specific and skip anything generic. When you're done, give me a short report on how to use each skill. Grab the videos at low quality and delete the files when you're done.' For text-only input, remove the download line.
5. Review each SKILL.md Claude writes. In the desktop app, save it. In a regular chat, download it and upload it under Customize > Skills.
6. Ask for the job as usual and Claude applies the skill. Add new reels later to update it.
