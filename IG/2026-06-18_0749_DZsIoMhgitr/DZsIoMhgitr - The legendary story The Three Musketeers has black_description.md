# Claim that The Three Musketeers hides Black history

The reel claims that Alexander Dumas and the three musketeers were Black, linking them to Huguenots, Moors and the Spanish Inquisition through quoted book passages. It is shown over paintings and an old manuscript illustration, with an "Across The Ages" logo.

## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. Alexandre Dumas did have Black ancestry. His grandmother, Marie-Cessette Dumas, was an enslaved woman in Saint-Domingue, and his father, General Thomas-Alexandre Dumas, was mixed-race. The claim that the Musketeers themselves were Black, or tied to Moors and the Inquisition through book passages, isn't supported by the novel's text. I did not open any sources, and the reel's quoted passages couldn't be checked.
