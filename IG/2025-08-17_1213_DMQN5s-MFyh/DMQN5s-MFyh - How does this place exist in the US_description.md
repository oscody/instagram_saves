### Key steps

1. Pick a tour on jetski2catalina.com: Avalon or Two Harbors (both from $399), or the 2-hour Long Beach coastal tour (from $249).
2. Click Book Now to open the Checkfront reservation page, then choose a date and pay. You can also call 562-286-6600 (daily, 9am–5pm PST).
3. Arrive at the marina at 700 Queensway Drive, Long Beach, CA 90802. A listing says departure is around 8am with return by about 4pm.
4. Ride about 1.5 hours each way in a guided group. The price includes the jet ski, wetsuit, safety gear and fuel.
5. Spend about 3 hours on the island. Avalon has free time in town. Two Harbors includes a Blue Cavern swim, with optional snorkeling and hiking.
6. Customers get 20% off a room at the Maya hotel in the same marina, if you want to stay the night before or after.
