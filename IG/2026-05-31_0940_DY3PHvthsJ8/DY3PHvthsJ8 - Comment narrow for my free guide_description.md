# Feelings chart: want vs. need vs. what to do

The speaker goes through a chart that pairs five feelings (bored, stressed, jealous, sad, shame) with the urge people usually act on, the underlying need, and a concrete action. The chart is shown on a TV screen behind him.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. When bored, don't doomscroll or goon; you need purpose. Imagine your best self and do one thing to become that version.
2. When stressed, don't panic; you need clarity. Write down what is actually important in this moment.
3. When jealous, don't compare yourself to others; you need security. Become a master of a craft and develop competency in at least one thing.
4. When sad, don't go to sleep; you need movement. Do as many push-ups as you can and go for a walk while the sun is out.
5. When you feel shame, don't isolate; you need perspective. Tell your shame to someone you respect, which helps you see yourself as you truly are and question the shame.
