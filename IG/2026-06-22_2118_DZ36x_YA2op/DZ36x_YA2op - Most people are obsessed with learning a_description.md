# Diagram for learning any skill

The creator shows a hand-drawn "How to Learn Anything" diagram of numbered steps linked by connections, with sequences and totality labelled around them. He describes skills as linear processes made of steps taken one after another.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Break the skill into its individual steps, identifying each one separately.
2. Identify the transitions (connections) between those steps separately as well.
3. Practice all the steps and transitions in every possible order (sequences).
4. If you discover a new step you hadn't identified, add it and practice it too.
5. Repeat this process indefinitely, which he says means no skill is out of reach.
