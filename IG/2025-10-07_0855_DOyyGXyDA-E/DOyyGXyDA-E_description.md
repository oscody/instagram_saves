# Recipe-import app for saving cookbooks

A short video promoting a recipe app that imports a recipe from a copied social media link and saves it to a cookbook. On-screen it shows the share sheet with "Copy link," an imported ingredient list titled "Blended queso sauce," and a Cookbooks screen with collections such as Slow Cooker, Sweets, Healthy, Dinner, High Protein Snacks and Ninja Creami.

## Research

* Verdict: Promo. The reel is an ad for ReciMe, a real recipe app. It imports recipes from social links and sorts them into cookbooks, and it has a free tier. Premium is paid, and I couldn't confirm current prices.
* Checked: 2026-10-23 by claude-sonnet-5-5

### Key steps

1. Open the Instagram post or reel and tap Share.
2. Tap Share to, then choose ReciMe. If it isn't listed, add ReciMe to your share shortcuts first.
3. ReciMe reads the caption first, then the video audio, then looks for the original recipe site.
4. Open the imported recipe in the app and file it into a cookbook. The help article doesn't cover this step.
