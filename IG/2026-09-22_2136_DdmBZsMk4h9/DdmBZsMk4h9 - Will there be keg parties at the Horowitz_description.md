# Horowitz Andreessen Academy: free AI school news

A Morning Brew news carousel reporting that Andreessen Horowitz is investing $35 million in a free, AI-focused college alternative, the Horowitz Andreessen Academy, starting with a one-year program in fall 2027 and led by Gagan Biyani. The slides show the academy's tagline, team members and founding partners such as Anthropic, Google, Meta, Nvidia and OpenAI.

## Caption

Will there be keg parties at the Horowitz Andreessen Academy?⁣
⁣
Venture capital giant Andreessen Horowitz is investing $35 million into a free, AI-focused alternative to traditional college. The San Francisco based academy will begin with a one-year program in the fall of 2027. The school, which plans to operate as an independent business, will be led by Gagan Biyani.⁣
⁣
Biyani told the Wall Street Journal that “The idea is to create an AI-native education offering so that these students can be ready for jobs at the top leading tech companies, or to start their own tech company”.⁣
⁣
The academy will have no shortage of access. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Nvidia, and others will be providing software, along with opening their offices to students.⁣
⁣
Famously a place where many have spurred traditional higher ed, Silicon Valley is once again shaking up the college landscape.⁣
