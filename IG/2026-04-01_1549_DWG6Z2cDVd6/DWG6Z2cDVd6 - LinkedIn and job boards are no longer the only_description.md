# Five LLM prompts for job applications

A six-slide carousel that presents five ChatGPT-style prompts for job seekers: resume tailoring, cover letters, LinkedIn profile rewrites, a networking post, and interview question prediction. Each slide shows the prompt text with a sample output.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Resume tailoring: paste your current resume and the job description, then ask the LLM to add exact keywords from the job description, quantify achievements, keep it under one page in bullet format, and summarize the top 3-5 matches. Ask for the full revised resume plus a list of changes.
2. Cover letter: paste the job description, 3-5 key experiences, and your resume summary. Ask for a three-paragraph letter that opens with company-specific excitement, proves fit with quantified stories, and ends with a strong interview call to action, in a confident, professional tone with no fluff.
3. LinkedIn profile: paste your current About section and headline, list your roles, skills, achievements, and target role. Ask for a rewritten headline and About section that is first-person, engaging, keyword-rich for recruiter searches, and ends with a subtle call to action. Also ask for three banner ideas.
4. Networking post: state an achievement and target audience. Ask for a post with a hook in the first line, a short story with lessons, a closing question to drive comments, and 3-5 relevant hashtags, aimed at building connections and visibility.
5. Interview prep: paste the job description and company/role. Ask for 15 likely interview questions (5 technical, 5 behavioral, 5 company- or role-specific), with the reason each is asked and a suggested answer framework for each.
