# Five LLM prompts for job search and interviews

A carousel of five prompts for using ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini as a career assistant, with a sample output shown for each. The prompts cover resume tailoring, cover letters, LinkedIn profile rewrites, networking posts, and interview question prediction.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. 1. ATS-proof resume tailor: paste your current resume and the job description, then ask the LLM to add exact keywords and phrases from the JD, quantify achievements where possible, keep the resume under one page in bullet format, highlight the top 3–5 matches in a summary, and output the full revised resume plus a list of changes.
2. 2. Cover letter builder: paste the job description and 3–5 key experiences plus your resume summary, then ask for a 3-paragraph letter with a hook about specific company excitement, proof of fit through quantified stories, and a strong call to action for an interview, in a confident, professional tone with no fluff.
3. 3. LinkedIn profile glow-up: paste your current About section and headline, your experience, and a target role, then ask for a rewritten headline and About section that are first-person, engaging, keyword-rich for recruiter searches, and end with a subtle call to action; also ask for 3 banner ideas.
4. 4. Viral LinkedIn networking post: give the achievement and target audience, then ask for a post with a hook in the first line, a short story with lessons, a closing question to drive comments, and 3–5 relevant hashtags, with the goal of building connections and visibility.
5. 5. Interview question predictor: paste the job description and the company and role, then ask for 15 likely interview questions split into 5 technical, 5 behavioral, and 5 company/role-specific, with the reason each is asked and a suggested answer framework for each.
