# Black Magic Part 3: Who Taught Belief?

A talking-head reel filmed beside a large stone statue, in which the creator argues that belief shapes behaviour and reality, so the most powerful "magic" may be belief itself. He also argues that colonial governments and missionaries labelled many spiritual practices as superstition, fetishes or black magic, and urges viewers to examine inherited beliefs for themselves.

## Caption

What if the most powerful “magic” humans possess isn’t supernatural at all…

What if it’s belief?

Words become beliefs.
Beliefs become behaviour.
Behaviour changes reality.

So maybe the real question isn’t:

“Is magic real?”

Maybe it’s:

Who taught you what to believe about it?
