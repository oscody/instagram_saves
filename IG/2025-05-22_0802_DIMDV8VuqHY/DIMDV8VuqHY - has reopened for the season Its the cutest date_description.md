# Warwick Drive-In reopens for the season

A reel promoting the Warwick Drive-In in Warwick, NY, which has reopened for the season. It highlights $13 per person double features (current movies), a dog-friendly, relaxed setup where guests bring their own chairs, blankets, or truck beds, and nearby wineries, shops, and restaurants.

## Caption

@warwickdrivein has reopened for the season! It’s the cutest date night or family night idea… a drive-in theatre an hour north of NYC & Westchester, and 10 mins from NJ! We saw people who brought their own Adirondack chairs, picnic blankets, or camped out in the back of their pick-up trucks. There’s also a ton of cute wineries, shops, restaurants and shopping in town. Definitely try it out!
