### Resources

* [Train and Earn Industrial & Health Care Programs for Youth (CB14 Brooklyn listing) (cb14brooklyn.com)](https://cb14brooklyn.com/resource/train-and-earn-industrial-health-care-programs-for-youth/): page, found by search, not opened, medium confidence. Lists St. Nicks Alliance's free Train and Earn program for ages 18–24, with a construction/industrial track, weekly info sessions and contact details. It doesn't name electrician training, and I only saw this in search results.

### Key steps

1. Check you are eligible: Train and Earn is for ages 18–24, and listings ask for a high school diploma or HSE and being out of work.
2. Go to a weekly info session. Listings say Wednesdays at 1 PM, but they give two addresses: 790 Broadway, 2nd Floor, and the Workforce Center at 92 Throop Ave, Brooklyn. Confirm which one first.
3. Plan to stay at least three hours. Those who continue take a TABE test.
4. Call 718-203-2057 ext. 450 or email TrainandEarn@stnicksalliance.org to confirm an electrician track exists. One listing shows 718-302-2057, so check the number too.
