# Soul Slide: Stopping Without a Heel Brake

A skater presents the Soul Slide as a way to stop quickly without the heel brake, shown in outdoor skate park clips with on-screen labels for each step. The caption and transcript list the same five steps.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Sit on your back leg and extend the other one.
2. Scissor your skates.
3. Draw a C-shape with the leading skate.
4. Bend your knee and get low to break friction.
5. Push with your heel.

## Caption
