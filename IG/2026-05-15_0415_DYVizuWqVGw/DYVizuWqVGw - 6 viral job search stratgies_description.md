# 6 viral job search strategies

A job-search reel that walks through several strategies, using screenshots of LinkedIn job search results and a list of hiring platforms and career-page subdomains. The caption promises the full set of six strategies to people who comment a keyword.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Step 1: Target recently promoted people at target companies, not senior executives, because executives get hundreds of connection requests and mostly ignore them, while newly promoted people are expanding their teams, are motivated to network, and are more likely to respond and refer you internally.
2. Step 2: Apply to jobs with three or fewer applicants. On LinkedIn, search for the job, turn on the filter for under 10 applicants, pick the postings with fewer than three applicants, and prioritize ones posted one to two weeks ago.
3. Step 3: Search Google for jobs posted directly by companies, which the reel says has about a 10% application-to-interview conversion rate, roughly three times LinkedIn's. Use a website or Google power queries for this; the on-screen list of hiring platforms (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, BreezyHR, Remote Rocketship, Pinpoint, Keka, and jobs, careers, people, and talent subdomains) appears alongside it.
