# Free AI tool grades restaurant website visibility

A presenter promotes a free AI grader from order.com that scans a restaurant's website, nearby competitors, Google reviews, photos and mobile/desktop setup. The on-screen example shows a health score and a short list of Google Business Profile fixes.

Did you know that over 75% of people looking to order food online choose a restaurant from the first 5 search results? Most restaurant sites are invisible to Google. ## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. The tool is almost certainly Owner.com's AI Restaurant Grader, not "order.com". That domain has no such tool. It's a lead-gen funnel for Owner's paid ordering platform, and the "75% choose from the top 5" stat is unsourced.
* Checked: 2026-10-23 by claude-sonnet-5-5

### Resources

It doesn't mention reviews, photos or mobile/desktop, or say whether it's free.
* [Owner.com demo quiz grader (owner.com)](https://www.owner.com/demo-quiz-grader): page, found by search, not opened, low confidence. A search snippet described an AI health report graded out of 100 with sub-scores for website, search and local listings. I didn't open the page.

### Key steps

1. Open grader.owner.com.
2. Enter your restaurant name.
3. Wait a few seconds for the scan.
4. Read the health score and the list of fixes, such as Google Business Profile items.
5. Compare the result with the nearby competitors it lists.
