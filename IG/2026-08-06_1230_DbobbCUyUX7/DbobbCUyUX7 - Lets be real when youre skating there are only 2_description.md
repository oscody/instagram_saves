# Skating basics: braking, falling, and pads

A skater on the Manhattan Bridge shares that she stayed injury free after a fall, then rode 50 miles that day. The clip shows her skating, a fall with another skater helping her up, and on-screen text about being asked if she fell.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Learn how to brake.
2. Learn how to fall properly, since it can significantly reduce the chance of injury.
3. Wear knee pads and/or wrist guards, especially if you are new to skating.

## Caption

Let’s be real… when you’re skating, there are only 2 things you really need to know:

1. How to brake. 🛼
2. How to fall. 😅

The first one is pretty self explanatory, but the second one is just as important. Learning how to fall properly can significantly reduce your chances of getting injured. That’s exactly why I was able to pop right back up after this fall with zero injuries. 😌

I’ll always recommend wearing knee pads and/or wrist guards, especially if you’re new to skating. After one nasty fall during my early skating days, I’ve always skate with knee pads. They give me the confidence to skate almost anywhere without worrying about every little mistake.

And because I stayed injury free, I was able to keep rolling and finish 50 miles that day… a new personal record! 💪🏿🛼🥳

Protect yourself, learn how to fall, and keep rolling. Your future self will thank you. 🤝🏿

Ohh and….don’t ever ask me have I fallen yet.  The answer is no. I don’t fall. 🗣️🤪🤣
