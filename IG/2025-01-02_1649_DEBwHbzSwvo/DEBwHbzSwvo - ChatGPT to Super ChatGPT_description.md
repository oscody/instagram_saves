# ChatGPT Mega Prompt: Seven-Part Structure

A speaker presents a "mega prompt" structure for ChatGPT, illustrated with an example of writing a marketing plan for a brand launch in two weeks instead of three months. On-screen text lists the seven elements.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Start the prompt by assigning the AI a persona, such as a digital marketing strategy persona.
2. State the task clearly, such as making a marketing plan.
3. List your constraints, such as limited time, no team, and no money or budget.
4. Add conditions that shape the output, such as asking for it to be creative and unconventional.
5. Define the goal, such as launching the brand within a set timeframe.
6. Specify the format, such as a turbo-style plan with listed activities.
7. Ask for the reasoning behind each suggested activity.
