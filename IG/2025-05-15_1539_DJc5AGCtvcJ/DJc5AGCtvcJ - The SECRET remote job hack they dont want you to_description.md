# Staffing agencies for fast remote job hires

A carousel presenting staffing agencies as the easiest route to a work-from-home job, naming SpectraForce, Insight Global and Beacon Hill with notes on each. The slides show SpectraForce's website and a "1. SpectraForce" heading.

## What it teaches

1. Apply for remote work through staffing agencies, which the post calls the easiest way to get a work-from-home job.
2. Apply to SpectraForce on a desktop or laptop, since it has many different jobs; recruiters have usually replied within a week, and its listings are mainly healthcare roles, so it suits remote healthcare work.
3. Apply to Insight Global, whose recruiters reply quickly, but whose listings are mostly mid- to senior-level roles, so it suits people with significant experience or a degree.
4. Beacon Hill is named as another staffing agency, with no details given.

### Key steps

1. Open the agency's careers or jobs page (SpectraForce, Insight Global, Beacon Hill/bhsg.com) on a desktop or laptop.
2. Search the listings for remote roles that match your field and level.
3. Apply, and upload a resume that matches the posting.
4. Expect a recruiter reply. The creator says SpectraForce replies within about a week and Insight Global replies quickly.
5. Try SpectraForce if you want healthcare roles, and Insight Global if you have mid- or senior-level experience or a degree. These are the creator's opinions and I couldn't confirm them.
