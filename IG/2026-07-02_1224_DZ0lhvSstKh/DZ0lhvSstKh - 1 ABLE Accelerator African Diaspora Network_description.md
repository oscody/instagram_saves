# 6 grants for African and diaspora entrepreneurs

A woman speaks to camera under the overlay "6 real grants for African and diaspora entrepreneurs, up to $1.5M available." The caption lists six programs (ABLE Accelerator, Builders of Africa's Future, Tony Elumelu Foundation, Africa's Business Heroes, Google for Startups Black Founders Fund: Africa, and USADF) with eligibility details for each.

## Caption

1. ABLE Accelerator — African Diaspora Network
If you're a Black or African diaspora founder based in the U.S. with a business already making revenue, this one's fully funded. You get mentorship, investor connections, and access to capital — no cost to you."
2. Builders of Africa's Future — also African Diaspora Network
"This one's for founders based in Africa. Your business needs to be registered and running for at least six months. They're looking at health, education, agriculture, energy, financial inclusion — and you get training, mentorship, and a shot at Silicon Valley visibility.
3. Tony Elumelu Foundation Entrepreneurship Programme
Open to any African entrepreneur, all 54 countries, ages 18 plus, business under 3 years old. $5,000 in seed capital, no repayment, plus training. Apply through TEFConnect.
4. Africa's Business Heroes — Alibaba Philanthropy
$1.5 million dollar funding pool for African-based entrepreneurs this year. Backed by Jack Ma's foundation. Open to any sector — they want bold ideas solving real problems.
5. Google for Startups Black Founders Fund: Africa
If you're building tech and you're a Black founder in Africa — this is equity-free cash, mentorship, and cloud credits to help you scale.
6. U.S. African Development Foundation (USADF)
For grassroots and social enterprises, especially outside the big hubs — rural areas, underserved communities. Applications are ongoing, not a one-time deadline.

## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. The programs are real, but several details are dated. The ABLE 2026 window closed July 12 and the TEF 2026 window closed March 1. The Google fund pages I found were from earlier cohorts. USADF's status is uncertain after the proposed 2025 closure. The $1.5M is the total pool for Africa's Business Heroes, not an amount per founder.

### Key steps

1. Check each program's official site for the current round, since several 2026 windows have closed.
2. Tony Elumelu Foundation: apply on TEFConnect.com when the next call opens.
3. ABLE (African Diaspora Network): apply via ADN's online portal only. Needs a revenue-generating business operating at least 6 months. No cost.
4. Africa's Business Heroes: apply at AfricaBusinessHeroes.org. Reports say it targets established businesses.
5. Google Black Founders Fund Africa: watch Google for Startups for a new cohort call.
6. USADF: confirm on usadf.gov that it is still operating before applying.
