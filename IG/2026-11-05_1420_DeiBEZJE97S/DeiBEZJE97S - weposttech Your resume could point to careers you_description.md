# Seven Claude prompts for career pivots from your resume

A carousel of seven Claude prompts for using a resume to find alternative job titles, transferable skills, qualification gaps, career-change paths, industries and a 30-day job-search plan. The caption adds a note to tailor the resume without inventing experience.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Hidden role finder: ask Claude to analyze your CV and identify 15 job titles matching your experience, skills, achievements and transferable abilities, including roles outside your current industry, ranked by fit, salary potential, demand and entry difficulty.
2. Transferable skills translator: ask Claude to extract every transferable skill employers could value, match each one to industries, departments and job titles, and explain how your past experience shows you can perform those roles.
3. Qualification gap analyzer: paste a target job description into a prompt comparing your CV with it, showing which requirements you already meet, which are partially covered and which are missing, plus the fastest way to close each important gap.
4. Career pivot mapper: ask Claude to create 5 realistic career-pivot paths from your CV, each with the starting role, transferable experience, missing skills, recommended projects, likely salary range and a 30-day transition plan.
5. Best-fit industry scanner: ask Claude to identify 10 industries where your skills could be valuable and rank them by hiring demand, compensation, remote-work potential, career growth and how easily you could enter without starting from zero.
6. Resume positioning rewriter: ask Claude to rewrite your professional summary and experience bullets for a target role, emphasizing relevant achievements, transferable skills, measurable outcomes and industry language without inventing qualifications.
7. Opportunity search strategy: ask Claude to build a 30-day job-search plan from your CV and target roles, including job titles, search keywords, company types, application targets, networking actions, recruiter outreach and weekly performance checkpoints.
