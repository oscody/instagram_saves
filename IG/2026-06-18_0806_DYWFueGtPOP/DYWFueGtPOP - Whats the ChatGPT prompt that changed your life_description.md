# What's the ChatGPT prompt that changed your life?

Grace Ling asks people at an OpenAI GPT-5.5 party in San Francisco for the ChatGPT prompt that changed their lives. She shares her own answers, which center on having AI verify its work and on reverse prompting.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Tell the model or agent to actually run its code and show the logs as proof that it works.
2. Keep going until the AI has verified its own output against your specifications.
3. Reverse prompt: give the AI your goals and ask for the highest-leverage thing it can do now to move you closer to them.
4. Ask the AI what it can do for you based on what it knows about you.
5. Use code generation to turn ideas into quick prototypes instead of writing them down in a notepad.
