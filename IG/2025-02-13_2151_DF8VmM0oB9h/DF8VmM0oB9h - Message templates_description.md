# Networking with peers for job applications

A career creator explains why networking with peers (people at a similar level in the target department) helps when applying for jobs, and walks through how to find and contact them on LinkedIn. The screens show a LinkedIn company page, a personalized connection note, and Google's Interview Warmup tool.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Apply for a role at a company you're interested in, then open the company's LinkedIn page.
2. Click the People tab and search for people in the same department or team as the role (the transcript suggests pasting the job title into the search bar).
3. Filter the results by location and check each profile.
4. Send a personalized connection request to each person, mentioning the role you applied for, rather than a generic message.
5. Once someone responds, ask for a 20-minute call.
6. Before the call, research the company and build a document with company information and a list of questions for the person.
7. Practice your pitch and questions out loud, for example with Google's Interview Warmup tool, and refine your answers based on the feedback.
8. Take notes on the company and role during the call and use them to prepare for your next interview.
