# Two running tips: shorts and heel lock lacing

A couple sets up a "Day 1 of running" scene, and the husband is given two tips before a run: choose proper running shorts for heat and humidity, and stop the foot sliding in the shoe. The video shows the heel loop lacing method on a white running shoe.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Choose running shorts rather than regular shorts, since heat and humidity make them uncomfortable on a run.
2. Find the eyelet at the top back of the shoe (the heel loop).
3. Feed the lace through that eyelet and make a bunny ear loop.
4. Feed the other lace back through the loop to make a second bunny ear, leaving enough space to pass it through.
5. Tighten the laces, then tie them down so the ankle locks in place and the foot stops sliding.
