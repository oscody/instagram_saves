# Xiaomi MiMo-V2 AI Models News Reel

A news-style reel about Xiaomi's MiMo-V2 models: the anonymous "Hunter Alpha" model on OpenRouter, MiMo-V2-Pro, MiMo-V2-Omni and MiMo-V2-TTS. It contrasts a hardware maker building full-stack AI models with Apple's Siri.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## Caption

China just released an anonymous AI model under the codename "Hunter Alpha" on OpenRouter, and it became one of the best models in the world within a week.

Meet Xiaomi's MiMo-V2 Pro, a model that hit top 8 globally on benchmarks while beating Grok and Qwen. And developers are saying the coding quality feels like Claude Opus, which costs five times more per token.

But Xiaomi didn't stop there. They dropped two more models the same week.

MiMo-V2-Omni understands images, video, and audio all at once. And MiMo-V2-TTS is an AI voice model that can whisper, laugh, sigh, and even sing, all from a single text description.

And this is pretty surprising for a hardware company. A company that makes phones, cars, and robots is now building full stack AI models. While a company like Apple has literally failed at making Siri even usable.
