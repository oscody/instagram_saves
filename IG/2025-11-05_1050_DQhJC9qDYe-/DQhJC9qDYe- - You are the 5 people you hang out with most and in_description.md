# Communities to join for getting into tech

A reel listing online communities and creators to follow for getting into software engineering, shown over footage of a person working on a laptop in an airport lounge.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Be intentional about who you spend online time with, since you become like the people you hang out with most.
2. Fill your social feeds with people and topics that help you learn and find work in tech.
3. Join beginner-friendly coding communities for hackathons, career advice, project feedback and code help (The Coding Den, freeCodeCamp's forum and Discord, r/learnprogramming).
4. Follow the career-focused communities (r/cscareerquestions) for interview, salary and job search insight.
5. Follow tech creators who explain coding and tech careers for beginners (the founder of freeCodeCamp, Michelle's Computer, It's That Lady Dev).

## Caption

You are the 5 people you hang out with most and in this digital age, we need to be intentional about who we’re spending online time with. When I was getting into tech, I made sure to fill my socials with people and topics that would actually help me on my journey and this greatly shortened how long it took me to actually learn and get a job as a software engineer.

The Coding Den @discord 
Best for complete beginners learning to code. Find Hackathons, career advice, project guidance, code help and more.

@freecodecamp Forum & Discord
A global hub for self-taught developers offering a full coding curriculum, project feedback, and practical career advice from an active learning community.

r/learnprogramming (reddit)
A beginner-friendly subreddit where you can ask any coding question, share your progress, and learn through real discussions with developers at all levels.

r/cscareerquestions (reddit)
Focused on tech careers, this subreddit provides transparent insights into interviews, salaries, and job search strategies from engineers across the industry.

Big Stage Energy (@bigstage.energy )
In the age of AI, your biggest advantage is showing up in person. Members get access to all conferences (like AfroTech) for free.
