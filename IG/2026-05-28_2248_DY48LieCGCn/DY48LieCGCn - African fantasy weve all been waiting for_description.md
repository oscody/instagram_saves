# Songs of the Sahara: African fantasy graphic novel

A six-slide carousel promoting Songs of the Sahara, an African fantasy graphic novel by Cameroonian creator Eposi Litumbe, shown through character art, a short video clip, a book spread and comic panels. The caption pushes a Kickstarter pre-order that is nearing its deadline.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## Caption

African fantasy we’ve all been waiting for.

Inspired by the kingdoms, myths and legends of pre-colonial West Africa, Songs of the Sahara is a cinematic graphic novel created by 🇨🇲 Cameroonian creator (@eplleseed) Eposi Litumbe.

Warriors. Empires. Ancient powers.A bold fantasy universe brought to life through hand-drawn and hand-painted artwork with no AI used in the creation process.

This is more than a book.It’s an independent African world built from the ground up.

Less than 48 hours remain on the Kickstarter campaign. If the project does not reach its goal in time, the book will not move forward.

Pre-order your copy now. Kickstarter link available in our story.
