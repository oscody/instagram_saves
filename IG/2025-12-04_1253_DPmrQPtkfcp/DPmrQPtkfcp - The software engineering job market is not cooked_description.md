# Three resources for finding software engineering job postings

A reel arguing the software engineering job market is workable if you apply early, naming three job-listing resources: a daily email of new postings, a startup job board, and a GitHub repo of internship and new grad postings. On screen it shows a sample daily email, the SimplifyJobs Summer2026-Internships repo page, and a sheet tracking application statuses.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Treat the job search as a numbers game and apply early, since being first to apply improves your chances.
2. Go to swelist.com, choose internships or new grad, and enter your email to get a daily digest of that day's new tech job posts.
3. Apply to the day's new postings within 24 hours of seeing them.
4. Check wellfound.com for startup job openings, since the post says startups are a good way to break into tech.
5. Save the SimplifyJobs GitHub repositories for new grad and internship postings, which the post says list postings across America and Canada.
