# Career moves for AI-era jobs from the AI Action Plan

## What it teaches

1. Earn certifications in AI-related tools, such as Microsoft Azure AI or AWS Solutions Architect, and list them on your resume.
2. Follow companies leading AI work, such as Microsoft, AWS, and Google, and on their LinkedIn company Jobs pages click the "I'm interested" button on job posts so recruiters are alerted to your profile.
3. Use free employer-led or federally backed learning programs, such as LinkedIn Learning, Microsoft Learn, AWS Academy, or Google's AI courses, to build AI skills.
