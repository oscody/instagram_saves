# Career moves for AI-era jobs from the AI Action Plan

The reel summarizes the White House's American AI Action Plan, which it says points to new jobs in cloud infrastructure, data centers, and tech trades backed by free training and apprenticeships. It then suggests three career moves for getting ahead of these changes.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Earn certifications in AI-related tools, such as Microsoft Azure AI or AWS Solutions Architect, and list them on your resume.
2. Follow companies leading AI work, such as Microsoft, AWS, and Google, and on their LinkedIn company Jobs pages click the "I'm interested" button on job posts so recruiters are alerted to your profile.
3. Use free employer-led or federally backed learning programs, such as LinkedIn Learning, Microsoft Learn, AWS Academy, or Google's AI courses, to build AI skills.

1. Read the plan's workforce section in the White House PDF. It covers prioritizing AI skills in education and workforce funding, expanding Registered Apprenticeships for AI infrastructure occupations, and early-career and pre-apprenticeship programs.

3. Follow Microsoft, AWS and Google on LinkedIn. On their Jobs pages, click "I'm interested" on postings.
4. Use free learning options such as Microsoft Learn, AWS Academy, Google AI courses and LinkedIn Learning.
