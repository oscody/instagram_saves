# Three-day life and business strategy sync framework

A creator introduces a three-day personal strategy sync for choosing an idea, deciding how to pursue it, and setting focus, sorted into three groups: know what, know how, and know focus. Short on-screen words ("taken", "purpose", "sense") appear over clips of the speaker and her partner.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Work out which group you are in: know what, know how, or know focus.
2. Know what: determine your skills, your story, and where the market meets you at the intersection of your passion and purpose.
3. Know how: find where your systems don't exist or don't work, then outline a plan to create relief, restore belief, and keep the planning brief.
4. Know focus: determine what makes sense to keep, so you can return to your who and your must-do.
