# 4 Claude Prompts to Rebuild Your Resume

A six-slide carousel with four Claude prompts for rewriting a resume, passing automated screening, quantifying achievements, and writing a cover letter.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Prompt 1, 6-second resume rewriter: Ask Claude to act as a senior recruiter who screens resumes in under 6 seconds. Have it write a 2-3 sentence summary, rewrite every bullet in XYZ form (accomplished X, measured by Y, by doing Z), add metrics (%, $, scale), swap weak verbs for strong action verbs, cut filler, and keep to 1 page (2 pages if 10+ years of experience). Organize skills into role-aligned categories, simplify education to degree, school and honors, remove red flags (gaps, outdated tools, irrelevant content), and improve visual hierarchy. Provide your resume and job description as input. Output: full rewritten resume, before vs after for the 3 weakest bullets, and a summary of key improvements.
2. Prompt 2, ATS optimization: Ask Claude to act as an ATS expert familiar with Workday, Greenhouse and Lever. Extract keywords from the job description and work them in, format for ATS readability (no tables, clean structure), raise keyword density without sounding unnatural, match skills, titles and phrasing to the target role, and check compatibility with parsing systems. Provide your resume and job description as input. Output: optimized resume, keyword match breakdown, and an ATS score estimate with improvements.
3. Prompt 3, achievement quantifier: Ask Claude to act as a resume strategist and turn every bullet into measurable impact. Add metrics for revenue, cost savings, growth, time saved and scale; quantify leadership, process creation and improvements; use the formula Achieved X, Result Y, by doing Z; and highlight promotions, ownership and outcomes. Provide your current bullet points as input. Output: before vs after for every bullet and fully quantified achievement statements.
4. Prompt 4, cover letter: Ask Claude to act as a recruitment director and write a 250-300 word cover letter. Open with a strong, company-specific opening (not generic), show research on the company's product, strategy or a recent move, match 3 key skills to the job requirements, include one quantified achievement, show cultural fit naturally, suggest a relevant contribution in the first 90 days, and close confidently with a clear next step. Provide the job description, 3 achievements and your interest in the company as input. Output: a ready-to-send cover letter with sections marked for per-company customization.
