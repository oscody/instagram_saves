## What it teaches

1. Set aside 30 minutes every day for the practice.
2. Start with breath work to calm the nervous system.
3. Move into a conversational prayer with Christ so the practice is grounded in His will.
4. Pick a scene likely to happen after you have become the renewed person.
5. Step into that life and think every thought that person would have, exude gratitude, and feel the physical sensations.
6. Stay with that feeling until it feels like fact.
7. Repeat every day for 21 days to reprogram your self-image.
