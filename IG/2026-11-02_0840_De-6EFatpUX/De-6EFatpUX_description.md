# Seven ChatGPT prompts for finding income opportunities

A man in a maroon shirt says he gave ChatGPT his salary and career history and got income ideas, then lists the seven prompts he used. The on-screen text and caption match.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Give ChatGPT your salary and career history first, since each prompt refers to your background.
2. Prompt 1, Find My Hidden Skills: ask for the 10 most valuable skills you underestimate, why each is valuable, who would pay for it, and how AI could help you monetize it.
3. Prompt 2, Find Income Opportunities: ask for 10 realistic ways to earn an extra 1-3k per month, with startup cost, time commitment, difficulty level, and earning potential.
4. Prompt 3, Monetize My Experience: ask it to act as a business strategist and identify services, consulting offers, digital products, or freelance work using skills you already have.
5. Prompt 4, Find My Highest-Leverage Opportunity: ask what to focus on first to raise income as quickly as possible with current skills, and why.
6. Prompt 5, Show Me What Pays More: ask for roles, industries, or opportunities that pay significantly more than your current position, and what makes you qualified for them.
7. Prompt 6, Create an AI-Powered Side Hustle: ask for 5 AI-assisted services you could offer that take less than 5 hours per week to start.
8. Prompt 7, Build My 90-Day Plan: ask for a step-by-step plan for more career security and extra income over 90 days, based on your background, schedule, and experience.
