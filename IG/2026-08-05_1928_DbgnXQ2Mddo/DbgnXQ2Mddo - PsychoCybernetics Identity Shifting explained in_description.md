# Psycho-Cybernetics identity shifting principles

A short video by a man speaking in front of a microphone, with numbered on-screen text, presenting Psycho-Cybernetics principles about self-image, goals and identity. The caption says it covers 13 principles.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Your performance cannot exceed your self-image, so change the self-image first.
2. Your brain is an automatic goal-seeking machine: give it a clear target and it will find a way.
3. Avoid negative targets, because they work against you.
4. Your subconscious cannot tell a real experience from one you vividly imagine.
5. Visualize the end result rather than the hard work.
6. Act as if you are already the person you want to be.
7. Treat mistakes as data to correct your course; you make mistakes but you are not a mistake.
8. Spend 15 minutes every day mentally rehearsing your biggest wins.
9. Happiness is a habit you practice, not an endpoint you reach.
10. Dehypnotize yourself from the limits society gave you.
11. Forgive yourself instantly so you can move forward.
