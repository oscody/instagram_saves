### Key steps

1. Search the iOS App Store for 'Google AI Edge Eloquent'. It was iOS-only at launch, and Android was said to be coming.
2. Dictate; text appears live and filler words are removed after a pause.
3. Keep it fully offline for on-device Gemma speech recognition. Cloud mode is optional and adds Gemini refinement.
4. Optionally add custom words or import names and jargon from Gmail.
5. Pick a rewrite style: key points, formal, short or long.
6. Copy the text into your AI app.
