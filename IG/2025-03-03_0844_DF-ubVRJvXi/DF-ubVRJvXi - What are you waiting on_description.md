### Key steps

1. Go to learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training and pick a learning path, or use Career paths to match a job goal.
2. Work through the modules at your own pace. Training content is free.
3. Check each path or event for a free exam voucher. Not every path has one.
4. Look at leap.microsoft.com for Leap, a 16-week immersive program. The page doesn't say how to apply, so check its Pathways link.
