# Fans plead for The Witch: Part 3

A fan reel over action clips from The Witch films, with on-screen text saying the franchise was meant to be a trilogy and asking director Park Hoon-jung for Part 3. The caption gives background on the two films, the Tyrant spin-off, and the fact that Part 3 has not been officially confirmed.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## Caption

The Witch is a South Korean movie series made by director Park Hoon-jung, about young people who were changed by science to have super powers. The first movie, The Witch: Part 1. The Subversion, came out on June 27, 2018, and starred Kim Da-mi, Jo Min-su, Park Hee-soon, and Choi Woo-shik. The second movie, The Witch: Part 2. The Other One, came out on June 15, 2022, and starred Shin Si-ah, Park Eun-bin, Seo Eun-soo, Jin Goo, Sung Yoo-bin, and Jo Min-su, with Shin Si-ah playing Ark 1, the twin sister of Ja-yoon, the girl from the first movie. The second movie did very well, passing two million ticket sales in just 11 days in Korean theaters, and later news reports said it also passed its break-even point. Over both movies, the story world grew bigger, built around ideas like enhanced humans, gene changes, groups of super-powered people, and secret test-subject networks. Director Park Hoon-jung has said he wants to make this a full trilogy and grow the story world even more. In 2024, this same story world grew again through a new spin-off show called Tyrant (폭군). Tyrant is a four-episode series that was released on Disney+, with all episodes out by August 14, 2024. Tyrant brought new attention back to the Witch story world when it came out in 2024, but at the same time, more fans and critics have started saying it feels irresponsible that talk of a real Witch sequel has quietly disappeared, since the first two movies dropped many story hints that were never followed up. As things stand now, The Witch: Part 3 has not been officially confirmed. There is no announced production start, no confirmed cast, and no official release date for the movie. No casting news has come out at all, which makes it hard for anyone to guess a real release date. So right now, The Witch: Part 3 is something the director has talked about wanting to make, and fans are hoping for, but it has not moved from an idea into an officially confirmed movie.
