# Daily 400-rep bodyweight routine for 14 days

A shirtless man in a gym demonstrates four bodyweight moves, each done for 100 reps: jumping jacks, single-leg raises, toe touches, and knee raises. The caption and voiceover present them as a daily routine to do for 14 days.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Do 100 jumping jacks to get the body moving and raise blood flow.
2. Do 100 single-leg raises to build stability and core support.
3. Do 100 toe touches to loosen tight hips and hamstrings.
4. Do 100 knee raises to work the lower abs and hip flexors.
5. Repeat the four-move set every day, done in the morning on an empty stomach, for 14 days.
