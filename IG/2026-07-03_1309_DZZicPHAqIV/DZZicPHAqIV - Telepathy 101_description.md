# Telepathy 101: five tips to read people

A reel presenting a five-point list of habits for reading people better, with the speaker's narration and on-screen images of puzzles, chess, body language books and sculptures. The title promises "telepathy", but the content is about social perception and interaction.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Solve puzzles and play chess: puzzles build problem solving and chess builds pattern recognition.
2. Read biographies to learn from other people's mistakes instead of only your own.
3. Study body language to build situational awareness, communicate better and form stronger relationships. On-screen books include Joe Navarro's 'The Dictionary of Body Language' and Allan and Barbara Pease's 'The Definitive Book of Body Language'.
4. Study human nature, including people's psychological behavior, so you can predict how they will act.
5. Take more risks by putting yourself in social situations often, such as taking jobs that require interacting with people, to sharpen situational awareness and reading people.
