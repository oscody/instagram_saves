# ChatGPT prompts for personal finance review

A six-slide carousel that opens with a headline claiming ChatGPT can review your whole financial life in an hour, then shows five numbered prompts for ChatGPT, each with a [PASTE YOUR DETAILS] placeholder.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Paste your own financial details into the [PASTE YOUR DETAILS] placeholder of a prompt.
2. Money audit: ask ChatGPT to act as a personal CFO, analyze expenses, debts, savings, investments and goals, flag unnecessary expenses, missed opportunities and mistakes, then create the simplest plan to improve your finances.
3. Wealth plan: give your current situation and ask for a long-term roadmap that prioritizes cutting expenses, building savings, managing debt, investing wisely and long-term security.
4. Cash flow optimization: list your monthly expenses and ask which to cut, which to keep, what to automate and how to allocate money each month so every dollar has a purpose.
5. Money leaks: give your spending history and ask for every unnecessary expense, subscription, impulse purchase and hidden leak ranked by annual cost, with the changes that save the most for the least lifestyle impact.
6. Debt destroyer: list your debts and ask for a repayment strategy based on cash flow, interest rates and goals, with the logic explained and ways to reduce debt as efficiently as possible.
