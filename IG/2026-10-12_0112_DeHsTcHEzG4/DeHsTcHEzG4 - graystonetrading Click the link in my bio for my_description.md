# ChatGPT prompts for personal finance audits

A slide carousel that says ChatGPT can review a personal financial situation in an hour if you ask the right questions, then lists five copy-paste prompts (money audit, wealth plan, cash flow, money leaks, debt). The final slides promote a free live masterclass on a trading system.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Gather your complete financial picture: expenses, debts, savings, investments, and goals.
2. Money audit: paste your details into a prompt asking ChatGPT to act as a personal CFO, identify unnecessary expenses, missed opportunities and financial mistakes, then create the simplest plan to improve your finances.
3. Wealth plan: paste your current situation and ask for a long-term roadmap that prioritizes cutting unnecessary expenses, building savings, managing debt, and investing wisely.
4. Cash flow optimization: list your monthly expenses and ask ChatGPT what to cut, what to keep, what to automate, and how to allocate money each month.
5. Money leaks: paste your spending history and ask for every unnecessary expense, subscription, impulse purchase, and lifestyle increase ranked by annual cost, with the changes that save the most with the least lifestyle impact.
6. Debt destroyer: list your debts and ask for a repayment strategy based on cash flow, interest rates, and goals, with the logic explained and ways to reduce debt as efficiently as possible.
