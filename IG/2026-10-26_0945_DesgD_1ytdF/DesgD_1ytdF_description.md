# How to duck dive under breaking waves

An animated surf tip reel showing a 3D character, Kevin, learning how to duck dive under whitewater on a surfboard. The narration and on-screen text walk through the dive in order.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Paddle toward the whitewater with speed and start the dive just before it reaches you, rather than waiting for it to hit.
2. Push the nose deep underwater with both hands.
3. Drive the tail down with your back foot, lifting your hips and following the board beneath the wave.
4. Once deep enough, level the board out and pull yourself toward it.
5. Stay streamlined while the strongest turbulence passes above you.
6. Angle the nose back toward the surface and let the board's buoyancy pull you up behind the wave.
