### Key steps

1. Open grow.google/career-dreamer. It is available in the US only, so you need a US location or VPN from elsewhere.
2. Enter a few basic facts about your roles, skills, education and interests.
3. Review the Career Identity Statement the AI drafts for you, and copy it into your resume or profile.
4. Explore the visual map of matching careers, and open the ones that interest you to see details and nearby jobs.
5. Use the Gemini hand-off to work on a cover letter, resume edits and first steps toward your goal.
