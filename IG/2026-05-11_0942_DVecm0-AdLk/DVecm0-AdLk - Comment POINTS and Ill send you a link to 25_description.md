# Finding grants by identity and affinity bias

A filmmaker explains that grant success depends on who the funder is likely to back, not only the project, and shows her AT&T Dream in Black win as an example. The post then points to a funding listing for US filmmakers.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Focus on who is most likely to fund your project, not only on what project you want funded.
2. Consider affinity bias: funders are more likely to support people whose backgrounds, interests or identities are close to their own.
3. List the traits that make you who you are, beyond race, such as other backgrounds, experiences and communities.
4. Search for narrow programs that match those traits, such as local grants, cultural fellowships and identity-based funding, rather than broad general grants.
5. Apply to narrower pools, because a smaller applicant pool gives a greater chance of success.
