# Vegan Jamaican Stew Peas Ingredient Notes

A reel showing garlic being peeled and chopped beside carrot pieces, a pot of kidney bean stew being stirred, and a finished bowl of the stew over rice. The caption lists the ingredients for vegan Jamaican stew peas with notes on each and substitutes.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Scotch bonnet peppers add heat; use habaneros instead if you are sensitive to spice.
2. Add carrots for a hint of sweetness, flavor, color, and nutrition.
3. Use red onion; yellow onion or shallots work in a pinch.
4. Include garlic and scallions, and don't skimp on them.
5. Use fresh thyme if you have it; if you only have dried, use a half teaspoon.
6. Season with salt, black pepper, dried parsley, smoked paprika, dried thyme, and allspice.
7. Cook in extra virgin olive oil; grapeseed or coconut oil also work.
8. Use low-sodium vegetable stock.
9. Add bell peppers, either regular ones chopped small or mini sweet peppers.
10. Use kidney beans, dried or canned; the post notes they can help reduce blood sugar levels.
11. Add pimento seeds (also called allspice or pimento berries), which look like black peppercorns and add flavor.
12. Thicken with arrowroot; all-purpose flour, regular starch, or tapioca flour can substitute.

YOUR GROCERY LIST:

🫘Scotch bonnet peppers. These pack some heat, so if you’re sensitive to spice, you can use habaneros instead. 
🫘Carrots. For a hint of sweetness, flavor, color, and nutrition.
🫘Red onion. In a pinch, a yellow onion or shallots will work. 
🫘Garlic and scallions. Don’t skimp on these!
🫘Thyme. I recommend fresh, but if you only have dried, use a half teaspoon. 
🫘Spices. The other seasonings in this dish come from salt, black pepper, dried parsley, smoked paprika, dried thyme, and allspice.
🫘Olive oil. I prefer the organic Extra virgin olive oil. You can also use grapeseed or coconut oil.
🫘Vegetable stock. I use low-sodium stock from Pacific Foods.
🫘Bell peppers. You can use regular sized bell peppers, chopped small or mini sweet peppers.
🫘Kidney beans. Can help reduce blood sugar levels. You can use dried beans or canned.
🫘Pimento seeds. These look like “black peppercorn”, but are also known as allspice, pimento berries, etc. Adds additional flavor.
🫘Arrowroot. If you don’t have arrowroot starch, feel free to substitute with all-purpose flour, regular starch, or tapioca flour, which all act as a thickening agent.
