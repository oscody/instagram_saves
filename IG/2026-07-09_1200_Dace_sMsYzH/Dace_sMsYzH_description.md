# Turn meeting notes into LinkedIn post drafts

A woman with a wireless mic explains how she uses meeting transcripts from Granola and Claude to generate LinkedIn post drafts on autopilot. Logos for Granola and Claude appear on screen.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Connect your meeting transcription app (Granola) to Claude.
2. Set the automation to run every day at 5pm.
3. Have it go through all of your meeting transcripts and pick 2-3 unique ideas that suit a LinkedIn post.
4. Give it frameworks to follow: a hook, a call to action at the end, and information about your ICP (ideal customer profile).
5. Have it save the LinkedIn draft into your Notion database automatically.

### Key steps

1. In Claude, open Settings > Connectors, find Granola and sign in with OAuth.
2. Check your Granola plan. Transcripts and full history need Business or Enterprise. Free covers only the last 30 days of your own notes.
3. Connect Notion as a connector in Claude too, so it can save drafts.
4. Create a scheduled task in Claude that runs daily at 5pm. I found no official docs for scheduling, so check the current Claude UI.
5. Prompt it to go through the day's Granola transcripts and pick 2-3 unique ideas that suit a LinkedIn post.
6. Include a hook framework, a closing call to action, and a description of your ICP in the prompt.
7. Tell it to save each draft to your Notion database.
