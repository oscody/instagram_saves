# Never Run Out of Things to Say

A whiteboard video where the speaker gives three conversation rules for keeping talk going with women. The rules are to build on what she says, make statements instead of asking questions, and bring in personal details about yourself.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Stack, don't switch: build on what she says and elaborate instead of jumping from topic to topic. Pull back layers, make assumptions and observations, and keep the conversation on one thread.
2. Make more statements, not questions: instead of asking 'Where are you from?', say something like 'Something tells me you're not from here,' which invites her to share.
3. Add personal input: when the conversation feels too centered on her, share something about yourself, such as a plan you're considering, so she asks why and starts offering her own observations.
