# Six Claude prompts for learning faster

A carousel that presents Claude prompt templates for learning a topic faster, with the prompt text shown on each slide. Five prompts appear across the slides, though the caption and cover refer to six.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Cheat sheet: ask Claude to summarize a topic's key concepts on one page with bullet points, simple diagrams and examples so you can understand it in 5 minutes.
2. 20-hour plan: ask for a 20-hour plan focused only on the most important things, split into 10 sessions of 2 hours each, with the best resources and a 15-minute review after every session.
3. Learning ladder: ask Claude to divide the topic into 5 levels from easy to hard and show how to move from Level 1 (beginner) to Level 5 (advanced), with a clear goal at each step.
4. Quiz until you break: after learning a topic, ask Claude for 10 questions that get gradually harder, then get a score after each answer and an explanation of what you got wrong.
5. Best resources: ask for the top 5 resources (books, videos, courses or people) to learn the topic quickly, with an explanation of why each one is useful for you.
