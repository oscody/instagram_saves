# Six Claude prompts for learning faster

A carousel with a caption claiming Claude can make learning much faster. Each of six slides shows one numbered prompt for studying a topic, with the prompt text in quotes. The last slide is a call to comment for the full prompt list.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Make a one-page cheat sheet: ask Claude to summarize the key concepts of a topic on one page, with bullet points, simple diagrams and examples, so you can understand it in 5 minutes.
2. Plan a 20-hour learning sprint: ask for a plan that focuses only on the most important high-yield things, split into 10 sessions of 2 hours each, with the best resources included and a 15-minute review after every session.
3. Build a learning ladder: ask Claude to divide the topic into 5 levels from easy to hard and show how to move from Level 1 (beginner) to Level 5 (advanced), with a clear goal at each step.
4. Quiz yourself until you break: after learning a topic, ask for 10 questions that slowly get harder, then get a score after each answer and an explanation of what you got wrong.
5. Find the best learning resources: ask for the top 5 resources (books, videos, courses or people) to learn the topic quickly, with an explanation of why each one is useful for you.
6. Use the Feynman technique: ask Claude to explain the topic in very simple words, then explain it back to it, have it point out what you missed and re-teach it, and repeat until you can explain it clearly by yourself.
