# Home lower-ab circuit with a shampoo bottle

A shirtless man demonstrates a floor ab circuit at home, using a shampoo bottle as the household object, with the caption saying to do it twice daily to target stubborn lower belly fat.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Grab a household object, such as a shampoo bottle, to use as the prop for the circuit.
2. Up and over: lie on the floor, keep your feet together, and bring the bottle up and over on each tap cue.
3. In and outs: keep your feet together, kick your feet all the way out, and keep your knees straight.
4. Alternating in and outs: move your legs in and out in turn, right then left, repeating.
5. Finish with ten leg raises.
6. Do the circuit twice a day (the caption says 2x daily).
