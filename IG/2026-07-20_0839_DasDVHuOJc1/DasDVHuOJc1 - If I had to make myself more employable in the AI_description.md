# Five AI systems to build for employability

A woman at a laptop lists five AI-powered systems she would build to become more employable, framed as useful if you have few connections and want opportunities. Each system comes with a named set of tools.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Build a networking CRM that flags contacts you haven't spoken to in a while and suggests reaching out (tools: LinkedIn, Clay, Claude, Google Calendar).
2. Build a searchable second brain of saved articles, books, podcasts and frameworks, then ask AI what ideas you've saved about an industry and what patterns recur (tools: NotebookLM, Obsidian, Capacities, or Mem).
3. Build a skills tracker that compares your current skills against target roles, job descriptions and people in those roles, then asks which skills keep appearing, what you're missing, and what project could prove a skill (tools: Claude Projects, Perplexity, Google Sheets).
4. Build a personal brand engine that stores your ideas, stories, opinions and achievements, turns them into content, and tracks topics, posts and people in your industry worth engaging with (tools: Notion, ChatGPT Projects, Feedly AI, Make).
5. Build an achievement vault of successful projects and positive feedback, then before an interview paste in the job description and ask AI for examples from your career that prove you can do the role (tools: Notion, Google Drive, ChatGPT Projects).

## Caption

If I had to make myself more employable in the AI era, these are the 5 things I’d build ASAP - (because applications are just not cutting it anymore ‼️)

1️⃣ A networking CRM that tells me who I shouldn’t forget-  Think of it as a personal relationship manager that reminds you: “Hey, you haven’t spoken to this person in 6 months. They just moved companies. This might be a good time to reach out.”
Tools: LinkedIn + Clay + Claude + Google Calendar

2️⃣ A second brain 🧠 The best ideas usually disappear because we don’t have a system to capture them.
I’d build a searchable library of everything I consume (articles, books, podcasts, frameworks) and use AI to connect the dots.
Instead of starting from zero every time, you can ask:
“What ideas have I saved about this industry?”
“What patterns keep showing up?”

Tools: NotebookLM, Obsidian, Capacities, or Mem.

3️⃣ A future skills and skill-gap tracker -  build a system that compares my current skills against:
- roles I want,  descriptions I’m interested in and people already doing those roles

Then it tells me:
“What skills are repeatedly appearing?”
“What am I missing?”
“What project can I build to prove this skill?”

Tools: Claude Projects + Perplexity + Google Sheets

4️⃣ Personal brand engine + reputation tracker - Your expertise is invisible if nobody knows what you think.

I’d build a system that stores my ideas, stories, opinions, achievements and turns them into content, while also tracking conversations I should be part of.

It could tell you:
* topics you should talk about
* posts worth engaging with
* people in your industry you should build relationships with

Tools: Notion + ChatGPT Projects + Feedly AI + Make

5️⃣ An achievement vault that becomes my career memory - I’d store my successful projects, positive feedback. Then before an interview, I’d drop in the job description and ask:

“Find examples from my career that prove I can do this role.” Instead of trying to remember stories under pressure, you already have your proof.

Tools: Notion + Google Drive + ChatGPT Projects.
