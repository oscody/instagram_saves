# Your attention and the versions of you

A six-slide carousel with quantum-physics imagery argues that the suffering version of you persists partly because you keep returning to it with attention. It contrasts that with other possible selves, such as the one building, exploring, or taking risks.

## Caption

What if the version of you that’s suffering… only exists because you keep observing it?

Your mind has a strange habit: it keeps returning to the same regret, fear, failure, or version of yourself you wish you could escape.

And the more attention you give it, the more space it occupies in your reality.

There are other versions of you too. The one creating. Exploring. Taking risks. Becoming someone you haven’t met yet.

Maybe the question isn’t “How do I become someone else?”

Maybe it’s “Which version of me am I continuously observing?”
