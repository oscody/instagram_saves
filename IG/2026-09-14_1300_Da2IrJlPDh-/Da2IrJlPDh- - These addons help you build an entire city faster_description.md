# Blender addons for building a city faster

A caption and slideshow of Blender addons for building a city faster. The frames name BlenderGIS (free, converts real-world maps into 3D city models), Buildify (paid, builds buildings and towers in seconds), and Procedural Crowds (free, populates a city with hundreds of people).

## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. All three addons exist and suit city building, but the post's pricing looks swapped. Buildify is free (Gumroad, pay-what-you-want style), while Procedural Crowds is a paid addon (about $18 Standard, $45 Pro).
* Checked: 2026-10-22 by claude-sonnet-5-5

### Resources

* [BlenderGIS (github.com)](https://github.com/domlysz/BlenderGIS): repo, found by search, verified, high confidence. Free addon that imports GIS data, OSM buildings and roads, and terrain into Blender.
* [Buildify (paveloliva.gumroad.com)](https://paveloliva.gumroad.com/l/buildify): product, found by search, not opened, medium confidence. The 80.lv article names it a free Geometry Nodes procedural building library by Pavel Oliva and links the Gumroad page; I did not open the page itself.
* [Procedural Crowds (superhivemarket.com)](https://superhivemarket.com/products/procedural-crowds): product, found by search, not opened, medium confidence. Search results list it as a paid Geometry Nodes crowd addon by Difffuse Studio; I did not open the page.

### Key steps

1. BlenderGIS: download the repo ZIP from GitHub (needs Blender 2.83 or newer).
2. In Blender, go to Edit > Preferences > Add-ons > Install and select the ZIP, then enable it.
3. For elevation data, register at opentopography.org and add the free API key in the addon preferences.
4. Use the GIS menu to load a web map and import the OSM buildings and roads.
5. Buildify: download the free pack from Gumroad and open its .blend file. Apply its Geometry Nodes to your building shapes.
6. Procedural Crowds: buy it, install the ZIP as an addon, and add a crowd on a surface or curve.
