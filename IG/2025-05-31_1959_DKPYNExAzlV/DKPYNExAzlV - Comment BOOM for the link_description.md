### Key steps

1. Open atoms.dev (mgx.dev redirects there) and sign in.
2. Describe the app in chat, or give a reference such as 'Make a copy of the Airbnb app'.
3. The agent team (Emma as product manager, Bob as architect, Alex as engineer, Mike as team leader) plans and builds it.
4. Refine with follow-ups, e.g. 'Change the accent colors to blue and a dark background'.
5. For voice input, use a separate dictation tool (OS or browser dictation) to speak into the chat box, as the post suggests.
6. Deploy, or export the code to GitHub.
