# 17 questions to ask her on a date

Two people dance in a room while on-screen text announces "17 questions to ask her on a date that will make her never forget you." The caption lists the 17 questions.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Explain a scar on your body. What's the story behind it?
2. What's a moment in your life that changed you, but nobody really knows about?
3. When was the last time you cried, and what caused it?
4. What's something you're still healing from?
5. What's a lesson you learned the hard way?
6. If your childhood self met you today, what would surprise her the most?
7. What's something you've lost that you still think about?
8. Who was the most influential person in your life, and how did they shape you?
9. What's a risk you took that completely changed the course of your life?
10. What's a memory that instantly makes you smile every time you think about it?
11. What's something you've always wanted to do but haven't had the courage to try yet?
12. What version of yourself are you trying to become right now?
13. What's a belief you had when you were younger that you no longer believe?
14. If you could relive one day from your life, which day would it be and why?
15. What's the biggest challenge you've overcome that you're proud of?
16. What do you think people misunderstand about you when they first meet you?
17. Five years from now, what would make you feel like your life turned out exactly the way you hoped?
