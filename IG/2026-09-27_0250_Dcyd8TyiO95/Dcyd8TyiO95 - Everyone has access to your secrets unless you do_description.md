# Make Claude chats and artifacts private

The reel shows a Google search returning public Claude artifacts, then a screen-recorded walkthrough of the Claude app's account menu and settings. The speaker says these settings keep shared conversations and artifacts from being found by anyone.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Open the Claude app and click your name in the bottom left.
2. Click Settings.
3. Click Memory.
4. Turn off "include sensitive topics in memory."
5. Click Privacy.
6. Under shared chats and shared artifacts, click Manage.
7. Set the visibility to private.

### Key steps

1. Open Claude, click your name (bottom left), then Settings.
2. Go to Privacy. Under Shared chats, click Manage and unshare any chats you don't want public.
3. Do the same for shared or published artifacts.
4. For a single chat, use Share and change the visibility to private.
5. Optional: in Memory, turn off including sensitive topics. This doesn't affect search indexing.
