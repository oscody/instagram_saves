## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. The Aqua Slidecoaster is real, and it is billed as the first hybrid coaster and waterslide with a magnetic launch. The "only on Norwegian Aqua" claim is outdated, because the same ride is planned for Norwegian Luna.
