### Key steps

1. Open the NYC Housing Connect listing (ID 5179) and create an account.
2. Check eligibility: income roughly $31,200–$181,740 depending on household size and unit.
3. Apply online before the deadline. It was Oct 28, 2024, so this one has passed.
4. Units: 12 studios from $784, 50 one-beds from $828, 21 two-beds from $967, 7 three-beds from $1,093.
5. No application fee or deposit. Tenants pay electricity, including electric stove and heat.
6. Preferences: 20% for Community Board 6 residents, 5% for NYC municipal employees, plus disability set-asides.
7. For a current chance, watch Housing Connect for new lotteries. The similar 499 President St lottery (88 units) also had 2-beds at $967.
