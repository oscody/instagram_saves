# Department of War Cyber Apprenticeship Program Info

The creator shares details of a 12-month, paid Department of War Cyber Apprenticeship Program, showing its program page on screen and listing its positions and requirements. She says the posting will appear on USAJobs.com in the coming months.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Eligibility: applicants must be over 18 years of age.
2. Eligibility: applicants must be United States citizens.
3. Eligibility: applicants must be able to obtain and maintain a government security clearance.
4. Positions covered: cyber defense analyst, cyber defense infrastructure support specialist, and cyber defense incident responder.
5. Where to watch for it: the posting will go up on USAJobs.com, which the creator says will open within the next couple of months.
