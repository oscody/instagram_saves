# Baron Samedi and Haiti's uncredited skull image

The reel claims the top hat, skull face, cigar and rum bottle associated with Baron Samedi, a lwa in Haitian voodoo, originated in Haiti. It says the image later spread through Hollywood, Halloween and Día de los Muertos art without credit to Haiti.
