# Baron Samedi and Haiti's uncredited skull image

The reel claims the top hat, skull face, cigar and rum bottle associated with Baron Samedi, a lwa in Haitian voodoo, originated in Haiti. It says the image later spread through Hollywood, Halloween and Día de los Muertos art without credit to Haiti.

## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. Baron Samedi's top hat, skull-like face, cigar and rum come from Haitian Vodou, where he heads the Gede lwa. The claim that Haiti is uncredited for Día de los Muertos art is overstated, because Mexican skull imagery has its own roots. Hollywood borrowing is plausible, but I found no source for it.
