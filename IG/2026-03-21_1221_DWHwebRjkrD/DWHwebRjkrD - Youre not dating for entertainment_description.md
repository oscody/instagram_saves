# Seven dates to test compatibility before commitment

A man sits in a blue chair in front of a slatted wooden wall and talks through seven dates he considers necessary before deciding on a relationship. The caption frames dating as a way to check alignment, focusing on consistency, discipline, and how someone shows up when it isn't convenient.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Date 1, a lunch or picnic: keep it sober and free of loud music and distractions, so you can talk, learn how the other person thinks, their values, and their love languages.
2. Date 2, a gym or active date: look for someone who cares about their health, since that affects how they show up in a relationship. Perfection isn't required.
3. Date 3, a dinner date: observe how they treat staff, handle money, stay present, use table manners, and carry themselves.
4. Date 4, a fun or creative date such as an escape room, game night, or game bar: notice whether they are playful, competitive, and open-minded.
5. Date 5, meeting each other's friends: see how your friends respond to them and how their friends respond to you, since their circle reflects them.
6. Date 6, a home visit: see how they live, including cleanliness, organization, and whether the space feels peaceful or chaotic, since it reveals their habits.
7. Date 7, a trip of at least three days together: see how they explore, what they are interested in, and whether you can travel well together.
