# 10 Fall/Winter Fashion Pickups for Men

A short men's fashion video listing fall and winter clothing staples, with a few styling tips for each. The frames show the presenter in a black henley and light sweatpants, holding up a black jacket and a khaki jacket on hangers.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Get a fitted henley; the post suggests shopping at H&M, Zara, Boohoo Man, Full Navy, and Unico, where it can often be found at a decent price.
2. Get a few quarter-zip tops (transcribed as 'quarters') in plain basic colors, found at H&M and larger chain brands.
3. Get a denim jacket, ideally cropped; wear it over a white tee, or with jeans or slacks.
4. Get boots (the post names 'Tim's') or a utility shoe, which look stylish and handle rain or snow.
5. Get wide-leg pants (jeans, carpenter pants, or sweatpants) and pair them with a tighter or cropped top so the proportions match.
6. Wear flannels either over a beater, unbuttoned all the way, or with no shirt underneath.
