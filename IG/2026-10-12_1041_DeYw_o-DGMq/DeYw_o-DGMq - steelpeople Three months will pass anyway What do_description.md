# Black Notebook Rule: Three-Month Life Plan

An 11-slide carousel with written prompts for a notebook-based self-review, followed by a three-month plan split into monthly focuses. Each slide pairs the text with a warm, stock-style photo of people writing or planning.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Get a black notebook and a pen, and find a quiet moment alone.
2. Where am I now: write honestly about your life financially, emotionally and physically, what you do every day, what you do just to look busy, and what you are putting off.
3. Where should I be: write how much you should be earning, what your daily routine should look like, who you should have become by now, and what level of discipline you should have.
4. What got you off track, without blaming anyone: note the decisions that brought you here, the behavior patterns you keep repeating, how you got in your own way, where you showed weakness, and what you stopped doing.
5. Month 1, cut out the excess: identify what you will give up right now, such as excessive social media use, bad habits, or time with people who drain you. Build one simple daily habit: wake up at the same time every day and complete one task each day.
6. Month 2, build a foundation: define a simple morning and evening routine and 3 daily commitments that are non-negotiable. Stick to your schedule, do what you planned, and don't negotiate with laziness.
7. Month 3, broaden your horizons: raise your standard of living by setting specific goals for finances, productivity and personal growth, and take on bigger challenges with more discipline, responsibility and action.
8. Every 15 days, revisit the notebook and answer: What have I done to get closer to the life I want to live? Where have I shown weakness? What will I change tomorrow?
