# Tracer: muzzle-camera real-life FPS build

A creator presents Tracer, a muzzle-mounted camera and AI companion app that detects human targets and what a shot hits, with plans for multiplayer matches, simulated UAVs and airstrikes. The clips show a 3D printer, soldering on a small battery charging board, and the app screen with accuracy, ammo, lives and a "MISS · PERSON" feed entry.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## Caption

Building a Real-life FPS. 🎯

This is TRACER a muzzle-mounted camera + AI companion app that turns any shot into real hit registration.

Here’s how it works: 
If a human’s behind your crosshairs when you pull the trigger, it’s a hit.
No vests. No sensors on the target. 
Just the gun and the software.

And it’s not just people. 
TRACER can tell what you hit. 
Walls, TVs, dogs, cats — whatever’s downrange, it knows.

Large-scale multiplayer with real-time damage and eliminations. 
Simulated UAVs, airstrikes, the whole battlefield layer.
