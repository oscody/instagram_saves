# Fantasy and sci-fi books inspired by African myth

A six-slide carousel from @whatrosemaryreads for Africa Day that lists fantasy and sci-fi books inspired by African myth, folklore, history and culture. Each slide shows book covers with a short description of the series, such as Tomi Adeyemi's Legacy of Orisha, Ayana Gray's Beasts trilogy and Namina Forna's The Gilded Ones.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## Caption

Happy Africa Day!

During Read Africa Month, @readsandart and I created a post celebrating fantasy books inspired by African myth and folklore, and while curating the list I found myself reflecting on my own reading.

I realised that although I’ve read several fantasy books and series that fall within this category, many of the fantasy worlds I know are rooted in myths, legends and folklore from outside of Africa.

So I spent some time researching, revisiting recommendations, and gathering even more titles to create a list that stretches across fantasy and sci-fi inspired by African mythology, history and culture.

Some of these books have been on my radar for a while, others were completely new discoveries, but putting this list together reminded me how expansive and imaginative African inspired fantasy truly is.

This felt less like making a recommendation list and more like opening a door I want to keep walking through.

If you have more favourites rooted in African myth, folklore or history, leave them below so we can keep the list growing 🩷
