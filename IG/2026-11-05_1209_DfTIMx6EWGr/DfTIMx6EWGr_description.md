## What it teaches

1. Recognize that a playful 'you're out of my league' tease is often a test of how comfortable you are with yourself.
2. Don't act insulted or treat the joke as an attack.
3. Don't justify your value or list credentials like career, money, travel, hobbies, or fitness, since that accepts her frame and turns the chat into a job interview.
4. Respond with relaxed confidence, such as taking the joke lightly and saying you like her confidence.
5. Enjoy the banter without needing her approval, which is what creates playful chemistry and attraction.
