# Using boredom to beat procrastination

A man talks to the camera about a productivity trick: sitting with nothing to stimulate you until boredom pushes you to start the task you've been avoiding. The caption asks viewers for their thoughts.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Put your phone down, stare at the ceiling, and do nothing for as long as you can.
2. Avoid all stimulation during this time: no phone, no TV, no music.
3. Once you get bored, do the task you have been procrastinating on.
4. Resisting the urge to constantly stimulate your brain lets boredom reset your focus.
