# Damien Echols on focus and ceremonial magic

A clip from The Midnight Gospel, hosted by Duncan Trussell, in which Damien Echols talks about meditation, focus, and how Eastern traditions compare with ceremonial magic. The caption introduces him as a ceremonial magician and says the poster disagrees with some of his work.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Meditation matters in magic because energy flows in the direction your attention is fixed.
2. When doing magic, keep the mind single-pointedly focused on the goal.
3. Meditation trains the mind so it does not scatter and disperse the energy you are building.

## Caption

Damien Echols on The Midnight Gospel hosted by Duncan Trussell.
If you don’t know Damien’s story- look it up. It’s wild. Although I disagree with some of his work, namely Abrahamic Ceremonial Magick, and I despise the use of the LBRP, he is a great teacher and I respect him.
