# Five LLM prompts for job search and interviews

A six-slide carousel that presents five chat-assistant prompts for job applications: resume tailoring, cover letters, LinkedIn profile rewrites, networking posts, and interview preparation. Each slide shows the prompt text with placeholders and a sample output.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Resume tailoring: paste your current resume and the job description, then ask for exact JD keywords, quantified achievements, a one-page bullet format, and the top 3-5 matches in a summary. Request the full revised resume plus a list of changes.
2. Cover letter: paste the job description, 3-5 key experiences, and your resume summary. Ask for three paragraphs: a hook about the company, a fit section backed by quantified stories, and a strong interview call to action, in a confident tone with no fluff.
3. LinkedIn profile: paste your current About section, headline, experience, and target role. Ask for a first-person, keyword-rich headline and About section ending with a subtle call to action, plus three banner ideas.
4. Networking post: give one achievement and your target audience. Ask for a hook in the first line, a short story with lessons, a closing question to drive comments, and 3-5 hashtags.
5. Interview prep: paste the job description and company and role. Ask for 15 likely questions (5 technical, 5 behavioral, 5 company/role-specific), and for each one, why it is asked and a suggested answer framework.
