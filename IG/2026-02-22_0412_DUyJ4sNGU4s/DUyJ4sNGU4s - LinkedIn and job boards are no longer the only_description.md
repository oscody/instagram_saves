# Five LLM prompts for job searching

A carousel that presents five ChatGPT-style prompt templates for job-search tasks: resume tailoring, cover letters, LinkedIn profile rewrites, a networking post, and interview preparation. Each slide shows the prompt text and a sample output.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Resume tailoring: paste your current resume and the job description, then ask the LLM to work in exact keywords and phrases from the JD, quantify achievements, keep it under one page in bullet format, and summarize your top 3-5 matches. Ask for the full revised resume plus a list of changes.
2. Cover letter: give the LLM the job description, 3-5 key experiences, and your resume summary. Ask for three paragraphs: a hook with company-specific excitement, fit proven with quantified stories, and a strong call to action for an interview. Keep the tone confident, professional, and enthusiastic, with no fluff.
3. LinkedIn profile: paste your current headline and About section, your experience, and your target role. Ask for a rewritten headline and About section that are first-person, engaging, keyword-rich for recruiter searches, and end with a subtle call to action. Also ask for three banner ideas.
4. Networking post: give the LLM an achievement and a target audience. Ask for a post with a hook in the first line, a short story with lessons, a closing question to drive comments, and 3-5 relevant hashtags.
5. Interview prep: give the LLM the job description and company/role. Ask for 15 likely questions, split into 5 technical, 5 behavioral, and 5 company/role-specific. For each question, ask why it is asked and a suggested answer framework.
