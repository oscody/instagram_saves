# Street fight clip with defensive tips

A night-time street scuffle filmed with a crowd watching, showing a fighter charging in with hands down and a knee strike and grappling near a sidewalk. The caption is a joke about the fight ("Bro did not have to do that") with MMA, boxing and UFC hashtags.

## What it teaches

1. Use the step back check hook against someone who runs at you with their hands down, since it redirects them away from you.
2. Don't run at people with your head down, because you can get kneed in the head.
3. Treat the knee as too dangerous to use.
4. If you end up on the ground, don't get up with your hands back; if you have to, just back row.
