# Due diligence checks before buying a business

A carousel and caption outlining three areas to review before paying for a business: legal, accounting, and general operations. It ends with a prompt to comment for a follow-up checklist.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Legal: have a business attorney review contracts, leases, licenses, pending lawsuits, and who actually owns the assets.
2. Legal: run lien searches and check bulk sales filings, articles of incorporation, and vendor contracts.
3. Accounting: compare tax returns against the books.
4. Accounting: check add-backs and work out the real owner cash flow, not the figures in the pitch deck.
5. General/operations: look at the owner and company background, customer relationships and concentration, competition, key staff, management and scheduling, and systems.
6. General/operations: find out the real reason the seller is selling.
7. Check all three areas, since checking only one leaves a buyer exposed.
