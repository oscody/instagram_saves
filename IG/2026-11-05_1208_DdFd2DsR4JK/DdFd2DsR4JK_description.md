# First date conversation topics framework

A handwritten notebook page, narrated by the poster, lays out a framework for first date conversation: the goal, how to keep it going, which topics to use, and why delivery matters more than content. The caption makes the same point that delivery matters more than what you say.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Goal of a first date: get to know each other, and treat it as a conversation, not an interview.
2. Keep the conversation fun, interesting, and light or deep, as needed.
3. Topics to draw on: travel, interests, hobbies, passions, and work.
4. Focus on delivery: what you talk about matters less than how you say it.

## Caption

You can honestly talk about anything on a first date, but the key is the delivery. It’s not about what you say, but how you say it.
