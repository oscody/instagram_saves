### Key steps

1. Find recently funded startups: on Crunchbase, filter Funding Rounds to the past 30 days, then by location and industry. TechCrunch's Startups and Funding sections work too.
2. Browse the YC job board and filter by role, location and experience level. Apply directly or email the founders.
3. Search '[your university] startup incubator or accelerator'. Look for student-founded companies, and ask professors, career offices and upperclassmen.
4. On LinkedIn, search for your role and filter company size to 1–10 or 11–50. Check the employees tab for past interns.
5. Skip LinkedIn DMs. Use Apollo to find the contact's email and send a cold email instead. Aim for 1–2 messages a day.
6. Cold email: use a short subject that includes your school or location. Say what you like about their work, give 3 facts about yourself (including one result with a metric), ask how you can intern, and attach a resume if the company is small.
