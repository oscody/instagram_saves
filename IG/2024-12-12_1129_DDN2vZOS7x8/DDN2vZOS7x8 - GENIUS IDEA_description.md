# Argentine fire pit sold in US: validation story

The speaker tells how a US founder brought the Argentine Fuego fire pit to America after seeing it sold only in Argentina. He tested demand with a basic website before committing to production.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Look for a product that sells well in one country but is not sold in another, here Argentina versus the US.
2. Compare market size and cultural fit: the US has about eight times Argentina's population and a similar meat-grilling culture.
3. Launch a rough website quickly and share it with friends and family.
4. Commission prototypes from a welder once early interest appears.
5. Start taking orders before the product is fully built.
6. Act without waiting for permission, and be willing to make big bets on yourself.
