# Using ChatGPT prompts to find jobs and grants

A carousel suggesting ChatGPT can help find jobs, scholarships, and grants and prepare applications, using seven example prompts. It stresses that AI cannot guarantee results and that eligibility and deadlines must be checked on official sources.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Upload your resume or CV to ChatGPT before using the prompts.
2. Find matching jobs: ask for 15 roles you are qualified for, with required skills, your matching strengths, and the gaps to fix.
3. Find scholarships: base the search on your CV, education, location, field, and achievements; prioritize no-application-fee options and explain eligibility criteria.
4. Find grants: ask for grants, fellowships, or funding programs relevant to your background, including eligibility, deadlines, funding amount, and the official application source.
5. Find hidden opportunities: ask for job boards, scholarship databases, grant directories, fellowships, and organizations where someone with your background could find less commonly advertised openings.
6. ATS CV match: paste a job description and ask the AI to act as an ATS recruiter, identify missing keywords and skills, and show how to improve the CV without adding anything untrue.
7. Personalized application plan: ask for a 30-day strategy listing which jobs, scholarships, grants, and fellowships to target each week and how many applications to submit.
8. Opportunity scout: ask the AI to search the web for currently open jobs, scholarships, grants, and fellowships matching your CV, include only those whose deadlines have not passed, and give the official application link for each.
9. Verify eligibility, deadlines, requirements, and application details through the official source before applying, since AI cannot guarantee a job, scholarship, or grant.
