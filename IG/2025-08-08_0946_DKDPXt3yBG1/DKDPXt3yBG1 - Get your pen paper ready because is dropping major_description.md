# AI for finding grants with Perplexity

A reel from the Access Granted episode in which Alicia Lyttle explains how to use AI to search for grants and start a proposal. The on-screen title says ChatGPT, but in the speech she names Perplexity for the grant search.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Use Perplexity's deep research feature, which she says is free, to search for grants.
2. Tell the tool exactly what you are looking for, such as that you are a woman business owner.
3. Ask it to list available grants, their deadlines, and the page where you can apply.
4. Pick the grant you are most interested in and give the AI information about your business and yourself.
5. Ask the AI what other information it needs from you to write a proposal for that grant.

### Key steps

1. Open Perplexity and choose Deep Research mode.
2. Say who you are and what you want, for example: woman-owned business looking for grants.
3. Ask it to list available grants with deadlines and the application page link.
4. Open each application page and check the deadline and eligibility yourself, because AI results can be wrong or outdated.
5. Pick one grant and give the AI details about your business and yourself.
6. Ask what other information it needs from you to write the proposal, then have it draft the proposal.
