### Resources

* [Himalayas (himalayas.app)](https://himalayas.app): product, found by search, verified, high confidence. Opened it: a remote job board with AI matching, resume, cover letter and interview tools. The free plan is limited and Plus costs $9.
* [RemoteJobs.io (remotejobs.io)](https://remotejobs.io): product, found by search, verified, high confidence. Opened it: a curated remote job board with categories, filters and alerts.
* [Global Work AI (profile) (pulse2.com)](https://pulse2.com/global-work-ai-profile-alex-chepovoi-interview/amp/): page, found by search, not opened, medium confidence. An interview profile of an AI remote-job aggregator with auto-apply. I did not open the site itself or find its exact URL.
* [AIApply (aiagentstore.ai)](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent/aiapply): page, found by search, not opened, medium confidence. A directory listing for AIApply, an AI auto-apply and resume tool. I did not open the official site or find its exact URL.

### Key steps

1. Himalayas: create a free profile, then use the AI matching, the filters (timezone, salary) and the application tracker.
2. RemoteJobs.io: browse by category, filter for fully remote, and set up alerts.
3. AIApply and Global Work AI: they offer auto-apply plus resume and cover letter tools, and AIApply is mostly paid. Check each site's current pricing before you sign up.
