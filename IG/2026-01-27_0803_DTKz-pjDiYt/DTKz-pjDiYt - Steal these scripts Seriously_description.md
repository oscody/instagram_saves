# Three LinkedIn voice note outreach scripts

A creator shares how she landed a six-figure job offer after a one-year career break by sending LinkedIn voice notes instead of typed messages. She walks through three outreach scripts she used.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Connection hook: find one relatable factor to anchor on, such as the same industry, school alumni, or city, and use it in your opening. The more specific the factor, the better.
2. Honesty script: explain something you are struggling with and why you are sending a voice note.
3. Pattern interrupt: skip a generic opener like "Hi Sarah, I hope you're doing well" and start with an unexpected hook, such as saying you sent a voice note because typing the message felt awkward.

### Key steps

1. Find one specific thing you share with the person (industry, school, city) and open with it.
2. Be honest: say what you're struggling with and why you're sending a voice note.
3. Skip generic openers like 'Hope you're doing well'. Start with an unexpected hook, e.g. typing this felt awkward so I'm sending a voice note.
4. Aim for a coffee chat first, then ask for a referral.
