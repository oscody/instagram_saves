## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. The game is real, but it's called Zairoo, not "Zyru" (the transcript misheard it). It's a Pan-African steampunk tabletop RPG, not a video game. Its classes are called "creeds". The June 24 release date comes from a TikTok post by the same creator and has no year. I couldn't confirm it on the site.
* Checked: 2026-10-22 by claude-sonnet-5-5

### Resources

* [Zairoo official site (zairoo.com)](https://zairoo.com/): product, found by search, verified, high confidence. Pan-African steampunk fantasy TTRPG by Molombo, listing creeds such as warriors, beast-mages and rogues (Usaliti), which match the post's warriors, mages and rogues.
* [Zairoo Kickstarter (kickstarter.com)](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/molombo/zairoo): product, found by search, not opened, high confidence. Campaign by Molombo Thillia Thillot III, whose TikTok handle is hey_molombo, the same as the post author's handle. I did not open this page.

### Key steps

1. Go to zairoo.com/shop and buy the Alpha PDF, which has the full core game.
2. Or back the Kickstarter to pre-order the physical book.
3. Pick a creed (Silaha, Nkotobek, Qori Ismari, Usaliti and others), a kin and abilities. The FAQ says you can mix them.
4. Join the Discord (discord.gg/zairoo) to find sessions and get updates.
