# Niche tech challenges for nerdy friends

A short video describing builder challenges to run with tech-minded friends, each more niche than the last. It shows four of them with on-screen labels.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Blind handoff game jam: everyone starts a game in their chosen genre. Halfway through the week, swap unfinished games with someone in the group, with no notes and no explanation. Everyone finishes the half-built game they received. The group playtests and votes for the best game.
2. Sip it and ship it: everyone picks their favorite beverage and has two weeks to build a website or web app whose only job is to hype that drink. The first person to get noticed online by the actual brand wins.
3. Visualizer challenge: everyone learns to build an interactive visualizer that reacts to something other than audio, using only a camera feed. Whoever has the most creative result wins.
4. Solve it in AR: the group picks the most annoying everyday problem. Each person builds an augmented reality prototype to solve it, and the first person to successfully prototype something useful wins.
