### Key steps

1. Log in to NYC PASSPort and open the RFx tab, then Browse Public RFx.
2. Search EPIN 05626P0001 and open the procurement. The documents are also in the PASSPort Public Portal's Procurement Navigator.
3. Check the current due date and any addenda. The notice said Aug 19, 2026, 4 PM, and the post says Sept 30.
4. Respond through PASSPort. For help, use the MOCS support portal (mocssupport.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/8).
