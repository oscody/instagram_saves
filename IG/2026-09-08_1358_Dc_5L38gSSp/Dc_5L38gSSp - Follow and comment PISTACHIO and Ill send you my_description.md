## What it teaches

1. Context: tell the AI about your situation so its advice is relevant, for example by typing "Ask me 5 clarifying questions."
2. Code words: use simple words like "ELI10" or "REDTEAM" to change how the AI talks to you.
3. Projects: group your chats by similar topic so the AI keeps the purpose of each chat and you stop repeating yourself.
4. Skills: when you do the same thing multiple times, turn it into a Skill to automate the repetitive task, and improve the Skill each time you use it.
