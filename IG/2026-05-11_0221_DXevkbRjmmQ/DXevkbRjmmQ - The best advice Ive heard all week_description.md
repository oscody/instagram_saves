# Gratitude, anxiety and living in the present

Two men sit in a gym cage while one shares a quote about gratitude, peace of mind, and not worrying about the past or future. The post is a short clip of spoken life advice, with no link or offer.

## What it teaches

1. Fill your cup with gratitude, since a full heart leaves no room for anxiety.
2. Be at ease knowing that what is meant for you will not miss you, and what misses you was never meant for you.
3. Don't live in the future, because that means living with anxiety.
4. Don't live in the past or keep dwelling on the glory days.
5. Live in the present and deal with whatever happens.
6. Be decisive instead of sitting around and wasting time.
