## What it teaches

1. Pick a real subject instead of vlogging everyday life with nothing to show, such as walking around the house or going to the deli.
2. Expect to fail many times in a row, and watch for progress between the failures.
3. Be obsessed with your work, to the point that missing a day of it makes you feel bad.
4. Work like you already getting paid, stay consistent, and never doubt yourself.
