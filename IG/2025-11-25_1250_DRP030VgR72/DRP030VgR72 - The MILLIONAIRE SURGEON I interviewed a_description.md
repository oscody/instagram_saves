# Millionaire surgeon street interview on wealth and faith

A street interview with a New York surgeon about how he built his wealth, how he moved from employed doctor to running his own practice, and how he came to his faith. On-screen text is a few short phrases such as "in the last year..." and "Your dream has to be so big."

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Quit employment and start your own practice, which the surgeon says is how he went from employee doctor to a multimillion-dollar business.
2. Pour your heart into something you absolutely love.
3. Have faith in the unknown when you take the step away from a secure job.
4. Make your dream big enough to give God the opportunity to show how powerful he is.
5. Test God personally, as in his 'taste the apple' story, to know for yourself that he exists.
