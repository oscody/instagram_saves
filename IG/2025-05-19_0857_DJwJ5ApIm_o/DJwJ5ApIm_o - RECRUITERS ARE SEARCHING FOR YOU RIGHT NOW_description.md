# ChatGPT Prompts for Recruiter-Friendly LinkedIn Profiles

A seven-slide carousel showing five fill-in-the-blank ChatGPT prompts for updating a LinkedIn profile so recruiters can find it. The last slide promotes a "700+ AI toolkit" that is sent by comment.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Headline: ask ChatGPT for a LinkedIn headline using your role, key skills and industry as keywords, kept short, scroll-stopping and recruiter-friendly.
2. About section: ask for a first-person summary that tells your career story, highlights wins and positions you for a target job or industry, kept clear, human and under 250 words.
3. Experience bullets: paste your old bullets and ask for a rewrite with action verbs, results and keywords aligned with the target job.
4. Skills: paste a job description and ask for 10 skills to add to your LinkedIn profile to boost visibility for that role.
5. Recruiter connection request: ask for a short, casual, friendly and direct message to connect with a recruiter hiring for a specific role at a specific company.
