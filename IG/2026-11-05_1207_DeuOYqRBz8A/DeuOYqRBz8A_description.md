# Swap small talk for deeper conversation questions

A man in a red sweater uses a whiteboard to contrast small-talk questions ("How r u?", "Game?", "Weekend?", "Evryt. good?") with open questions that arrive at the same topics as "Interesting," "Fun?," "Excited?," and "Hard?" The caption says the questions are meant to help you truly understand other people.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Replace "How are you?" with "What's an interesting thing that happened to you today?"
2. Replace "Did you catch the game last night?" with "What's a fun thing you've done in the past few days?"
3. Replace "How was your weekend?" with "What are you excited for this week?"
4. Replace "Is everything good?" with "What's something that's been hard for you to talk about lately?"
5. Stop asking small questions and use intentional ones that get at what someone is actually going through day to day.
