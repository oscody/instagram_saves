# 7 Essential AI Prompts You Can't Miss

A carousel of AI prompts, each framed as "Write as a [role]" and shown in full on slides 2 to 6. The title promises seven, but only five prompts (numbered 1 to 5) appear in the slides.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Focus Rebuilder: ask the AI to act as a productivity architect and write a 3-step focus framework. Diagnose the root cause of attention loss (mental overload, constant notifications, emotional fatigue), create one grounding technique that resets focus in under 60 seconds, and end with one sustainable focus rule using environment design, daily rhythms, or pre-task rituals.
2. Learning Shortcut: ask the AI to act as a learning strategist and build a daily learning ritual under ten minutes. Include one mindset shift that removes the fear of forgetting or failing, one technique that compresses learning time (summarizing while teaching or spaced recall), and one creative real-world test to apply the material the same day.
3. Clarity Map: ask the AI to act as a life and strategy coach and turn a vague goal into a 3-stage plan. Define success in specific, emotionally honest terms; break it into the next three visible moves that can be tracked; set a clarity checkpoint rule for when to pause, adjust, or stop. Keep it realistic, measurable, and forgiving.
4. Habit Loop: ask the AI to act as a behavioral designer and create a habit system that does not rely on motivation. Define the trigger that starts the habit automatically, design a satisfying reward, and shape the environment so the habit becomes effortless. End with a daily progress template that takes two minutes or less.
5. Idea Factory: ask the AI to act as a creativity engineer and build a repeatable framework that generates ten original ideas from any topic. Gather examples, patterns, or opposing views; mix two unrelated ideas; test each idea for usefulness, novelty, and emotional impact.
