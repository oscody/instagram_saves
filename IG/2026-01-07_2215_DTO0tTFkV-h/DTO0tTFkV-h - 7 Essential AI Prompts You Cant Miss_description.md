## What it teaches

1. 1. Focus Rebuilder: ask the AI to act as a productivity architect and produce a 3-step focus framework. Diagnose the root cause of attention loss (mental overload, constant notifications, emotional fatigue). Create one grounding technique that resets focus in under 60 seconds (breathing pattern, short physical reset, or digital cleanse trigger). End with one sustainable focus rule using environment design, daily rhythms, or pre-task rituals, kept simple enough to apply right away.
2. 2. Learning Shortcut: ask the AI to act as a learning strategist and build a short daily ritual under ten minutes that feels enjoyable. Include one mindset shift that removes the fear of forgetting or failing, one technique that compresses learning time (summarizing while teaching or spaced recall), and one creative real-world test that forces applying what was learned the same day.
3. 3. Clarity Map: ask the AI to act as a life and strategy coach and turn a vague goal into a 3-stage plan. Step one: define what success means in specific, emotionally honest terms. Step two: break it into the next three visible, easily tracked moves. Step three: set a clarity checkpoint rule that signals when to pause, adjust, or stop. Keep it realistic, measurable, and forgiving to avoid burnout.
4. 4. Habit Loop: ask the AI to act as a behavioral designer and create a habit system that does not rely on motivation. Define the trigger that starts the habit automatically, design a satisfying reward, and shape the environment so the habit becomes effortless. End with a simple daily tracking template that takes two minutes or less.
5. 5. Idea Factory: ask the AI to act as a creativity engineer and build a repeatable framework that generates ten original ideas from any topic. Collect examples, patterns, or opposing views on the theme. Mix two unrelated ideas to spark new thinking. Test each idea for usefulness, novelty, and emotional impact.
