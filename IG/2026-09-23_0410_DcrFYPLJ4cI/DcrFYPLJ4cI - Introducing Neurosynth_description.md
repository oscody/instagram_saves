# Introducing Neurosynth: real-time brain-response content

The creator presents "Neurosynth," which he says generates and alters video content in real time based on a predicted viewer brain response. The demo shows a Rick and Morty clip scored for attention drops, then regenerated to hold attention, with access limited to the first 500 people.
