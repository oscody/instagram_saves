# Reactive networking with a video CV on LinkedIn

A job seeker walks through how they apply and reach out to a recruiter on LinkedIn, using a video pitch attached to their CV. The screen recordings show a LinkedIn job listing with the "Meet the hiring team" section, a Reslink "Write with PitchAI" button, and a LinkedIn Easy Apply page.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Find a LinkedIn job listing where you meet at least 70% of the requirements and that shows the "Meet the hiring team" feature.
2. Identify the recruiter listed under "Meet the hiring team".
3. Use Reslink's Pitch AI feature to draft a personalised video pitch for that recruiter, using key points from your CV matched to the job description.
4. Once the video is ready, download it from the Reslink dashboard, which adds a clickable badge to the top of your CV.
5. Apply on LinkedIn as usual with the badged CV.
6. Send the recruiter a short message with a link to your Reslink video so they do not miss it.
