# Inflatable Yacht Resale Business Idea

A short business-idea pitch claiming around $1,600 profit per inflatable yacht sold. It shows footage of inflatable yachts on water and describes them as folding compactly, inflating in about 10 minutes, and measuring 4 to 5 meters long.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Buy inflatable yachts from a Chinese supplier, one unit at a time, since no bulk order is required.
2. Be the first to offer this product in your own city.

## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. Chinese suppliers do sell single inflatable boats, but the post names no supplier, and I found no 4–5 m model that is confirmed to inflate in 10 minutes. The $1,600 profit per unit is unsupported. Listed 4–5 m boats cost about $1,000–3,000 before shipping, duties and safety certification.
