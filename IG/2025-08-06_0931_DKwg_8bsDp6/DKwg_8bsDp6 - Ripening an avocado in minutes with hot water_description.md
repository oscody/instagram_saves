### Key steps

1. Wrap the avocado fully in foil and put it in a heatproof bowl.
2. Cover it with boiling or very hot water and wait about 10 minutes. The post gives no time, and 3–5 minutes failed in one test.
3. Unwrap it, let it cool, then cut it open. The flesh will be softer but may taste less ripe.
4. If you have time, use a paper bag with a banana instead. It takes 1–3 days and gives better flavor.
