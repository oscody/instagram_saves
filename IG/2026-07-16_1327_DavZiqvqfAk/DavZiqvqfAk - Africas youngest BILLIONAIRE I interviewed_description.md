# Nigerian billionaire's advice on scaling and networking

A street interview in Lagos, Nigeria, with businessman Iyinoluwa Aboyeji, who says he built two billion-dollar companies. He answers questions about his wealth, how he scaled, doing business in America versus Nigeria, and what he would tell the younger generation.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. To scale, build systems and work as a team: a billion-dollar company is a group of people who could each have built a 10 million dollar business, combined into one.
2. Make money for your friends: the more you help others make money, the less you have to worry about money.
3. Networking starts with serving people: ask "How can I help?" and give rather than take.
4. In Nigeria, you only make money if you make other people money; in America, you can't make other people money if you don't make money yourself.
5. His final advice to the younger generation is one word: build.
