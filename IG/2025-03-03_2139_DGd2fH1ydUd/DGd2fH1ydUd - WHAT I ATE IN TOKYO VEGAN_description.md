## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. The post is a personal list of Tokyo vegan restaurants. I confirmed Izakaya Masaka by opening a page about it, and Ain Soph Ginza only from search results. Marugoto Vegan, Universal Bakes, Jikasei Menso and Pizza Marumo were not confirmed.
* Checked: 2026-11-10 by claude-sonnet-5-5

### Resources

* [Vegan Izakaya Masaka (Shibuya Parco B1, Chaos Kitchen) (eightyflavors.com)](https://eightyflavors.com/vegan-izakaya-masaka-tokyo/): page, found by search, verified, high confidence. Opened it. It places Masaka in the Shibuya Parco basement food hall and names the karaage as the signature dish, as the post does.
* [Ain Soph Ginza (HappyCow listing) (happycow.net)](https://happycow.net/reviews/ain-soph-ginza-tokyo-23681?page=3): page, found by search, not opened, medium confidence. Seen only in search results. They describe an all-vegan Ginza restaurant, which fits the post's dessert stop.

### Key steps

1. Izakaya Masaka: Shibuya Parco basement, Chaos Kitchen food hall. HappyCow reviewers say it takes no reservations, is often full, and serves only lunch sets from 12 to 3pm. Order the Szechuan karaage.
2. Jikasei Menso (tantanmen ramen): the post says it is in the same food hall as Masaka. I couldn't confirm this.
3. Ain Soph Ginza: search results give the address as 4-12-1 Ginza and recommend reservations for dinner. The post went in the afternoon for desserts.
4. Marugoto Vegan (Asakusa curry), Universal Bakes & Cafe (Daita) and Pizza Marumo (Shibuya): check current Google Maps or HappyCow listings before going.

### Not found

I found no web pages confirming Pizza Marumo or Jikasei Menso. Marugoto Vegan Dining Asakusa appeared only in a list with no details. Universal Bakes in Daita was not confirmed directly. I did not check opening hours or whether any place is still open.
