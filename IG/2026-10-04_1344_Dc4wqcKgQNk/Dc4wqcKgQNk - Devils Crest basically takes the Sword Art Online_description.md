# Devils' Crest vs. Sword Art Online anime take

A creator reacts to the anime Devils' Crest, saying it flips the Sword Art Online setup: the characters log out of a full-dive VR game, but the game follows them into the real world. The clip shows a man in a headset reacting over anime footage with on-screen captions about the game merging with reality.
