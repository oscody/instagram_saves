# Stew Peas becomes first Jamaican film distributed by Caribbean Cinemas

A news graphic announcing that the Jamaican psychological thriller Stew Peas will get a simultaneous theatrical release across seven Caribbean territories on October 29, with six Caribbean Film Awards nominations and a limited US release planned for January 2027. The image shows the film's poster with the headline "Stew Peas makes history as first Jamaican film distributed by Caribbean Cinemas."

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## Caption

afrocentricfilmscollaborative Jamaican psychological thriller Stew Peas is set to make history as the first Jamaican film to be distributed by Caribbean Cinemas, with a simultaneous theatrical release across seven Caribbean territories on October 29! 🎉

“Written, directed and produced by Sosiessia Nixon-Kelly, the film will be screened in Trinidad and Tobago, Barbados, Antigua, Guyana, St Lucia, St Kitts and the US Virgin Islands.

The milestone comes as the film celebrates six nominations at the inaugural Caribbean Film Awards, including individual recognition for Nixon-Kelly in the Best Director category and Kerry-Ann 'Chiney K' Collins for Best Actress.

The regional expansion follows the film's successful run in Jamaican cinemas and paves the way for a limited US theatrical release in January 2027.”
