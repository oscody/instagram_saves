# Texting lines to replace "how was your day"

A five-slide carousel of text-message openers aimed at a woman, with a woman in a cap and red top, a woman at a gym mirror, and a woman in a group setting as the images. It lists alternatives to "how was your day" and ends with a note on tone.

## What it teaches

1. Swap boring questions like "How was your day?" for a flipped question: "Rate your day out of 10 and what would've made it a 10?"
2. Be unexpected: text "What's something random that happened today that made you laugh?" to break the pattern of small talk
3. Build tension: ask "What were you thinking about before I texted you?"
4. Master the vibe: it's how you say it, not just what you say. Combine confidence and curiosity to get connection
