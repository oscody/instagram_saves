🚨You can now create your own TV show with AI.

Amazon has backed Showrunner, a new streaming platform that turns written prompts into full animated episodes. You describe a scene, upload a photo, and the AI creates a cartoon with you as a character.

Their debut series, Exit Valley, parodies startup culture with surprisingly realistic characters. No studio, actors, or crew needed, just your imagination.

Think of it as ChatGPT meets Netflix, where viewers become creators. The platform is free for now, with paid features and creator monetization planned. It’s already gaining traction with viral AI-generated South Park clips and is scaling rapidly.

The technology essentially democratizes TV production, letting anyone with an idea become a showrunner from their laptop.
