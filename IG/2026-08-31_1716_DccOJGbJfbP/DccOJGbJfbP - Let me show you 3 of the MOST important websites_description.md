# 3 Essential Websites for 3D Artists

A 3D artist walks through three websites for 3D work: noclip.website for exploring video game levels in the browser, thebasemesh.com for free CC0 3D models, and omglord.com for a curated directory of creative resources.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. noclip.website: open a game level in the browser and fly through it to see how it is built. Controls default to Unreal-style, and you can change them in the settings.
2. noclip.website: use fullscreen to catch motion, and use the pause button to inspect a frame closely.
3. noclip.website: study asset topology, animation states, individual texture maps, and how NPCs react to the camera in a level.
4. thebasemesh.com: browse the library of free models and click one to open a 3D viewer where you can rotate it and see its details.
5. thebasemesh.com: check that models have clean topology, UV unwrapping, and real-world scale before using them.
6. thebasemesh.com: models are under CC0, so you can edit, combine, kitbash, and use them in projects. Crediting the site is optional, but the author asks that models not be put behind a paywall in a significant way.
7. omglord.com: scroll the directory on the right side by topic, such as Figma plugins, typography, and Chrome extensions, and read the blurb under each link for what it is and why it is useful.
