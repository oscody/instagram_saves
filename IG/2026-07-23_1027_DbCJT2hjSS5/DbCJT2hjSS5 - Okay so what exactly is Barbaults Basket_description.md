## Caption

Okay, so what exactly is Barbault’s Basket? 😂

In July 2026, Jupiter in Leo, Uranus in Gemini, Neptune in Aries and Pluto in Aquarius are connecting through a rare mix of supportive angles and one powerful opposition. When astrologers map those connections on the zodiac wheel, the pattern resembles a basket or cradle.

It was named after French astrologer André Barbault, who studied how slow-moving planetary cycles often line up with major turning points in society. Because these planets move slowly, astrologers view this as the beginning of a longer chapter, not one magical day where everybody suddenly gets rich and meets their soulmate 😂

For Scorpio Sun and rising, this shift puts pressure on home and family patterns, daily habits, shared money, trust, intimacy and career.

Some things may finally start making sense. Other things may become impossible to ignore. This energy isn’t coming to fix everything for us. It’s showing us what needs to change so we can stop building a new life on top of old habits.
