# 20 solo side quests for 2026

A man eats outdoors in a backyard while on-screen text reads "20 side quests to do in 2026 (ALONE)". The caption lists 20 solo activities for the year, from a solo breakfast date and a phone-free day to volunteering anonymously and planning your ideal life.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## Caption

Here are 20 solo side quests for 2026:
	1.	Take yourself on a solo breakfast date at a place you’ve never been.
	2.	Spend one full day without your phone.
	3.	Learn 10 constellations and find them in the night sky.
	4.	Write a letter to your future self and seal it until 2027.
	5.	Try a hobby for 30 days straight (no quitting early).
	6.	Wake up at sunrise and watch it fully without distractions.
	7.	Visit a museum alone and move slowly through it.
	8.	Memorize one powerful poem or passage.
	9.	Take a different route home for an entire month.
	10.	Read a book outside your usual taste.
	11.	Have a full “silent day” (only speak when necessary).
	12.	Start a small balcony or windowsill garden.
	13.	Train for and complete a physical challenge (5K, hike, long bike ride).
	14.	Declutter 100 items from your space.
	15.	Cook a full 3-course meal just for yourself.
	16.	Volunteer anonymously once.
	17.	Learn basic self-defense.
	18.	Go somewhere you’ve never been in your own city.
	19.	Keep a daily handwritten journal for 60 days.
	20.	Spend one evening planning your “ideal life” in detail — no limits.
