## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. Future Caribbean is a real 21-day agentic AI buildathon with 10 tracks, run by Lily Dash. The "first billion-dollar tech company" line is the post's aspiration, not a result. Applications closed in July 2026.
* Checked: 2026-10-22 by claude-sonnet-5-5

### Key steps

1. Applications closed July 3, 2026 and the cohort was announced July 17, so you can't apply to this edition.
2. The builder sprint ran July 17 to August 7. Winners were due September 1, followed by an investor showcase and a pitch day at the NYSE.
3. Prizes: a US$50k cash pool plus US$20k in AI machines. Teams also got compute and mentorship.
4. Applications went through Future Caribbean's website. No URL appeared in the sources I opened, so check @future.caribbean for the link and any later editions.
