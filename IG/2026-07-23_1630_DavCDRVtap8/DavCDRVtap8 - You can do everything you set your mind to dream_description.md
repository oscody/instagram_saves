# Visualizing a high self-vision and believing it

A motivational reel with on-screen captions over street, crowd and interior footage. The narration asks the viewer to picture the highest version of themselves and says there is no wrong answer.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Picture how high you can see yourself and decide who you see yourself as; there is no right or wrong answer.
2. Clarify and define that vision in the highest way you can imagine, going past what your mind believes is possible, even a little further.
3. Believe in that vision (number two).

## Caption

You can do everything you set your mind to, dream big and do the work, that‘s what you were brought on this earth to do.
