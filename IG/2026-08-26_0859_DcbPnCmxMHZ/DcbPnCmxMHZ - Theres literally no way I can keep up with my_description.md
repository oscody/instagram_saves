# LinkedIn connections to targeted outreach list with AI

A creator shows how she exports her LinkedIn connections and has an AI model sort them into a targeted outreach list for her goals. The on-screen prompt and a sample results table show the scoring, categories and suggested openers.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. In LinkedIn, open the profile menu, go to Settings & Privacy, then Data privacy, then Download your data.
2. Select the first option and request an archive of your data, then wait for the email or download to become ready.
3. Upload the connections file to an AI tool and paste a prompt that states your exact goal and target audience.
4. Have the AI scan every row of the file without sampling or skipping any.
5. Score each connection from 1 to 5 on how well they match the target audience, using job title, company and other signals in the data.
6. Flag the top matches (scores 4 or 5) and explain in one sentence why each fits your specific goal.
7. Group the flagged matches into 2 to 4 categories that fit the goal, such as warm intro path, direct outreach or potential referral source.
8. For each top match, write one specific opening line that references something concrete from their title or company, not a generic greeting.
9. Note that the AI only sees the CSV, so for high-priority matches open their full profile to find a more specific detail to reference.
10. Flag gaps where part of the target audience has few or no matches, so you know which types of people to reach out to more.
11. Review the results table with columns for name, company, title, score, category, why they fit and suggested opener.
