# Five Claude prompts to optimise your LinkedIn

A carousel presenting five prompts for Claude that rewrite a LinkedIn headline, About section, referral messages, skills keywords and experience entries. The cover claims the author got recruiter messages within a week of using them.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Paste your LinkedIn profile data and resume into Claude, and name your target role.
2. Headline: ask for a rewrite under 120 characters that says what you do, who you help and what sets you apart, avoiding words like passionate, motivated or driven, and ranked by searchability and clickability.
3. About section: ask for a first-person rewrite under 2000 characters with a strong opening line, what you do, the problems you solve, skills relevant to the target role, what you are looking for next, a human tone, and a closing line on how recruiters can reach you.
4. Cold referral message: give Claude the target company, role and resume, and ask for three LinkedIn messages under 300 characters each: polite and professional, warm and conversational, and one that opens with a specific compliment about the person's work, none sounding desperate or generic.
5. Skills and keywords: paste five job descriptions for the target role, have Claude extract repeated skills and phrases, compare them with your profile, list keywords you are missing and keywords that are buried, and get an exact order for your top 15 skills.
6. Experience section: ask for each entry to be rewritten in the form 'Did X, using Y, which resulted in Z', with numbers added (suggesting realistic ones where none are given), each bullet readable in under two lines and free of generic description.
