# Ten questions to truly know someone

Three frames show friends chatting at an outdoor pool party under the text "Want to truly know someone? Ask them these questions (see caption)." The caption lists ten get-to-know-you questions and ends with a note to listen and ask curious follow-ups.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Ask the person one of these questions: (1) If you could invite anyone to dinner, who would it be? (2) What has been the highlight of your life? (3) What trait in others do you admire most? (4) If you wrote a book, what would it be about? (5) If you could write a letter to your younger self, what advice would you include? (6) What makes you feel pure joy? (7) If you had more than enough money, what kind of life would you live? (8) What would your dream day look like? (9) If you could master any skill overnight, what would it be? (10) What's the most challenging decision you've ever made?
2. Listen to understand, not just to reply.
3. When the person stops talking, ask curious follow-up questions.
