1. Go to movinbed.com and pick the city and date.
2. Buy tickets. They are emailed and you show them on your phone at check-in.
3. Arrive when gates open (6pm at Barangaroo). Beds are not assigned, so earlier arrival gives a better choice of spot.
4. Do not bring outside food or drink. Bags are checked, and food and drinks are ordered on site.
5. Bring warm clothes. Weather cancellations are announced by email and SMS by 4pm on the day, with replacement tickets and no refund.
