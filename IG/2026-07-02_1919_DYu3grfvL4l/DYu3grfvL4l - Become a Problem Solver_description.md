# Become a Problem Solver

A woman in earbuds, filmed indoors, gives a motivational talk urging viewers to treat life's problems as things to solve, citing the book The Obstacle Is the Way. It ends by pointing to a fitness challenge in the bio.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Treat each problem you face as an opportunity to become a better person, not as something beneath you.
2. Solve the problem in front of you instead of lying down in front of it.
3. Apply the same approach to areas such as weight, relationships, money, your job, and past trauma by seeing each as a problem to solve.
4. Don't let fear of judgment or fear of failure stop you from thinking this way.
5. Start critical thinking and work on the problem you're facing now.
