# Seven Claude prompts for ADHD-style focus

A nine-slide carousel presenting seven prompts it calls "ADHD Executive Function Mode" for Claude, with a claim that they can finish a week's work in 4 hours. Each slide gives one named prompt with placeholders like [Task] or [Project].

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Task paralysis: paste the stuck task into the prompt in place of [Task], and ask for it to be broken into steps under 1 minute each, with the first step and where to put your hands.
2. Low stimulation: ask for a 'Dopamine Menu' with 5-minute quick-movement 'Appetizers', 20-minute deep-work 'Entrees', and 10-minute creative 'Sides'.
3. Focus check-ins: ask Claude to act as a virtual body double for 30 minutes, state the work you're doing, and check in every 10 minutes for a status update.
4. Switching tasks: after finishing [Task A], ask for a 3-minute 'Mental Palate Cleanser' routine to transition into [Task B].
5. Boring admin tasks: name the task and a current interest, then ask for a 'Quest' structure where finishing the task unlocks a reward.
6. Time underestimation: state the expected time for [Project] and the usual actual time, then ask Claude to identify 3 hidden sub-tasks to plan a realistic deadline.
7. Mental clutter: dump every worry into the prompt, ask Claude to sort items into 'Now', 'Later', and 'Trash', and write a one-sentence next step for each 'Now' item only.

## Caption

🚨 BREAKING: Claude has a feature called ADHD Executive Function Mode.

You can use it to hack your brain’s dopamine and finish a week’s worth of work in 4 hours.

Here are 7 prompts to access it: 👇

1. The Task Paralysis Shatterer

Prompt: "I am staring at [Task] and can't start. Break this down into 'Ridiculously Small' steps that take less than 1 minute each. Give me the first step and tell me exactly where to put my hands to begin."

2. The Dopamine Menu Architect

Prompt: "I am feeling under-stimulated. Create a 'Dopamine Menu' for me with 5-minute 'Appetizers' (quick movement), 20-minute 'Entrees' (deep work), and 10-minute 'Sides' (creative play) to keep my brain engaged."

3. The "Body Doubling" Simulator

Prompt: "Act as my virtual body double for the next 30 minutes. I will tell you what I’m working on, and I want you to check in every 10 minutes to ask for a status update and keep my focus anchored."

4. The Context-Switching Guide

Prompt: "I just finished [Task A] and need to start [Task B], but my brain is stuck. Design a 3-minute 'Mental Palate Cleanser' routine to help me transition between these two different types of energy."

5. The Interest-Based Filter

Prompt: "I have a boring administrative task: [Task]. Help me gamify this by connecting it to my current hyper-fixation: [Interest]. Create a 'Quest' structure where finishing the task unlocks a reward."

6. The Time-Blindness Auditor

Prompt: "I think [Project] will take 20 minutes, but it usually takes 2 hours. Help me 'Time-Map' this by identifying the 3 hidden sub-tasks I always forget to account for so I can set a realistic deadline."

7. The Executive Function Externalizer

Prompt: "My brain is full of 'Open Loops.' I will dump everything I'm worried about below. Categorize these into 'Now,' 'Later,' and 'Trash,' and then write a 1-sentence 'Actionable Next Step' for only the 'Now' items."

## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. The seven prompts are real and usable in any Claude chat, but Claude has no "ADHD Executive Function Mode." The "week's work in 4 hours" claim is clickbait. I did not search for an official feature.

### Key steps

1. Paste any of the seven prompts into a Claude chat and fill in the [Task], [Interest] or [Project] placeholders.
2. Task Paralysis Shatterer: ask for steps under 1 minute each, then the first step and where to put your hands.
3. Body Doubling: ask Claude to check in every 10 minutes for 30 minutes. This only works if you reply to each check-in.
4. Executive Function Externalizer: dump your worries, ask for Now/Later/Trash, and get a 1-sentence next step for the Now items only.

### Not found

No official "ADHD Executive Function Mode" exists as far as I know. I did not search the web or check Anthropic's docs, because the post is just a list of prompts. The vault had no matching notes, and I did not grep it myself.
