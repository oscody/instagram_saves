# Four Claude Prompts for Career Redesign

A carousel of four Claude prompt templates for career analysis, each written as a Tim Ferriss-style role with a task, steps, rules and expected output. The cover slide frames them as a way to use Claude to rethink your career.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Structure each prompt in five labelled sections: ROLE, TASK, STEPS, RULES, OUTPUT.
2. Discover Your Hidden Edge: give the AI your skills, interests, work experience and personality first. Use the 80/20 rule to find the 20% of skills driving 80% of results. Find rare skill combinations. Explain the market value of each combination. Rank them by earning potential and lifestyle fit. Rules: skill pairings beat single abilities; each edge must link to a real market ready to pay; lifestyle alignment is a filter from the start.
3. Identify Your Leverage Gaps: tell the AI what you do now and how you make money. Apply the DEAL framework (Define, Remove, Automate, Free). Separate tasks only you can handle from tasks that can be delegated or automated. Map where a lean business or scalable income fits your skills. Build an action plan to regain time. Rules: flag busy tasks clearly without sugarcoating; every automation or delegation suggestion must be doable now; the action plan must free at least 10 hours weekly as a base target.
4. See Where Your Career Leads: share your current role, income model and weekly time commitment. Categorize each key activity as freedom-building, skill-building or time-for-money trap. Measure the freedom ratio (how much work compounds versus consumes your time). Apply fear-setting: the worst case of changing versus staying the same. Compare the next 5 years on your current path with the redesigned path. Rules: state directly if the career is a time-for-money trap; freedom-building counts only if it lowers required hours over time; fear-setting must use realistic downsides.
5. Build a Wealth Strategy: share your background, skills and ideal lifestyle. Identify your highest-leverage skills and the lean business models that fit. Map scalable income streams such as productized services, digital products, licensing or equity. Build a 10-year roadmap: years 1-2 launch, years 3-5 automation, years 6-10 ownership. Highlight the single most important move for the next 90 days. Rules: traditional career growth is excluded unless it moves toward freedom; every income stream must scale without matching time input; the 90-day move must be doable with current resources and no permission needed.
