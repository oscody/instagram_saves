## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. Hugging Bay is a real catalog of open AI models at huggingbay.xyz. The "torrent" claim isn't visible on the homepage and rests on press coverage only. "Friendlier Pirate Bay" fits loosely, since the site labels licenses and checks files.
* Checked: 2026-10-22 by claude-sonnet-5-5

### Resources

* [Hugging Bay (huggingbay.xyz)](https://huggingbay.xyz): product, in the post, verified, high confidence. The site calls itself an independent catalog of open AI models with a finder, docs, license labels and verified files, but its homepage doesn't mention torrents.
* [hugging bay: is there a pirate bay for AI models (IT-Connect) (it-connect.tech)](https://www.it-connect.tech/hugging-bay-is-there-a-pirate-bay-for-ai-models/): page, found by search, not opened, medium confidence. Search summary says the site offers magnet links, torrents and SHA-256 hashes, with about 146k artifacts, mostly imported from Hugging Face. I didn't open the article.
* [hugging-bay (PyPI client) (pypi.org)](https://pypi.org/project/hugging-bay/): tool, found by search, not opened, medium confidence. Search result lists it as a client for the huggingbay.xyz API. I didn't open the page.

### Key steps

1. Open huggingbay.xyz/finder, pick your computer and a task, or browse the catalog.
2. Get a chat app (or the desktop app at /desktop) and download one model file.
3. Check the file's license label and its SHA-256 hash against the original Hugging Face repo before loading it.
4. Open the model in the app and send a message. The /docs/start-here guide covers setup.

### Not found

I couldn't confirm that the site offers torrents from its own pages. The homepage doesn't mention them, and that claim comes only from search summaries. I didn't see the X post from the reel, and I didn't check the Licensing or Takedown pages.
