2. Combine them gently, leaving no large lumps, and rest the batter 10–15 minutes. You can make it the night before.

4. Heat a nonstick pan or griddle on medium-low. Pour the batter, then pipe or spoon the swirl on top and swirl it lightly.
5. Cook slowly until bubbles form, then flip gently without pressing down. Wipe the pan between batches and keep finished pancakes warm in a 200°F oven.
