# Four ChatGPT prompts for tailoring a CV

Instead of asking ChatGPT to "align my CV to the job," the post gives four prompts: one for a skills section, one for top ATS keywords, one for a professional summary, and one for a line-by-line CV review.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Skills section: paste the job description into the prompt and ask for a skills section with two subsections, Functional Competencies and Technical Tools, listed briefly, in a list format, separated by vertical bars, with no keyword from the ATS scan missed.
2. Keyword optimisation: paste the job description and ask for the top 15 keywords in order of priority to include in the CV to pass the ATS keyword scan.
3. Spread those keywords through your CV so they are impactful and make sense in context.
4. Professional summary: ask for a 3-line, achievement-oriented summary where each line gives evidence of your success, using examples from your work experience.
5. CV review: ask ChatGPT to act as a career coach and recruitment expert for your industry, then review your CV line by line for impact and clarity, relevance to the target role, ATS optimisation, and grammar, structure and formatting, with suggested wording and examples.
