## What it teaches

1. Make more assumptions about her instead of only asking questions, for example guessing where she's from or what she likes to do, so she can correct or agree with you.
2. Include personal storytelling: when she mentions something, share a relevant story from your own life, such as a trip or something that happened to you, and connect it back to what she said.
3. Playfully disqualify her: tease her in a light way (for example, joking that you're not sure she can join a conversation about her own location) to show you're not easily won over.
