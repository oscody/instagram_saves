# Making a space shooter with MGX AI

A creator shows an AI app builder (MGX) generating a space shooter from plain-language requests, then upgrading it with graphics, scoring, and level-up perks. He also turns a photo of himself into a pixel-art spaceship with Flux Kontext and adds it to the game.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Describe the game you want in plain language to the AI app builder, for example 'make a space shooter game'.
2. Refine the first version with follow-up requests, such as upgrading the graphics, adding a point system, and adding level-up perks.
3. To make a character sprite, open an image tool with Flux Kontext Max, add a photo as the image reference, and use the prompt 'Turn this image into a pixel image'.
4. Upload the resulting pixel image into the app builder and ask it to include that character in the game.
