## Caption

Steal My 48 hour Poconos Itinerary!❄️

📍THE SWIFTWATER
I stayed at the Swiftwater Hotel that opened about 8 months ago and it was amazing. The hotel was kept very clean, the service was great, and the amenities were stacked. We definitely enjoyed the indoor/outdoor heated pool! The hotel is in a 15 minute proximity to most poconos hotspots!

2060 PA-611
Swiftwater, PA 18370

📍CAMELBACK RESORT SNOW TUBING
I’m not a big fun of winter activities but this was definitely fun. When we did get cold, the fire pits were perfect! Prices vary based on time and day!

193 Resort Dr
Tannersville, PA 18372

📍DESAKI
This restaurant was definitely a great experience. The staff were very friendly and the food was prepared in a timely manner. Luckily, it is right next to the Swiftwater hotel! If you stay at the Swiftwater, you have the opportunity to deliver sushi from Desaki right to your hotel room!

2054 Route 611
Swiftwater, PA 18370

📍DOUBLE CUT STEAKHOUSE
I was pleasantly surprised by this steakhouse. The ribeye was cooked to perfection and the food was better than I expected. The service was also a 10/10. I would definitely recommend that spot.

250 Kalahari Drive
Pocono Manor, PA 18349
