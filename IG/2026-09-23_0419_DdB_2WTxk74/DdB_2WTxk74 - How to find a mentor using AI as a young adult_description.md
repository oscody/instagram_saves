## What it teaches

1. Search for nonprofits in your hometown and make a list of about 100.
2. Look at who sits on each nonprofit's board.
3. Find the entrepreneurs on those boards and get their email addresses.
4. Send each one a message introducing yourself as a 17-year-old young entrepreneur who has built two companies that failed and has a new idea.
5. Ask for six minutes of their time for advice, and offer that they can text instead if that's easier.
