# Where to buy an existing online or local business

The reel argues that you can buy an existing business instead of starting one from scratch, and names three marketplaces to search. On-screen it shows Flippa.com and LoopNet browsing pages, including a Flippa listing for a dropship store.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Instead of starting a business from scratch, look for an existing one to buy.
2. For online or e-commerce businesses, search Flippa, which lists blogs, Shopify stores, Amazon stores, and YouTube channels; some are already cash flowing and come with vendors and inventory.
3. For online and e-commerce businesses, also check Acquire.com.
4. For brick-and-mortar businesses, search LoopNet and filter by industry to find a fit.
