# MiniMax Design + H3 Max AI video showcase

A reel showing a 15-second, Blender-style character modeling timelapse generated with MiniMax H3 Max in MiniMax Design, with a chrysanthemum-to-eagle effect at the end. It claims about 30 seconds of generation time and low per-second pricing.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Open MiniMax Design.
2. Select the MiniMax H3 Max model.
3. Enter your prompt. The example on screen is set to Text to Video, 16:9, 768P, 15s, and starts with "Create a 15-second fast-paced Blender 3D character modeling timelapse."
