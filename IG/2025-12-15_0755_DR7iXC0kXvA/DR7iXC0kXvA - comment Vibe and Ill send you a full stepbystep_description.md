# Vibe-coding 101: build a portfolio in 2 hours

A carousel showing the creator's process for building a personal portfolio site with AI tools in under two hours, broken into inspiration, a product requirements doc, building, and shipping. The full guide with prompts is offered by comment.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Collect inspiration: find as many websites as you can on the Framer marketplace or Dribbble that make you stop scrolling, and screenshot about 3 you like (15 minutes max).
2. Note what you like about each screenshot, and keep the screenshots in a swipe file to use as your reference.
3. Write a PRD (product requirement document): give ChatGPT your resume, upload the screenshots, and describe your website, then ask it to make a PRD with a clear structure, features, and design direction (about 15 minutes).
4. Give the PRD to an AI coding tool. The creator used Google Antigravity; Cursor, Claude Code, or any vibe-coding tool also works. The caption names Bolt.new for pasting the PRD in and generating a base.
5. Break larger tasks into smaller ones so both the AI and you can keep up.
6. Build in micro steps: iterate with specific prompts and commit each small change to GitHub.
7. Keep refining until it just feels right (about 60 minutes of building in the caption's timing).
8. Ship: drag your project folder into Netlify Drop and get a live URL (about 12 minutes).
