### Key steps

1. Copy the full job description from LinkedIn or another job site.
2. Open hire.dev, where your resume is already uploaded.
3. Click custom job generation and paste the job description.
4. Click Generate ATS resume and wait about 10 seconds.
5. Review the list of changes, then download the tailored resume as a PDF.
