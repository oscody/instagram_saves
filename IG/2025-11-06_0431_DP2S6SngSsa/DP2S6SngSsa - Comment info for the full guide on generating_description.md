# Getting first paying app users via influencers

A short reel pitching a seven-day plan to get 100 paying users for an app, shown with a revenue chart, a Cursor screen, and a sample invite message. The full distribution process is offered only by comment.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Start with the most validating idea, and find someone who already has an audience.
2. Build a minimum viable product first, for example by prompting Cursor AI with 'build me an app' plus your app idea.
3. Find influencers to promote the app, either through a site like Tapco or by searching your niche for creators.
4. Ask the influencer to post a story to capture attention.
5. Have the influencer set up a ManyChat automation so that when someone clicks yes, they receive an invitation to the app.
6. Repeat this process 10 times to reach 100 paying subscribers.
