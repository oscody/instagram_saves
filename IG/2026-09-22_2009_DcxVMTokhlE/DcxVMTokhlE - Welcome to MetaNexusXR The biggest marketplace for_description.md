# Sharp's haptic half-glove VR controller prototype

A narrator presents Sharp's prototype VR controller, a haptic half-glove where each finger sits in its own sleeve while the thumb still uses the buttons and thumbstick. He describes fingertip actuators that simulate texture, temperature and material density, and notes the device is not yet on the market.
