# Three-prompt Claude chain for making money

A woman in a kitchen walks through a three-prompt sequence to run in one Claude conversation to find a fast income path, a person to learn from, and a 90-day plan. She also shares an optional bonus prompt for procrastination.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Run all the prompts in the same Claude conversation, in order, so each one builds on the previous answer.
2. Prompt 1: "Based on everything you know about me, what's the fastest way for me to hit my income goal? Limit it to one offer and one channel only."
3. Prompt 2: "Who's the single most successful person I should be learning from to make this happen? Give me one name."
4. Prompt 3: "Pretend you are this person. Create a 90-day action plan needing less than $1,000 up front and gets me my first paying customer as soon as possible."
5. Optional bonus if you struggle with procrastination: "Ask me questions one at a time until you find the one thing holding me back. Then reframe it and give me a 2-week action plan to prove this works."
