# NYCHA Clean Energy Academy Cohort 3 recruitment

A recruitment carousel for Cohort 3 of the NYCHA Clean Energy Academy, a free 8-week program with a $1,500 stipend and job placement support for NYCHA and Section 8 residents aged 18+. It lists applications opening August 11, 2025 and due August 29, 2025, cohort dates of September 22 to November 21, 2025, and virtual info sessions on August 20 and 27, 2025; those dates have all passed as of this note.
