# Breaking into tech: AI resumes, networking, bootcamps

A speaker in a green bomber jacket, seated in a studio, shares his views on how to break into tech and network your way in. He covers using ChatGPT to tailor a resume, attending Black tech conferences, asking for referrals, and choosing bootcamps with strong track records.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Paste your resume into ChatGPT and ask it to act as a career coach.
2. Give it the specific job description and ask it to customize the resume to fit that job.
3. Ask the AI for recommendations that emphasize measurable results and metrics in the resume.
4. Go to Black tech conferences to network, since shared background builds rapport quickly.
5. Ask people in tech for referrals.
6. Connect with the referrer on LinkedIn and send your resume ready to submit, so it takes them about 30 seconds.
7. Choose bootcamps with a proven track record of placing graduates, since employers see that as less training needed.
