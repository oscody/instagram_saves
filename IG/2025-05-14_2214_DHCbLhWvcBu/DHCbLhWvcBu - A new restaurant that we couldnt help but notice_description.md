## Caption

A new restaurant that we couldn’t help but notice last year when we walked by their ‘Mango Bay’ sign on a Sunday afternoon stroll in Fort Greene. We told them to hit us up once they opened and they did, so we pulled up! 

We absolutely love what they’ve done with the place 😉🤭 especially the menu,

Let’s be real. We’ve seen plenty of Caribbean fusion spots, but there’s something special here. 

Personally, we couldn’t get enough of the Ras and Pasta with lobster. The Curry Goat Puff Pastry was a treat we just wanted more of, and the king prawns? They’re huge! Like nothing we’ve seen before! And don’t even get us started on the glazed ribs they’re technically an appetizer, but trust us, they could easily be entrée with some sides. The octopus and crudo are gems too! Every dish comes with a flavorful twist, blending Afrocentric and Caribbean vibes.

 If you’ve been, what’s your favorite dish or what would you like us to try next? 

And we’ll give it up to Shamah for putting love into all the cocktails.
