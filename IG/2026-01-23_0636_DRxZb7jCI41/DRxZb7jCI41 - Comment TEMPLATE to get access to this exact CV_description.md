# Career switcher CV tips with product manager example

A hiring manager compares a consulting CV with a product manager version of the same CV, pointing out the changes that make a career switch clear to recruiters. The post also shows a Play Intro button linking to a one-minute video pitch.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Put the exact target role directly under your name at the top of the CV.
2. Write the summary around the skills the new role needs instead of listing past job titles.
3. Move the skills and tools section up near the top, with the keywords for the new field.
4. Use the language of the new field, such as roadmap, user feedback loops, product delivery and product-market fit, in place of the old field's terms.
5. Reframe past projects as proof of transferable skills, with specific examples.
6. Write every achievement as an outcome with a number, avoiding words like supported, helped or assisted.
7. Restate old metrics through the lens of the new role, for example time to launch features or user adoption rather than cost reduction alone.
8. Add a one-minute video intro that explains your story, why you are switching and what you bring.
