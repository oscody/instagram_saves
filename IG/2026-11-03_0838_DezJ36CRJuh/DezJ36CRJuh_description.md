# Keeping Claude chats private: memory, privacy, artifacts

The speaker warns that Claude conversations, including bank statements, medical questions and personal rants, can show up in a basic Google search. The on-screen graphics show the Settings menu and the artifacts list with each item's visibility set to Private.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Open Claude, tap your name in the bottom left corner, then click Settings.
2. Go to Memory on the left side and turn off "include sensitive topics."
3. Remove anything in memory you don't want saved.
4. Go to Privacy, find your shared chats, and hit the trash can next to anything you want private.
5. Go to Artifacts, hit Manage, and set everything to Private.

### Key steps

1. Open Claude, click your name at the bottom left, then Settings.
2. Go to Privacy and open Shared chats (Manage). Click the trash can next to each shared link to stop sharing it. This removes the public link but does not delete the chat.
3. Shared artifacts are listed separately. Set them to Private, or remove the share, in the artifacts manager.
4. Pages Google has already indexed or cached may stay visible until they refresh.
5. Optional: in Settings > Memory, review and delete saved memories. I could not confirm the 'include sensitive topics' toggle from a source I opened.
