### Key steps

1. Go to the Massive 101 forum download thread and register for a free account.
2. Agree to the terms of service (non-commercial use only).
3. Download the Windows 10 or Linux build.
4. Install it, then open the included demo and tutorial files and the Massive 9.3 manual.
5. Render with Arnold or Velocity. Renders are watermarked, and USD and geometry export are disabled.
