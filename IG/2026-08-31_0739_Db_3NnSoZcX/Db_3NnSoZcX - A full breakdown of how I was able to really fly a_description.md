# Flying a stunt plane in Las Vegas

A solo traveler breaks down his stunt plane flight in Las Vegas with Sky Combat Ace, from the hotel pickup to the in-air maneuvers. It also covers the package prices and the optional Red Rock Canyon scenic flight.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Sky Combat Ace picks you up on the Strip and takes you to their location.
2. Choose the Afterburner Package (about $1,000) to fly the maneuvers yourself.
3. The pilot behind you demonstrates each maneuver, then you copy it right after.
4. Optionally add a scenic flight over Red Rock Canyon for about $500; they sometimes run a deal on it in person.
5. You can bring your own camera if it is secured, as the poster did with a 360 camera.
6. They also shoot multi-angle video, and the pilot takes their hands off the controls to show you are the one flying.

### Key steps

1. Book the Afterburner (You Fly) on skycombatace.com, or ask about the Red Rock Canyon add-on.
2. Get picked up at a Strip or Downtown hotel (restrictions apply, limited seating).
3. At the hangar, meet your instructor and get suited up with a callsign.
4. Do the pre-flight safety briefing.
5. Fly for 16-20 minutes while the instructor coaches you through the maneuvers.
6. Preview your multi-angle flight video, with an optional 360° view.

### Not found

The Red Rock Canyon add-on price (the post says about $500) and the policy on bringing your own camera. Neither is stated on the pages I checked. The $799 figure comes from a search snippet and I did not open that page.
