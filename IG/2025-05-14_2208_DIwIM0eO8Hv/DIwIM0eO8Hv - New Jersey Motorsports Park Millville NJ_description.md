A promotional reel for New Jersey Motorsports Park in Millville, NJ, listing its outdoor go-karting, axe throwing, dining, group packages, and upcoming 2025 events such as festivals, races, and a monster truck rally. The on-screen text notes karts reaching up to 55 mph and prices from $35.

🏎️ New Jersey Motorsports Park (Millville, NJ)
Thrill seekers, this is your spot! Open to the public for outdoor go-karting and axe throwing (ages 13+).

Go-Kart Fun:
Arrive & Drive: 20 laps in 15 minutes (Fri–Sun)
Rental Kart Leagues: 10-race championships (no experience needed)
Endurance Races: 2-hour team races for cash prizes

Perfect for birthdays, team outings, and parties — add track time, catering, axe throwing, and pool access!

Enjoy chef-crafted meals, cocktails, lawn games like cornhole, horseshoes, and pickleball.

From food truck festivals to racing events and monster truck rallies—there’s always something happening! (Check njmp.com)

Drive your car on a professional track with beginner track days or join exclusive Drivers & Riders Clubs!
