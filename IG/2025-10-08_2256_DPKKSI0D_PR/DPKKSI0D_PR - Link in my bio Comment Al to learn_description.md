# AI voice receptionist books appointments via n8n

A demo of an AI phone receptionist that answers calls, collects a caller's booking details, and books them automatically. The creator says the voice agent is connected to an n8n workflow that writes bookings to Google Calendar.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. The voice agent (VAPI) answers the call and collects the caller's full name, phone number, email, requested service and preferred time.
2. Once the caller has given all details, the data passes through the n8n workflow.
3. The n8n workflow sends the booking to Google Calendar, which is booked automatically.
