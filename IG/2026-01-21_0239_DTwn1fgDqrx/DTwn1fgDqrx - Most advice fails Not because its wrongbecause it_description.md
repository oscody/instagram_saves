# Why most advice fails for you

A clip from The Directors Cut podcast in which the host argues that advice fails because it isn't tailored to the listener's context, timing, costs, and experience. Captions appear over the host's on-camera talk.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Check whether advice fits your own context, timing, and what it costs you before using it.
2. Don't take someone else's advice at 100%; the host suggests taking it at about 50%.
3. Learn from others by understanding the context of their experience, then build your own approach from it.
4. Expect that the same method can produce different outcomes for different people because their circumstances differ.

## Caption

Most advice fails. Not because it’s wrong—because it isn’t yours.
