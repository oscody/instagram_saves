### Key steps

1. Start with SYMSYS 1 (Minds and Machines), the gateway course.
2. Cover math: calculus and linear algebra (e.g. MATH 51 or CME 100).
3. Philosophy: an intro course, then an advanced one.
4. Formal methods: formal logic, theory of computation, probability and statistics.
5. Computation: programming I and II (CS 106A and CS 106B), then one further computational methods course.
6. Cognitive science: PSYCH 1, a cognition/language/neuroscience course, and linguistic theory.
7. Add a cross-area course that combines two of these areas, then finish with a capstone project.
