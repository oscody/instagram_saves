# Ford Framework: Moving Past Small Talk

A creator presents the "Ford Framework" on a whiteboard, pairing four conversation topics (family, occupation, recreation, dreams) with what each tends to lead to. The caption says meaningful conversations are better than small talk.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Ask about the other person's family; they will get sentimental with you.
2. Ask how they feel about their job; they will be real with you.
3. Ask what they do for fun; they will enjoy talking with you.
4. Ask about their dreams; they will want to be friends with you.
