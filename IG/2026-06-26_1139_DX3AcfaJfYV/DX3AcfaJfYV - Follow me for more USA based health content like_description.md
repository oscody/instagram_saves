# Herbal lime, clove and ginger tea recipe

A reel by @mother.nia shows a simmered tea of cloves, lime, cinnamon, ginger and turmeric, which the narration says destroys intestinal worms, reduces bloating, clears cystic acne and boosts energy. The health claims are the post's own and are not verified.

## What it teaches

1. Add whole cloves, chopped limes, a cinnamon stick, a few slices of fresh ginger and a pinch of turmeric to boiling water.
2. Let the mixture simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Strain the tea into a mug before drinking.
