## Caption

50 Deep Questions to Ask on a Date ❤️

The goal isn’t to impress each other—it’s to understand each other.

1. What’s a life experience that changed you forever?
2. What are you most proud of?
3. What have you overcome that most people don’t know about?
4. What does a happy life look like to you?
5. What are you currently working on in yourself?
6. What is your biggest dream?
7. Who has impacted your life the most?
8. What lesson did you learn the hard way?
9. What makes you feel truly loved?
10. What are you afraid of losing?
11. What was your childhood like?
12. What’s your favorite childhood memory?
13. What values are most important to you?
14. What does trust mean to you?
15. What does loyalty look like to you?
16. What’s a relationship dealbreaker for you?
17. How do you handle conflict?
18. What makes you feel safe with someone?
19. What do you wish people understood about you?
20. What makes you feel appreciated?
21. What are you passionate about?
22. What gives your life meaning?
23. What motivates you?
24. Who do you want to become?
25. What are you most grateful for right now?
26. What are you still healing from?
27. What does commitment mean to you?
28. What are your goals for the next five years?
29. What would your ideal weekend look like?
30. What’s on your bucket list?
31. What makes a friendship last?
32. What quality do you admire most in others?
33. What earns your respect instantly?
34. What does success mean to you?
35. How do you want to be remembered?
36. What legacy would you like to leave?
37. What risk are you glad you took?
38. What are you looking for in this stage of life?
39. What makes a relationship healthy?
40. What story best explains who you are today?

Great conversations help people feel seen, heard, and understood. ✨❤️
