# NHT Voluntary Contribution Program for Jamaican Diaspora

The post promotes the National Housing Trust's Voluntary Contributor scheme for Jamaicans living overseas, presenting it as a route to low-interest mortgages for property in Jamaica. It is a short spoken explainer with on-screen words "jamaica", "complete" and "nothing".

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Confirm you legally work and live abroad, and keep proof of this.
2. Gather proof of income and proof of employment.
3. Complete the NHT voluntary contributor application form on the NHT site.
4. Pay roughly 3% of your salary per month as a voluntary contribution.
5. Keep contributions active for two years to qualify for low-interest loans to buy land or property, or build, in Jamaica.
6. Start contributing well before you plan to buy, since the two-year wait applies.
7. Contributions are refunded to you in the eighth year.

## Research

The post leaves out that loan eligibility needs 104 weeks of contributions, 52 of them recent, and that loans are not guaranteed. It also gives no actual mortgage rate.

### Resources

* [NHT – Who is a Voluntary Contributor? (nht.gov.jm)](https://nht.gov.jm/voluntary-contribution): page, found by search, verified, high confidence. The official NHT page lists the 3% rate, the proof of residency and income documents, the application form and the refund with interest in the 8th year.

### Key steps

1. Check you are eligible. You must be a non-Jamaican resident abroad who isn't legally required to contribute, or a Jamaican pensioner under 70 approved by the Branch Manager.
2. Get proof of residency: a valid Permanent Resident Card, passport or foreign work permit.
3. Get proof of income: a recent pay slip, an employment letter or your latest tax return.
4. Fill in the Voluntary Contributor's Application Form through the "Register Now" link on the NHT page. Submit it with your documents.
5. Pay 3% of gross earnings each month. Self-employed people pay 3% of income after business expenses. Amounts are quoted in your local currency and converted at the Bank of Jamaica rate.
6. To qualify for a loan, you need at least 104 weeks of contributions, with 52 of them paid just before you apply. This comes from search-result summaries, not the NHT page I opened.
7. Each year's contributions are refunded with interest in the 8th year. Refunds go to a Jamaican bank account.
8. For questions, call (876) 929-6500 or use the NHT contact page.

### Not found

The 104-week loan rule did not appear on the NHT page I opened. It came from search-result summaries. The post gives no actual mortgage rate or loan limits.
