# NHT Voluntary Contribution Program for Jamaican Diaspora

The post promotes the National Housing Trust's Voluntary Contributor scheme for Jamaicans living overseas, presenting it as a route to low-interest mortgages for property in Jamaica. It is a short spoken explainer with on-screen words "jamaica", "complete" and "nothing".

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Confirm you legally work and live abroad, and keep proof of this.
2. Gather proof of income and proof of employment.
3. Complete the NHT voluntary contributor application form on the NHT site.
4. Pay roughly 3% of your salary per month as a voluntary contribution.
5. Keep contributions active for two years to qualify for low-interest loans to buy land or property, or build, in Jamaica.
6. Start contributing well before you plan to buy, since the two-year wait applies.
7. Contributions are refunded to you in the eighth year.
