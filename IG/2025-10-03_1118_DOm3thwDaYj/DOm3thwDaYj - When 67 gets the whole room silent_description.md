# Counting to seven to quiet a choir

A choir teacher shows a counting call that silences a room of about 50 energetic tenor-bass singers. On-screen text says it works every time, and a "10 min later" clip follows.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Count aloud slowly from one to seven to get the singers' attention.
2. Say "Now, I will complete" and count one through seven again.
3. Finish with "All right, now" to bring the room to silence.
