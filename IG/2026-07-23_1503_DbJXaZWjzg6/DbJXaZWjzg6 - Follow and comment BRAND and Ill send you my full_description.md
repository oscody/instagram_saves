# Copy viral openers for your first 20 days

The creator explains how she reached 3 million followers by filming one day a week, and describes a 30-day approach for a new platform that starts with copying the openers of the most viral videos in your niche. The full version is only shared by comment or DM.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Pick one topic where what you enjoy, what you're good at, and what people already pay for overlap, and write it as one sentence: 'I teach [who] how to [what].' Keep it for 30 days.
2. Pick one platform that suits how you communicate, then find the top creators in your niche and collect their most viral videos.
3. Write down the first line of each of the 10 most viral videos exactly as spoken.
4. For about your first 20 posts, copy the title and opener of the proven viral videos exactly, then add your own twist to the rest of the video.
5. Post one short video a day, filmed in one take on your phone without over-editing.
6. Around day 21, swap in your own openers and start making original hooks.
7. Don't try to sell early: build attention first, then monetize through coaching, info products, or consulting.
