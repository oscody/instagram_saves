# VLC Is More Powerful Than Most People Realize

The carousel says VLC can open live TV channels and streams through publicly available IPTV playlists, and that VLC does not own or host those channels. It shows the setup steps, with sports playlist examples and a note that some content may need a VPN where it is geo-restricted.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Install VLC for free: videolan.org for Windows, Mac or Linux, or search for VLC in the app store on Android or iOS.
2. Find a public playlist, such as the sports channels at iptv-org.github.io/iptv/ or the full TV lineup at github.com/Free-TV/IPTV.
3. In VLC, go to Open → Network Stream, paste the playlist URL, then click Play.
4. Use the playlist view to switch between channels; some streams may take a few seconds to load.
5. If content is geo-restricted in your region, you may need a VPN.
6. Use only public, legal, region-permitted streams, since availability and quality can change at any time.

### Key steps

1. Install VLC from videolan.org.
2. Copy a playlist URL, e.g. https://iptv-org.github.io/iptv/index.m3u or https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Free-TV/IPTV/master/playlist.m3u8.
3. In VLC choose Media > Open Network Stream (Open → Network Stream on some versions), paste the URL, and click Play.
4. Open the playlist view (Ctrl+L on Windows/Linux, Cmd+Shift+P on Mac) to switch channels.
5. For sports or country-specific lists, see PLAYLISTS.md in the iptv-org repo.
6. Geoblocked channels (marked Ⓖ in Free-TV) may not play outside their country.
