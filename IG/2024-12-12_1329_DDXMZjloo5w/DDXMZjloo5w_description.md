# Immersion cooling with fluorinated liquid explained

A demo shows computer hardware submerged in a non-conductive fluorinated liquid at a trade show, with the narrator describing immersion cooling as a way to cool high-heat processors and GPUs. The post describes the liquid as safe around electronics, with vapor condensing back into the pool.

## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. Two-phase immersion cooling is real and works as the reel describes. The "20 times faster than air" figure is unsourced, and the reel leaves out that the main fluids (3M Novec) are PFAS and have been discontinued.
