# Side projects that impress employers

A tech creator argues that side projects nobody uses mostly help you learn but don't impress employers. He lists the traits of projects that get attention and suggests how to describe them in interviews. The post ends by offering a paid training by comment.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Build projects that have real users, not ones only you use.
2. Aim for production-level problems, since those show real engineering experience.
3. Make the engineering technically impressive.
4. Build something interesting enough that people want to share it.
5. You don't need millions of users; even a small number of real people using it gives you a story to tell.
6. Frame the project in interviews as: built it, launched it, people used it, here's what broke, and here's how I improved it.
