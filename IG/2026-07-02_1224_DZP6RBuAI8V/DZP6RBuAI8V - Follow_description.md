# Grants for first-time Black-owned businesses

A man stands at a waterfront with the Manhattan Bridge behind him while on-screen text urges viewers to apply for five grants for first-time Black-owned businesses before Dec 31, 2026. The listed grants are the FedEx Small Business Grant ($50,000), WISE 2026 Program ($75,000), Women Business Amber Grant ($10,000), NASE Growth Grant ($4,000), and Hello Alice Grant ($15,000).

## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. The grants exist, but most are not specific to Black-owned or first-time businesses. For example, the Amber Grant is for women-owned businesses. The Dec 31, 2026 deadline doesn't fit the programs I checked, which use their own cycles.
* Checked: 2026-10-22 by claude-sonnet-5-5

### Resources

* [Amber Grant for Women (WomensNet) (ambergrantsforwomen.com)](https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/get-an-amber-grant/): page, found by search, not opened, medium confidence. Search result shows a $10,000 monthly grant for women-owned businesses. That matches the post's amount, but it is not Black-owner specific.
* [FedEx Small Business Grant 2024 official rules (fedex.com)](https://www.fedex.com/content/dam/fedex/us-united-states/Small-Business-Center/upload/Small-Business-Grants-Program-Rules-2024.pdf): page, found by search, not opened, medium confidence. Official rules for the 2024 cycle: a $50,000 top prize, 1–99 employees, and an active FedEx account. The 2026 rules weren't confirmed.

### Key steps

1. Check each grant's official page for its own deadline and eligibility. Don't rely on a single Dec 31 date.
2. FedEx: you need a for-profit U.S. business with fewer than 100 employees and a FedEx account. The window is usually early in the year.
3. Amber Grant: apply at ambergrantsforwomen.com. It is for women-owned businesses, and one $10,000 grant is awarded each month.
4. Search the Hello Alice, NASE and WISE sites directly for current terms.

### Not found

I did not find or check official 2026 pages for FedEx, WISE, NASE or Hello Alice. I opened no pages, so nothing is verified. The sources I did see conflict on FedEx prize amounts and dates. Nothing in the vault covers this post.
