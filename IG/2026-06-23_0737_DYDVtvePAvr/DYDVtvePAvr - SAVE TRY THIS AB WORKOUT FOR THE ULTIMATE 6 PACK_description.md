## What it teaches

1. Do 2 rounds of the ab circuit.
2. Spend 30 seconds on each exercise, moving straight to the next one.
3. Work through the exercises in the order shown: leg-based core moves such as bicycle kicks and leg raises, then sit-ups and Russian twists, then alternating in-and-outs (Level 2), then heel touches for the obliques (Level 9), and finish with a hold.
4. Keep sit-ups, leg raises and heel touches controlled, with the lower back and legs managed as shown on the mat.
5. Eat in a calorie deficit or keep your diet healthy for the best results.
