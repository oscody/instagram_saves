# Juicy damage numbers for games

A game-dev tutorial that turns plain damage numbers into more satisfying ones, using a purple block as a demo target and showing each effect in turn. The post's on-screen labels are pop, float, fade, arc, size, colour, crit, stack and spread.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Choose a good font; the post uses Bungie from Google Fonts.
2. Pop the damage numbers in when they arrive.
3. Float them out as they appear.
4. Fade them out once their sequence ends.
5. Give each number an arc-like offset so it falls.
6. Adjust the size based on the value of the number.
7. Change the colour over time based on the action happening.
8. For crits, add particles and a shake.
9. Try stacking damage numbers on very fast attacks.
10. Spread the numbers out at their spawn location so they look random.
11. These are optional steps: apply whichever ones suit the feel of your game.
