# Four storytelling resources and their key takeaways

The speaker lists four storytelling resources they would watch or read if they had two hours to save their parents: a video on the neuroscience of addictive storytelling, a video on three storytelling ingredients, a video on a five-line story skeleton, and Toni Morrison's novel Song of Solomon. ## What it teaches

1. Treat storytelling as a neurochemical reaction: build content so viewers get repeated dopamine hits and keep watching (Kallaways, "The neuroscience of addictive storytelling").
2. Strip storytelling down to three ingredients and make the audience feel they are there in the moment, watching the story unfold (Vinh Gang, "The only 3 ingredients you need to be a better storyteller").
3. Write out a five-line story skeleton in about two minutes to organize your thoughts, and use it to stress-test a content idea before spending time filming or editing (Tim Runia, "the only 5 lines you need to tell any story").
4. Read Song of Solomon to see how Toni Morrison guides the reader through her stories to make them believe certain things, then pulls those beliefs away.
