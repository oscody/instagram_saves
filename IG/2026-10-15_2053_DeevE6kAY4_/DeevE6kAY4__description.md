# Build an AI project to get interviews

The post says that job seekers with no interview responses should build an AI project tied to their target role to discuss in applications. It shows a ChatGPT chat with a table of sample project ideas, each with a cost and time estimate.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Open Gemini, Claude, or ChatGPT.
2. Paste this prompt, replacing the blank with your target job title: "I'm applying to this role _____. Brainstorm 10 easy AI project ideas I can do for <$100 that are highly relevant to the role and would help my resume stand out because everyone's obsessed with AI."
3. Pick one of the 10 ideas and ask the AI for the steps to build it.
4. Ask which tools you would need and check their costs before you start.
5. Finish the project before adding it to your resume.
6. On your resume, describe what you built and how it relates to the job.
