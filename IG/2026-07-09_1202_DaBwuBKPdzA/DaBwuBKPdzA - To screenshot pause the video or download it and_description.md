# Claude settings and Excel-with-AI setup steps

The post is a part 2 of a Claude tips series, showing a settings checklist titled "9 Claude Settings you must turn on" and an infographic called "Everything You Can Do In Excel (with AI)" credited to Ruben Hassid's "How to AI".

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Settings to turn on: 1) Model: Opus 4.8. 2) Effort: High. 3) Tools: turn on Web search. 4) Connectors: connect Gmail and Google Drive. 5) Skills: manage saved skills, add skills, or create new skills. 6) Projects: manage or add projects. 7) Workspace mode: Cowork. 8) Instructions: set global instructions Claude should always follow. 9) Memory: edit global instructions that apply to all sessions.
2. Excel with AI setup: download Claude at claude.com/download (Pro plan, $20/month). Open the Cowork tab. Select a folder from your computer. Pick Opus 4.8 + Thinking. Connect Google Drive via Connectors.
3. Prompt: paste a prompt that fills in your data, purpose, sheets needed (name, columns, formulas) and formatting (currency, dates, frozen rows, totals), and asks Claude to list its top 10 assumptions before building.
4. Export: click Google Drive in Cowork; the file opens in Google Sheets with every tab, formula and format carried over.
5. Iterate inside Cowork for structural changes, such as adding a dashboard tab, changing a scenario, or adding conditional formatting.
6. Edit with ChatGPT in Google Sheets: go to Extensions > Add-ons > Get add-ons, connect ChatGPT, pick the Heavy model, use @ to tag a tab, then click allow.
7. Pro tip: start in Cowork, then edit in Sheets, never the other way around.

### Key steps

1. Download Claude and get a Pro plan ($20/month), which Cowork requires.
2. Open the Cowork tab and select a folder. Pick the top Opus model with thinking (the guide says Opus 4.7 + Adaptive thinking).
3. Connect Google Drive through the + menu under Connectors.
4. Give Cowork your data. Uploading it works best.
5. Paste the prompt template: data source, purpose in one sentence, sheets (name, columns, row meaning, formulas), and formatting (currency, dates, frozen header, totals). Ask it to list its top 10 assumptions before building.
6. Fix any assumptions you disagree with, then tell it to build. Complex models can take several minutes.
7. Click Google Drive to open the result in Google Sheets.
8. For targeted edits in Sheets, go to Extensions > Add-ons > Get add-ons, install the official ChatGPT add-on, pick the Heavy model, and use @ to tag a tab.
