# Why trees grow in two directions at once

A man on a couch explains that a seed's roots grow down under the ground while the shoot grows up toward light, then compares the hidden underground struggle to unseen work that makes visible results possible.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Roots grow down, away from light and toward gravity (the speaker calls this gravitropic).
2. The part of the tree above ground grows up, toward light and away from gravity (phototropic growth).
3. A seed's root pushes through dark, damp, resistant soil first, and once it breaks through, the upward growth meets no resistance.
4. Life lesson: the difficult work done in the dark, out of sight, is what makes the work people see in the light possible.
