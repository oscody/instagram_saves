# Claude prompt hack for goals and daily schedule

A man in a hoodie sits in a lounge and talks to the camera about a Claude method for working toward your goals. On screen text and captions state the steps, with the prompts spelled out.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Open Claude and write down all of your goals and desires, everything you want to accomplish.
2. Right before sending, add this prompt: "Tell me a story of a day in my life as my future self based on this description."
3. Read the story Claude returns and truly visualize yourself in that future position.
4. Then type this prompt: "Create me a daily schedule to follow that will allow me to achieve this life."
