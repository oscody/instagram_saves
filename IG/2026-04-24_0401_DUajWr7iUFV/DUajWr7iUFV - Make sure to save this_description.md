### Key steps

1. Minoxidil 5% (Kirkland): apply 1 mL to the dry scalp twice daily with the dropper.
2. Let it dry 2–4 hours before contact with pillows or hats.
3. Expect results at about 2–4 months of steady use.
4. The label says it is for crown thinning, not a receding hairline. Don't use it if hair loss is sudden or patchy, or if you're under 18.
5. The other items (As I Am, Hollywood Beauty tea tree oil, Wild Growth, Jamaican Mango & Lime castor oil) are scalp-care oils. They are not proven to regrow hair.
