# Bare minimum skincare routine with product picks

A reel filmed in a pharmacy aisle where the creator walks through his bare-minimum skincare routine and the products he uses, including a CeraVe cleanser shown on screen and a glycolic acid toner. It ends with a promise of a dental or body care routine if viewers ask in the comments.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Build a basic routine from four steps: cleanser, toner, moisturizer, and SPF.
2. Pick the cleanser by skin type: the creator suggests one for normal to oily skin and a different one for dry skin.
3. Use a glycolic acid toner (The Ordinary, per the speaker) to remove dead skin cells.
4. Apply the glycolic acid on cotton pads and wipe it over the face instead of rubbing.
5. The creator says the same glycolic acid can go on underarms and elbow creases to reduce bad odor.
6. For SPF, choose one that leaves no white cast, which he recommends especially for Black skin.
7. Include a moisturizer as the final step; the creator mentions one but does not name it.
