# Three questions to ask on a first date

A clip from the Jamal Bryant Podcast in which Dr. Alduan Tartt suggests three questions to ask on a date, to gauge how someone handles relationships and whether your goals line up. The caption promotes a full podcast episode.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Ask how good the person is at relationships, and what their past exes would say about them, including the pros and cons.
2. Ask for their idea of an ideal spouse; if marriage isn't the topic, ask what they think a good boyfriend or girlfriend is.
3. Ask what their relationships have been like, to see whether you should even like them and whether you are headed to the same place, including whether they are only looking to casually date.
