# Claude vs OpenAI vs Gemini resume test

A reel reporting that the author sent 50 job applications with each of three AI-written resumes, and that the Claude version got 5 interviews versus 3 for OpenAI and 1 for Gemini. It walks through the reasons the author gives for Claude's result and shows a Claude prompt asking for an artifact to keep the resume updatable.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Give the AI the same prompt, template and experience for each resume you want to compare.
2. Tailor each resume's bullet points to the target role by pulling keywords from the job descriptions.
3. Rewrite bullets to match what hiring managers look for in those postings.
4. Add metrics to every achievement, including ones you did not think to quantify.
5. Keep the resume skimmable, since recruiters skim for about six seconds.
6. Use industry language taken from real job postings so the resume does not sound generic.
7. Highlight your own strengths and unique achievements rather than letting the AI replace your personal voice.
8. Ask the AI to build an artifact from the winning resume so you can update it quickly for each new job.
