# 8 ChatGPT prompts for job searching

A carousel listing eight ChatGPT prompt templates for job hunting, covering cover letters, LinkedIn outreach, resumes, resume-to-job matching, application emails, and interview practice. Each slide shows one prompt with bracketed placeholders and a sample ChatGPT reply.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Cover letter: prompt ChatGPT to write a cover letter for a [Title] role at [Company], emphasizing your experience in [Specific Field/Technology], and explaining how your contributions at [Previous Company] match the job requirements. Paste the job description.
2. LinkedIn networking: prompt ChatGPT to draft a message to a professional at [Company] that states your interest in the [Title] position and how your background in [Specific Field/Technology] makes you a strong candidate.
3. Resume from scratch: prompt ChatGPT with the [Title], [Company], your [X] years of experience, 3-5 bullet points per role, 10 key job description keywords, your past titles with years at each company, and the pasted job description.
4. Resume match check: prompt ChatGPT to compare your skills with the job description for the [Title] role at [Company] and report a mismatch percentage. Provide the job description text or link and your skills list.
5. Update resume: prompt ChatGPT to revise your current resume for the [Title] role at [Company], focusing on skills named in the job description. Paste both the job description and your current resume.
6. Application email: prompt ChatGPT to write an email to the employer's address that attaches your resume and cover letter for the [Title] role at [Company], highlights key achievements at [Previous Company], and expresses enthusiasm for the role.
7. Interview prep: prompt ChatGPT to list [number] interview questions based on the pasted job description.
8. Mock interview: prompt ChatGPT to run a technical mock interview for the [Job Role], asking 15 questions one at a time about your [Specific Field/Technology] to gauge your expertise.

### Key steps

1. Open ChatGPT and paste the job description into the prompt.
2. Cover letter: "Write a cover letter for the role of [Title] at [Company], emphasizing my experience in [Field]. Mention how my contributions at [Previous Company] align with the job requirements: <job description>."
3. LinkedIn: ask for a connection message to a professional at [Company] about the [Title] role and how your background fits.
4. Fit check: give the job description and your skills, and ask whether they match and for a mismatch percentage.
5. Update resume: paste the job description and your current resume, and ask it to focus on the relevant skills.
6. Interview prep: ask for [number] interview questions based on the job description.
7. Mock interview: "Conduct a technical mock interview for [Job Role]. Ask me 15 questions related to [Field], one after the other."
8. Check every output for invented facts before you send it. Use only true details about yourself.
