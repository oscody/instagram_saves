A professor describes a Skittles trading game he ran for his Duke and Silicon Valley class, in which he charged a one-skittle fee on every trade. He uses it to argue that fees, credit cards, stock exchanges, team owners and social platforms profit whoever wins, and that people should ask who designed the game and why they are playing it.
