# Auditing what AI remembers about you

A talking-head reel about AI memory, with on-screen text and a screenshot of an Andrej Karpathy tweet about LLM knowledge bases. It describes asking a chatbot to list what it remembers about you and fix the wrong entries.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Open a chat with your AI assistant and ask it to list everything it remembers about you, flagging anything that is old, wrong, or came from just one chat, then ask you what to fix.
2. Don't tell the AI what to save. Instead, say something like "save whatever helps you write like me, do my work better, you decide what's worth keeping."
