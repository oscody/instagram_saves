### Key steps

1. Open a new chat in your AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.).
2. Paste: "List everything you remember about me, flag anything that's old, wrong, or came from just one chat, then ask me what to fix."
3. Answer its questions and correct or delete the wrong entries.
4. Don't dictate what to save. Say: "Save whatever helps you write like me and do my work better. You decide what's worth keeping."
5. Optional: build a Karpathy-style markdown wiki from your notes (see his LLM Wiki gist).
