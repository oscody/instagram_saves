# Starting a Book: The Why Questions

A reel for people who want to write a book but don't know where to start, showing a numbered list titled "The Why" with questions about the story's purpose. The post says answering them comes before working on plot and theme.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Ask why you want to tell this story.
2. Ask why you write.
3. Decide what you want readers to walk away with.
4. Consider what this will reflect about you.
5. Identify what kinds of stories you want to read and love, and want more of.
6. Answer these questions first, since they help you dig into the plot and theme in the next step.
