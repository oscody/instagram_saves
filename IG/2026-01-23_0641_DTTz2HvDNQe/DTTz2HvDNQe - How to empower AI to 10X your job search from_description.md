# Using AI to 10X your job search in 2026

A four-step whiteboard walkthrough of using AI tools to build a LinkedIn profile, generate an ATS resume, auto-apply to jobs, and find referral contacts, with time estimates for each step. The post mentions a paid pre-built template and a comment-to-get offer, which are not included here.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Step 1: Create a LinkedIn profile in about 30 minutes. Narrate each job experience into an AI audio tool, then enter five prompts at the end of each section, and paste the results into LinkedIn.
2. Step 2: Create an ATS resume in about 20 seconds. Download your LinkedIn profile as a PDF, paste it into Claude with the prompts, and use the LaTeX code it generates in Overleaf to produce the resume.
3. Step 3: Auto-apply to jobs in about 1 hour. Use n8n with an AI agent: enter your resume, your details and your job preferences, and it sends jobs with a 75% or greater match. Customize the resume for each role and use Easy Apply.
4. Step 4: Find referrals and prepare for interviews in about 20 minutes. Download the sponsorship application data from the DOL government site, which lists sponsoring companies and referral contacts, use the template email to reach out, and use the AI interview prompt to prepare.
