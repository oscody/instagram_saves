## Caption

As the debut entry in Lucasfilm’s new "Star Wars: Visions Presents" initiative, which expands fan-favorite anthology shorts into full spin-off series—Star Wars: Visions Presents – The Ninth Jedi has officially premiered on Disney+ and Hulu. 

Animated by Production I.G, the story follows Kara, the daughter of master armorer Lah Zhima, who forged six lightsabers to rebuild the Jedi Order. When the Sith capture her father, Kara sets out across the galaxy to rescue him and gather other surviving Force-sensitives.
