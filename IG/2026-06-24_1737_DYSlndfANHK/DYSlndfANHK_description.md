# Using AI to fix a restaurant's website

A pizzeria owner demonstrates a website-grading tool on a laptop, showing a scan of her restaurant against local competitors, an online health score of 94, and a list of fixes. She points viewers to grader.owner.com.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Go to grader.owner.com and type your restaurant's name.
2. The AI analyzes your online presence and website, including your Google Business profile, your reviews, and how you rank against local competitors in your zip code.
3. Review the AI's list of everything on your website that needs fixing to rank number one on Google.
4. Click the button to let the AI fix those problems and rebuild the website for you.
