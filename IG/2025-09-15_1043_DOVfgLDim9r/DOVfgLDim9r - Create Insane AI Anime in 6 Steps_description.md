# Create Insane AI Anime in 6 Steps

The post walks through a six-step workflow for making anime-style AI video with Nano Banana and Seedance, shown with screenshots of the Weavy interface and generated anime frames. The full guide with the prompts is offered by comment.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Sign up at weavy.ai, which gives access to both Nano Banana and Seedance.
2. Upload a source image of your character to keep the character consistent.
3. In Seedance, set the duration to 5 seconds, enter a prompt, and run it to get a multi-shot scene.
4. Switch to Nano Banana, use the same source image from step 2, enter a prompt, and generate a detailed anime frame.
5. Take the image generated in step 4 back to Seedance and enter a prompt to animate it.
6. Go back to Nano Banana, use the last picture produced as the input, and enter a prompt.
7. Repeat the alternating Seedance and Nano Banana steps to keep extending the animation.
