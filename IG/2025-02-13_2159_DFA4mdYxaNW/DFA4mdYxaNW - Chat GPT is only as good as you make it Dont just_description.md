## What it teaches

1. Prompt 1: Ask ChatGPT to act as a picky hiring manager and tell you why it would reject your resume for this role.
2. Prompt 2: Ask it to evaluate your resume for clarity and suggest ways to make it more readable in 6 seconds.
3. Prompt 3: Ask it to adapt your resume for a leadership role without removing technical hands-on details.
4. Keep prompting again and again rather than asking for generic help, so the results get better.
