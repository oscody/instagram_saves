# Finding and messaging hiring managers on LinkedIn

A walkthrough of three ways to find hiring managers and recruiters on LinkedIn, followed by two message templates: a connection request note and a longer follow-up once connected.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Find hiring managers: open the company's LinkedIn page, go to the 'People' tab, and search '[target position] manager'.
2. Find recruiters: on the same People tab, expect to sift through a few profiles to pick the right ones.
3. Optional search: type 'Hiring [target position]' into the LinkedIn search bar, but note you can only filter by recency and connection level (1st, 2nd, 3rd+), so results may be less useful.
4. Search your network (preferred option): go to the 'My Network' tab and search '[target position] manager'.
5. Filter those results for 1st or 2nd connections and your target geographic location; optionally add past employers and school to find people with shared background.
6. Send a short connection request note: greet the person by first name, introduce yourself, say you'd love to connect with a fellow [shared similarity], mention you saw they recently posted the [target position] role, and ask for a 15-minute call to learn more about the role.
7. After connecting, send a longer message: thank them, restate your interest in the [target position] role, explain why you can contribute based on a similar role you've held, list 2-3 skills or accomplishments tailored to the job description, and offer a 15-20 minute chat in the coming week, with no pressure if they're busy.
