# Cheapest months to visit Caribbean islands

A three-slide graphic lists the months each Caribbean island is typically cheapest to visit, from Jamaica and the Dominican Republic to St. Kitts & Nevis. The caption asks viewers which island they'd book first and notes that prices change with flights, hotels, events, and departure city.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Pick the island you want to visit and check its listed cheapest months (for example, Jamaica, Dominican Republic, Puerto Rico, and Cayman Islands are May and September; Barbados is June and November; Bahamas and Aruba are September and October).
2. Book in those months to keep the trip cheaper.
3. Expect prices to change with flights, hotels, events, and where you fly from.
4. Do your own research before you book.
