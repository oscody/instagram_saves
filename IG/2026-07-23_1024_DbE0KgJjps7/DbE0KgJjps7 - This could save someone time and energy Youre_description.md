# Ten questions to ask before catching feelings

A six-slide carousel of ten questions to ask a man before you get emotionally invested, each with a short note on what to look for in his answer. The caption also includes an unrelated Estonian paragraph about a music artist.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Ask what kind of relationship he is open to right now; look for clear answers, not vague ones like "we'll see."
2. Ask how he usually responds when conflict happens; see whether he works through it or shuts down and runs.
3. Ask what commitment looks like to him; people use the same word but can mean very different things.
4. Ask whether he has ever been unfaithful in a relationship; pay attention to his energy, not just his words.
5. Ask what really ended his last relationship; check whether he can take accountability or places all the blame.
6. Ask what he is building in his life right now; his direction says a lot about his stability.
7. Ask who he is when life gets hard; anyone can be consistent when life feels easy.
8. Ask what standards are non-negotiable for him; a man with no standards often has no structure.
9. Ask how he views providing in a relationship; listen for maturity, not ego.
10. Ask what he wants most from a woman; see whether he is looking for a teammate or just validation.
