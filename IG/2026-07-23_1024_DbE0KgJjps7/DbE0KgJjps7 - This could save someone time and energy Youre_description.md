# 20 questions to ask before catching feelings

A 11-slide carousel of 20 questions to ask a man before getting emotionally invested, each with a short note on what to look for in his answer. The caption is a one-line intro ("This could save someone time and energy").

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Before catching feelings, ask him the questions below and pay attention to his answers.
2. 1. What kind of relationship are you open to right now? Want clarity, not vague answers like 'we'll see.'
3. 2. When conflict happens, how do you usually respond? Does he work through it or shut down and run?
4. 3. What does commitment look like to you? People use the same word but mean very different things.
5. 4. Have you ever been unfaithful in a relationship? Pay attention to his energy, not just his words.
6. 5. What really ended your last relationship? Can he take accountability, or is it all blame?
7. 6. What are you building in your life right now? His direction says a lot about his stability.
8. 7. Who are you when life gets hard? Anyone can be consistent when life feels easy.
9. 8. What standards are non-negotiable for you? A man with no standards often has no structure.
10. 9. How do you view providing in a relationship? Listen for maturity, not ego.
11. 10. What do you want most from a woman? Is he looking for a teammate or just validation?
12. 11. How do you handle jealousy? This tells you a lot about emotional maturity.
13. 12. What's something you regret in past relationships? Look for self-awareness, not finger-pointing.
14. 13. Are you emotionally ready for a real relationship? Notice if he answers honestly or jokes it away.
15. 14. What are your long-term goals? A man with vision usually moves differently.
16. 15. How do you manage money? Financial habits often reflect life habits.
17. 16. Do you create peace or chaos in relationships? Some people confuse drama with passion.
18. 17. How do you act when you realize you were wrong? The ability to own mistakes matters a lot.
19. 18. What does loyalty mean to you? Never assume your definitions match.
20. 19. What would make you leave a relationship? His boundaries reveal his mindset.
21. 20. Are you genuinely ready to build something serious? Hesitation can be an answer too.
