# Getting your Instagram content into Google search

The creator says Google now shows social content in search results, giving posts a longer shelf life and letting you see which searches lead people to your content. The screen shows the Google Search Console welcome page with Instagram, TikTok, X and YouTube "Add" options.

## What it teaches

1. Open Google Search Console.
2. Sign in to Google Search Console.
3. Click Add next to Instagram (or TikTok, X or YouTube) to connect your social account.
4. Use the search queries that lead people to your content as the basis for what you post next, instead of guessing.

### Key steps

1. Open Google Search Console and sign in.
2. Open the property selector and choose to add a new property.
3. Pick Instagram, TikTok, X or YouTube. Each account is its own property.
4. Follow the on-screen prompts to authorize and verify the account.
5. Use the Performance report to see clicks, impressions and queries by post.
6. Use the Insights report for traffic trends and top posts.
7. Use the Achievements report for milestones.
8. Base future posts on the queries that already lead people to your content.
