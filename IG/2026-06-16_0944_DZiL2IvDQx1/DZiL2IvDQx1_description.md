# Better text openers to replace small talk

A five-slide carousel of text message lines meant to replace dull small-talk questions like "how was your day," shown over photos of a woman in a cap, a red top, a gym and a campus setting.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Don't ask a dull question like "How was your day?"
2. Flip it into "Rate your day out of 10, and what would've made it a 10?" to show interest without sounding basic
3. Be unexpected by asking "What's something random that happened today that made you laugh?" to break the small-talk pattern
4. Build tension by asking "What were you thinking about before I texted you?" to be flirty and get her emotionally engaged
5. Master the vibe: it's how you say it, and confidence + curiosity = connection
