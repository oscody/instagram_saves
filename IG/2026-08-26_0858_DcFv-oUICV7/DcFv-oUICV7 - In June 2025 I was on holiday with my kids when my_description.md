# Nine-movement Tai Chi-inspired daily routine

A man shares his weight-loss story and a nine-movement, Tai Chi-inspired sequence he does daily, with each move labelled on screen. Frames show him outdoors by the London Eye demonstrating Body Waves, Dead Arms and Ballet Squats.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Do each movement for 60 seconds.
2. Let the video loop, then move on to the next movement.
3. Work through the nine movements in order: Lymphatic Hops, Body Waves, Trunk Twists, Arm Swings, Dead Arms, Golf Swings, Marches, Ballet Squats, Horse Stance.
4. Go at your own pace and adapt where needed.

## Caption

In June 2025, I was on holiday with my kids when my eldest pointed at a man in the pool and said…

“Woooah Dad, look! That man’s got a six-pack!”

I remember looking over and thinking…

I want to be that man.

Not going to lie, I’m pretty pleased to say… I’m nearly there! 😂💪

But this transformation didn’t come from one magic exercise.

These Tai Chi-inspired movements have certainly become part of my journey, but they’re NOT the reason I lost the weight.

What they HAVE done for me:

✅ My shoulder pain has gone
✅ Improved my mobility & range of movement
✅ Reduced morning stiffness
✅ Helped me recover from gym sessions
✅ Helped me feel ready to train harder
✅ Given me more energy
✅ Become a daily mental reset
✅ Given me 9 minutes to practise gratitude, be present and visualise my goals

Want to join me?

Do each movement for 60 seconds. Let the video loop and move onto the next:

1️⃣ Lymphatic Hops
2️⃣ Body Waves
3️⃣ Trunk Twists
4️⃣ Arm Swings
5️⃣ Dead Arms
6️⃣ Golf Swings
7️⃣ Marches
8️⃣ Ballet Squats
9️⃣ Horse Stance

Go at your own pace and adapt where needed.

And for full transparency, here’s what actually helped take me from 122.9kg → as low as 76.2kg:

✅ Improving my diet & prioritising protein
✅ Tirzepatide (Mounjaro)
✅ TRT after discovering low testosterone
✅ Weight training 3–4x a week
✅ 12,000+ steps most days
✅ Running/cardio
✅ Good hydration & supplements
✅ And consistently showing up

The six-pack was the thing that caught my attention…

But becoming healthier, stronger, more confident and proving to myself that I can stick to something has been the real prize. ❤️
