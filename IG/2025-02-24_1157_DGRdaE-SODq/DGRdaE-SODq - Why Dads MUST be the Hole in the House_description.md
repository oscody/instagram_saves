# Dads Should Make Kids Figure It Out

A dad reflects on how his strict, unemotional parents taught him to solve his own problems, and says he now tells his own kids "figure it out" more often than "I love you." The post is a personal parenting viewpoint with on-screen captions from a podcast-style interview.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. When a child asks for something, make them earn it instead of buying it.
2. When a child says they are bored, suggest a task such as reading a book or figuring something out.
3. When a child wants to do something, tell them to work out how to do it themselves.
4. Let children create their own solutions to problems rather than handing them answers.
5. Aim to build a growth mindset in children.
