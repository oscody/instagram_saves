# Six ChatGPT prompts for LinkedIn recruiter visibility

A carousel presenting six AI prompts meant to make a LinkedIn profile attract recruiters and job offers. Placeholders in square brackets are to be filled in by the reader.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Headline upgrade: ask the AI to rewrite your LinkedIn headline to attract [roles] in [industry], keyword-rich, value-packed and recruiter-friendly.
2. Magnetic About section: ask the AI to write your About section for a [field] professional that feels human, result-driven and builds instant trust.
3. Job description writer: paste your old job description for a [job title] at [company] and ask for a rewrite focused on results, metrics and top-performer proof.
4. Skill optimizer: ask for the top 10 most-searched skills and keywords in your [role/industry], then optimize your LinkedIn profile using those terms.
5. Recruiter language: ask what language recruiters in your [industry] respond best to, then mirror that language in your profile to increase visibility and credibility.
6. Visibility boost plan: ask the AI to create a simple 3-day-per-week LinkedIn activity plan to increase profile views and attract inbound offers without sounding desperate.
