### Key steps

1. Flash Raspberry Pi OS Bookworm (Debian 12). MediaPipe doesn't support Debian 13 Trixie.
2. Attach the Fusion HAT+ to the Pi (Pi 5, 4B, 3B+, 3B and Zero 2 W are supported).
3. Follow the 'Getting Started (Important)' guide in the docs.
4. Pick a module: Python basics, LLM voice assistant, or OpenCV/MediaPipe/YOLO vision.
5. If you hit a permissions error on kit OS 0.0.1, update to 0.1.0.
