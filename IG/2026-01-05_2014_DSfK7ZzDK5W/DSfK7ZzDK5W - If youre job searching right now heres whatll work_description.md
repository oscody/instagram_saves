# Job search tips for 2026

A short video with a four-point caption list of job search tips for 2026, plus an on-screen slide on leverage, follow-up and negotiation during late-stage interviews.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Make your resume and LinkedIn communicate the problems you solve.
2. Talk to people adjacent to the role, not just recruiters.
3. Address gaps before interviewers ask, and explain why you made decisions, since interviewers test judgment rather than knowledge.
4. When there is silence, don't read it as rejection; follow up calmly.
5. Keep interviewing even when one process looks strong, because momentum increases perceived value.
6. Signal selectivity by saying you are being thoughtful about team fit, which recruiters read as confidence.
7. Keep follow-up messages short, calm and free of apology.
8. Mention competing timelines without giving numbers, e.g. "I'm in late-stage conversations elsewhere."
9. Negotiate scope, not just salary: title, first projects, 90-day goals and review timelines.
