# How to get your brand recommended in ChatGPT

A reel walking through a process for writing an SEO article meant to get a brand recommended in ChatGPT. It uses ChatGPT's search queries, the top Google results' headings, a TLDR-led article, and Google Search Console indexing.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Open your ChatGPT chat URL and copy everything after /c/.
2. Right-click the page, choose Inspect, and open the Network tab.
3. Paste the copied string into the Network filter and refresh the page.
4. Click the request with orange brackets, open Response, and search for "queries" to see what ChatGPT searched.
5. Copy one search query and search it on Google.
6. Open the top three results and use the Detailed Chrome extension to go to Headings and click Copy for each page.
7. Paste all the headings into ChatGPT with your keyword and ask for an improved outline using the best of everything.
8. Ask ChatGPT to write the full article, starting with a short TLDR of 2–3 sentences, using clear sections and short paragraphs.
9. Ask ChatGPT for the page title, a confident meta description, and a URL slug.
10. Before publishing, add your own experience and images, proofread, remove em dashes and AI buzzwords, and rewrite anything generic or promotional.
11. Optionally set custom instructions for a professional but conversational, clear, direct, natural tone that avoids press-release style.
12. Publish the article.
13. In Google Search Console, go to Search Results, turn on Average Position, and sort Pages by position.
14. Link to the new article from 2–3 existing ranking pages.
15. Paste the new URL into the top bar and click Request Indexing.

## Caption

How to get your brand recommended in ChatGPT.

First: See What ChatGPT Searches
1. Open your ChatGPT chat URL.
2. Copy everything after `/c/`.
3. Right-click the page and choose Inspect.
4. Open the Network tab.
5. Paste the copied string into the Network filter.
6. Refresh the page.
7. Click the Request with orange brackets.
8. Open Response and search for Queries.

These queries show how ChatGPT broke up your prompt and what it searched.

Second: Check Google Results
1. Copy one ChatGPT search query.
2. Search it on Google.
3. Open the top three results.
4. Use the Detailed Chrome extension.
5. Go to Headings and click Copy for each page.

Third: Create a Better Outline

Paste all headings into ChatGPT and say:
> My keyword is [earlier ChatGPT search query].
> These are the headings from the top three Google results.
> Create an improved outline using the best of everything.

Fourth: Write the Article

Then prompt ChatGPT:
> Write the full article.
> Start with a short TLDR that gives the main points immediately.
> Use clear sections and short paragraphs.

Keep the TLDR to 2–3 sentences at the top.

Fifth: Get SEO Metadata

Ask ChatGPT:
> Give me the page title, meta description, and URL slug.
> Make the meta description confident.

Sixth: Humanize Before Publishing

Add your own experience and images.
Proofread carefully.
Remove em dashes and AI buzzwords.
Rewrite anything that sounds generic or promotional.

Optional custom instruction style:

Professional but conversational
Clear, direct, and natural
No emdashes or buzzwords.
Don’t sound like a press release.

Seventh: Publish and Index
1. Publish the article.
2. Open Google Search Console.
3. Go to Search Results and enable Average Position.
4. Sort Pages by position.
5. Link to the new article from 2-3 existing ranking pages.
6. Paste the new URL into the top bar and click Request Indexing.
