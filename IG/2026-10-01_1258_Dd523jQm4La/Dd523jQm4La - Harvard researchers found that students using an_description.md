# Five AI study prompts after a Harvard AI tutor claim

A carousel that opens with a claim about a Harvard study on AI tutoring and then lists five study prompts for Claude or ChatGPT, one per slide. Each slide gives a short rationale and a prompt with a [topic] placeholder.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Feynman technique: ask the AI to explain a topic in the simplest way possible, then explain it back in your own words, have it point out your mistakes and re-teach what you missed, and repeat until you can explain it on your own.
2. Test yourself until you master it: after studying a topic, ask the AI for 10 increasingly difficult questions, give an answer to each, and get a score plus an explanation of what you got wrong.
3. One-page study sheet: ask the AI to summarize the main concepts of a topic on a single page using bullet points, diagrams and examples so you can review it in 5 minutes.
4. Learning ladder: ask the AI to break a topic into 5 difficulty levels from beginner to advanced, with a clear goal at each stage.
5. Best resources: ask for a list of the 5 best resources (books, videos, courses or experts) for learning a topic quickly, with an explanation of why each one deserves your time.

## Caption

🧠🤖 Harvard researchers found that students using an AI tutor learned more than twice as much as those in a traditional active-learning class, while spending less time learning. 

The key wasn’t simply using AI, but using it like a real tutor.

Instead of giving answers immediately, the AI guided students through mistakes, provided feedback, and helped them reach solutions step by step. 

This suggests AI may be more useful for learning when it challenges your reasoning, tests your knowledge, explains concepts in different ways, and adjusts difficulty as you improve.
