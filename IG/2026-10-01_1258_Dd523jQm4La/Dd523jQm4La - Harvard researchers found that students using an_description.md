# Six Claude and ChatGPT study prompts for learning

The post cites a Harvard study claiming students using an AI tutor learned more than twice as much as in an active-learning class, then lists six study prompts for Claude and ChatGPT. Each slide pairs a learning idea with a prompt.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Feynman technique: ask the AI to explain a topic in the simplest way, then explain it back in your own words; the AI points out mistakes, re-teaches what you missed, and repeats until you can explain it on your own.
2. Test yourself until you master it: after studying a topic, ask the AI for 10 increasingly difficult questions, and after each answer get a score and an explanation of what you got wrong.
3. One-page study sheet: ask the AI to summarize the main concepts of a topic on a single page, using bullet points, diagrams and examples, so you can review it in 5 minutes.
4. Learning ladder: ask the AI to break a topic into 5 difficulty levels from beginner to advanced, with a clear goal at each stage, and build knowledge step by step.
5. Find the best resources: ask the AI for a list of the 5 best books, videos, courses or experts for learning a topic quickly, with a reason each one is worth your time.
6. Learn anything in 20 hours: ask the AI for a 20-hour plan focused on the 20% of concepts that produce 80% of the results, split into 10 two-hour sessions, each ending with a 15-minute review.

🧠🤖 Harvard researchers found that students using an AI tutor learned more than twice as much as those in a traditional active-learning class, while spending less time learning.
