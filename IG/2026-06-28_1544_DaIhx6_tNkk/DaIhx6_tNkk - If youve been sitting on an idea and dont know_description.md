10 Claude Prompts to Start Your Business From Scratch ⬇️

1️⃣ Check Your Idea
“Analyze this business idea for demand, competition, and profit potential: [insert idea].”

2️⃣ Clarify Your Audience
“Help me define a clear, profitable niche for this business idea: [insert idea].”

3️⃣ Build Your Offer
“Create a high-value offer I can sell based on my skills in [insert skill or industry].”

4️⃣ Name Your Business
“Give me 10 strong, brandable business name ideas for a [type of business].”

5️⃣ Simple Business Plan
“Write a one-page business plan with my audience, income model, and growth path for [insert idea].”

6️⃣ Sales Page Copy
“Write persuasive landing page copy that speaks directly to my target audience: [insert offer details].”

7️⃣ Content Plan
“Create a 7-day content plan that attracts the right people and turns them into customers.”

8️⃣ Email Welcome Series
“Write a 5-email welcome sequence that builds trust and converts new leads for my product or service.”

9️⃣ Pricing Help
“Help me price my offer so it matches its value and market demand.”

🔟 Launch Blueprint
“Create a simple 24-hour launch plan to promote this offer using email and social media.”
