# The Akan Three-Part Soul

A short reel claims that Akan philosophy divided the soul into three separate parts, each with its own job, origin, fate after death, and weakness. It is narrated over illustrated figures holding a gold orb, a feather, and fire symbols.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. The Akan people identified three separate souls, each with a different role.
2. The Krah is the divine life force given by the creator Nyame at birth. It leaves at death and keeps a person alive at the most basic level.
3. The sunsum is the personality or personal spirit. It travels during dreams and can be attacked by outside spiritual forces, which the Akan would say when someone feels drained for no clear reason.
4. The Moga is the blood soul. It passes only through the mother's line and connects a person to every ancestor in that line.
5. Each of the three souls has a different fate after death and a different weakness.
