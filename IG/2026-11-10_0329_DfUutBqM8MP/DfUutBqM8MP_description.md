# LARP formula for LinkedIn connection requests

The creator describes a four-part method called the LARP formula for sending LinkedIn connection requests to recruiters, hiring managers, and professionals, and says it earned a 90% response rate. The post also offers a free tool to people who comment a keyword.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Learn: read the target's LinkedIn profile to understand who they are.
2. Analyze: paste the profile into an AI tool with a prompt asking for a background on who they are, what they do, and what they're working on that excites them, plus any mutual friends or interests that can help build rapport.
3. Reach out: send a simple connection request of about three sentences covering who you are, why you're reaching out, and why you want to connect.
4. Personalize: mention one specific thing from their profile that stood out to you.
