# Five-stage conversation question sequence for connecting

A man presents a five-stage order of questions for building connection in conversation, each with a short reason for its place. The on-screen labels mark the stages as curiosity, identity, imagination, playful teasing, and emotional questions.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Start with curiosity: ask about the highlight of their week so far to trigger positive memories.
2. Move to identity: ask what they are weirdly passionate about, so they tell you who they are instead of being asked to talk about themselves.
3. Spark imagination: ask where they would go for a month if they could disappear to any country, to activate visual thinking.
4. Add playful tension: ask whether they are the responsible or the chaotic friend, then tease them whatever they answer.
5. Finish with emotional questions: ask what kind of moments make them feel most alive, to invite meaningful sharing.
6. Use the questions in your next interaction, and the post says the same questions also work when you are not approaching someone romantically.
