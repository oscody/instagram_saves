# Three Android anti-theft settings to turn on

A short reel showing three Android settings meant to make a phone harder to steal or easier to find. The caption frames it as a tip for locating a phone even if the SIM is removed.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Open Settings > Security and privacy > More and turn on Require Password to Power Off, so a thief cannot switch the phone off without the password (the post notes Samsung phones have this on by default).
2. Open Settings > Notifications & Quick Settings and set quick settings to not be allowed on the lock screen, so the Control Center cannot be used to turn off data or enable Airplane mode.
3. Open Settings > Security and privacy, go down to Device finders, and turn on Find My Device.
4. Inside Find My Device, open Find your offline devices and set it to With network in all areas, so the phone can still be located after the SIM is removed.
