# Blade's supposed West African folklore roots

The reel claims that Marvel's Blade draws on older West African folklore, naming the Adzi of the U people and the Obafo of the Ashanti as shape-shifting creatures and describing a half-human hunter who could detect and destroy them. It is illustrated with animated clips of a seated village woman holding a mask, a hooded green-eyed figure, and a bat perched on a painted mask.
