### Key steps

1. These are plain-text cues, not commands. Add them to a prompt, for example "/godmode" at the start or "L99" at the end.
2. OODA stands for Observe, Orient, Decide, Act. Spelling out those four steps in your prompt does the same thing more reliably.
3. For the same effect, say what you want directly, for example: "answer as a senior expert, state assumptions, skip hedging."
