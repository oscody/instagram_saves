# Three-step progression to learn backwards skating

A roller skater shows a three-step progression for backwards skating, using lemon (pizza-wedge style) movements, with each step demonstrated in the frames. The caption says to practice the steps in order.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Step 1: Do lemons in place, going forward and backward with your arms out for balance, to get comfortable and feel your edges. Once comfortable, do them backwards in motion.
2. Step 2: Stagger your feet and do the lemon on one leg, front or back, whichever feels most comfortable. Keep your arms out and your eyes where you're going.
3. Step 3: Alternate lemons with both legs to connect the movement into backward skating, which builds speed and balance.
4. Practice each step in order, building control before trying to roll backwards.
