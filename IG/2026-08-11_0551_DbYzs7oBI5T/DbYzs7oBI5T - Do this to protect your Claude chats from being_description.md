# Protect your Claude chats from Google leaks

A man with curly blond hair talks to the camera about shared Claude conversations appearing in Google results. A screen recording shows a site-restricted Google search returning a public Claude artifact titled "Brooks Miami Farewell Weekend Flyer," which he says he won't open.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Open Claude's settings and select Privacy.
2. Scroll down and select Shared chats.
3. Uncheck any shared chat you don't want visible on the internet.

## Caption

Do this to protect your Claude chats from being leaked..
