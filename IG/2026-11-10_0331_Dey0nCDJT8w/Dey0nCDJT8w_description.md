# Four kinds of ambition for AI prompting

A creator relays an engineer's idea that people ask AI for too little and sorts ambition into four types: speed, altitude, depth, and range. The post shows a depth prompt on screen and suggests asking "could I aim higher?" and "could I go deeper?" about a task.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Speed: use AI to finish the same work in less time.
2. Altitude: ask for the outcome you want rather than an individual task.
3. Depth: consider the task the way a top expert in the field would, using this prompt: say what a top professional would do that you're not asking for; name the rawest material they would start from instead of pre-digested input; name the specific methods and tools they would use; say what, other than you or the AI, would show the result is right; then rewrite your prompt to ask for exactly that.
4. Range: draw on multiple fields rather than only the one the task seems to need, e.g. a marketing problem could draw on psychology, statistics, or design.
5. Pick one task you're working on and ask whether you could aim higher, go deeper, or bring in another perspective.
