### Key steps

1. Go to learnyourway.withgoogle.com (Google Labs). No comment or DM is needed.
2. Upload a source text such as a PDF, or open one of the sample lessons.
3. Set your grade level and interests so the examples are adapted to you.
4. Pick a format: immersive text, quiz, narrated slides, audio lesson or mind map.
5. Use the quizzes for feedback and explanations.
