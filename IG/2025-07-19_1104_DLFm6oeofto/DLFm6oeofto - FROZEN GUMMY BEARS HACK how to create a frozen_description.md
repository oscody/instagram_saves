# Frozen gummy bears hack using soda

A short reel showing a "frozen gummy bears" hack, with the creator testing whether it works. Gummy bears are shown being drained through a strainer and then displayed frozen on a tray, and the creator tastes one.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Put gummy bears in soda and leave them for 24 hours.
2. Drain the gummy bears through a strainer over a bowl.
3. Freeze the gummy bears for 3 hours.
