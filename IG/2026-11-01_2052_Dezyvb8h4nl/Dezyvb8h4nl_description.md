# Debunking the Scorpio and sex myth in astrology

The speaker explains that linking Scorpio with sex is a modern European development, not part of the older astrological tradition. He contrasts it with the Islamicate tradition, using a Persian Zodiac Man image that places Scorpio at the genitals.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. In Zodiac Man diagrams, each sign is assigned to a body part, and Scorpio is assigned to the genitals.
2. In medical astrology, Scorpio's link to the genitals does not mean it signifies the act of sex.
3. In an interrogation horoscope, Scorpio with medical indicators points to something affecting the genitals or anus.
4. The Islamicate astrological tradition ran continuously from 9th-century Baghdad into the early 20th century, so its correspondences were not flattened the way they were in Europe.
5. The European tradition had repeated periods where astrology died out and was revived, and during revivals practitioners assembled fragments.
6. The association of sex with death (le petit mort) is, according to the speaker, a 19th-century European fabrication, dated to 1882 in the textual record, and not an ancient Greek idea.
7. The Scorpio-sex link therefore comes from that later European tradition and does not reflect the wider astrological tradition.

## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. The Zodiac Man point and the 1882 date for "la petite mort" (orgasm sense) hold up. The wider claims are overstated: 1882 is only the first recorded use in the OED, and the Scorpio-sex link is stated without evidence.
