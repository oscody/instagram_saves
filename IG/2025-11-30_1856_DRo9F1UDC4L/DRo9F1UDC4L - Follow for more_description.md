## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. The Meta certificates are real on Coursera, but they are paid (subscription) programs with a free trial or financial aid, not free. The post's "free" claim is overstated, and I found no Meta Full-Stack certificate.
* Checked: 2026-10-23 by claude-sonnet-5-5

### Resources

* [Meta Professional Certificates on Coursera (coursera.org)](https://www.coursera.org/meta-professional-certificates): page, found by search, not opened, medium confidence. Search result listing Meta's professional certificates, including Back-End (beginner, 9 courses, ~8 months), which matches the post's course list.
* [Meta Back-End Developer Professional Certificate (coursera.org)](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/meta-back-end-developer): page, found by search, not opened, medium confidence. Coursera page for one of the five listed courses; it includes the Programming in Python course.

### Key steps

1. Open the Meta professional certificates page on Coursera and pick a program.
2. Click Enroll and start the free trial, or apply for financial aid, to avoid paying the full price.
3. Complete the courses to earn the certificate; the Back-End program is about 8 months at 6 hours a week.

### Not found

I did not open the Coursera pages, so none are marked verified. I found no Meta Full-Stack certificate. I did not check the exact current price or free-access terms. The post gives its links only to people who comment "Meta", but the courses are public on Coursera.
