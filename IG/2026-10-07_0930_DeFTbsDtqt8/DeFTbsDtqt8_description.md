# Jamaican proverb "Duppy know who fi frighten"

The post presents the Jamaican Patois proverb "Duppy know who fi frighten" as its phrase of the day. It gives the literal meaning, a usage example, and a line-by-line translation.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Proverb meaning: bullies know whom they can intimidate; like a duppy (ghost) choosing whom to scare, people who threaten others often target those they think will not fight back.
2. Example usage: "Him cyaa try mi — duppy know who fi frighten."
3. Translation of the example: "He cannot intimidate me — bullies know whom to target."
