## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. Trapp does make coconut shredders (TRC-40 electric, TRC-50 petrol) for green or dry coconut, so the product type is real. I found no page for a "TRC 500", and the "extra income" claim is the poster's opinion with no figures.
* Checked: 2026-10-22 by claude-sonnet-5-5

### Key steps

1. Check the model with Trapp or a dealer: the reel says TRC 500, but only the TRC-40 (electric) and TRC-50 (petrol) turned up in search.
2. Shred coconuts that have no water in them, as the manual recommends.
3. Before starting, check the feed for stones, glass or other things the machine can't shred.
4. The machine ships without a power switch or motor wiring, so the buyer has to add them. The wiring diagram is on the motor.
5. The petrol TRC-50 ships without oil or fuel, so fill both before the first run.
6. Use Trapp technicians for spare parts and servicing.

### Not found

I found no official Trapp page, price or availability for a "TRC 500". I did not open any of the search results, so the manual details come from search summaries only. There is no evidence for the income claim.
