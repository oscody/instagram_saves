# Mermaid foraging and sea swim with mindset quotes

A mermaid-themed reel that pairs a foraged food plate and a sea swim in Gqeberha with a spoken mindset monologue about thoughts, emotions, habits and choices. The caption credits the foraging workshop and sea session partners and the original audio creator.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Treat emotions as signals: fear shows what matters, anger shows which boundaries were crossed.
2. Treat anxiety as a sign you are stuck in the future and come back to the present.
3. Change your habits rather than relying on motivation, because habits are what shift your life.
4. Treat your past as one chapter: learn from it, but live in the present.
5. See a trigger as a mirror showing what is still unhealed in you.
6. Recognise control as an illusion; your real power is your choices.
7. Remember that your thoughts shape you, your habits shape you, and your choices define you.
