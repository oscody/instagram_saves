* Verdict: Partly valid. The 1983 CIA-held document is real (McDonnell's Gateway Process assessment, declassified 2003). It's an Army assessment of a Monroe Institute meditation program, not a CIA finding that reality is a simulation. The "page 25" is missing from the official copy, and the circulating version is unconfirmed.
