# Top 5 Free Asset Websites for Game Development

An infographic listing five free asset websites for game developers, each with its best-for category and sample assets: Kenney, OpenGameArt, itch.io, Mixamo and Poly Pizza. It ends with a tip that free assets suit learning, game jams and prototypes, and can be swapped for your own or licensed art as a project grows.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Use free assets to build prototypes, learn, and take part in game jams.
2. As the project grows, replace free assets with your own art or licensed assets.

## Caption

🎮 Top 5 Free Asset Websites for Game Development 🚀

You don't need a big budget to build amazing games.
