## What it teaches

1. Watch Kallaway's 11-minute video on becoming a master storyteller, which covers how other companies use storytelling in marketing and how word count within sentences affects how a story is received.
2. Watch Adrian Per's 13-minute video, which reframes storytelling as a repeatable skill you can learn like any other, using a unique and repeatable structure.
3. Watch Phillip Humm's 9-minute video, which the creator describes as cutting through over-complicated advice and leaving an actionable framework you can reuse for future stories.
4. Read Chinua Achebe's Things Fall Apart and note that it goes deep into one culture's truth instead of trying to appeal to everyone.
5. Build content from a unique perspective or fresh idea based on your own experience rather than generic content.
