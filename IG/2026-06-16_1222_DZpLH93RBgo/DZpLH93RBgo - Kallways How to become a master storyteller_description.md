### Key steps

1. Humm's 9-minute video teaches five techniques: location, actions, thoughts, emotions and dialogue.
2. Location: say where you are, and skip extra detail.
3. Actions: use clear verbs for what you were doing.
4. Thoughts: share raw, honest thoughts, not polished ones.
5. Emotions: show them through physical details, such as a tapping pen or a glance at the clock.
6. Dialogue: quote the key moment briefly and vividly.
7. Kallaway: replace 'and then' with 'but' or 'therefore', vary sentence length, and keep a conversational tone.
