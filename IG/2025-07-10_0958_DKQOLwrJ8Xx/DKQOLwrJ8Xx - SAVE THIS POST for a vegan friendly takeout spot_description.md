SAVE THIS POST for a vegan friendly takeout spot near Central Park!✨🌳

Plant Blossom in Hell’s Kitchen is my go-to spot when I’m in the mood for lunch at the park, and it’s only a 10 minute walk! 

The menu is vegetarian & vegan - all vegan options are labeled and there’s TONS. They have everything from pancakes to burgers and everything I’ve tried so far has been solid🔥

My faves to get are the vegan satay skewers, beyond cheesesteak garlic sub, and the beyond vegan burrito 🌯 

If you’re in the area be sure to check them out for tasty vegan food!🌱

📍 881 10th Ave, New York (open daily til 9pm)
