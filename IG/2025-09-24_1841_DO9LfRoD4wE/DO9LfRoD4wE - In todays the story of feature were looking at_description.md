# Jamaica Town: Jamaican Legacy in Limón, Costa Rica

A six-slide history feature on Jamaica Town in Limón, Costa Rica. It covers the 1872 arrival of the Lizzie with 123 Jamaican railroad workers, the later settlement and citizenship struggles, and the local Mekatelyu language, Jamaican cuisine and football links.
