# Using AI to find business blind spots and fixes

The creator argues that AI can either hurt or grow your learning, depending on discipline. He describes a workflow that uses AI to read your business software data for problems, then to find how others in your niche solved them. The full prompts are only sent after a comment.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Connect your business software to the AI you use.
2. Run a prompt that has the AI analyze your software data and find blind spots, the problems going wrong in your business.
3. Have the AI save those problems to a table.
4. Run a second prompt that has the AI find where experts in your niche discuss those problems.
5. Have the AI find examples of people who solved your problems and explain how they did it.
