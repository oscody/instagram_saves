# Chinese medicine Jing, Qi, Shen and eye signs

A reel explaining the Chinese medicine model of Jing, Qi and Shen, shown with a diagram of the body. It then describes what bright eyes and under-eye dark circles or bags are said to indicate about a person's vitality.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Jing is the body's stored sexual or life-force energy; when it is transmuted, it becomes Qi, the vitality of the life force.
2. Qi is transmuted into Shen, which is expressed through your actions and words and determines your spirit in the physical plane.
3. Bright, clear, shining eyes indicate strong Shen, strong Qi and vitality, meaning Jing is being transmuted into Shen cleanly and healthily.
4. Dark circles, swelling or bags under the eyes are linked to kidney deficiency, since the kidney is said to store and manage Jing.
5. Dark circles can follow periods of intense sexual release, but the post frames them as a sign of an issue managing life force and possibly a karmic pattern or self-sabotage.
