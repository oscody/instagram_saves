# How to keep going with nothing left

A tongue-in-cheek reel with memes and on-screen text about getting through an overwhelming pile of tasks. It shows a spin-the-wheel app picking a task, a 50-minute timer, and a dog in a face mask for the shower step.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. When there are too many tasks and you can't decide, list them and spin a random task wheel to pick one.
2. Brain dump your tasks by category into a visual task menu, then work through them across the week.
3. For small, random, unrelated tasks, set a 50-minute timer and batch them together.
4. Finish the batch and cross items off your to-do list to get a dopamine boost.
5. Take care of the basics one at a time: go for a walk, drink water, shower, and have a good meal.
6. If you need to cry, do it, then go back to handling one thing at a time.
