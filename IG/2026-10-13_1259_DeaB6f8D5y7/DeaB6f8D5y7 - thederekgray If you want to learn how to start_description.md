## What it teaches

1. Step 1, find your first client: search a service plus a city on Google Maps and look for businesses with 4+ star reviews that are running paid ads.
2. Step 2, send them a 2-minute Loom showing that 60% of clicks go to the top 3 Google Maps businesses (organic listings, not sponsored), then offer to rank them for a fraction of what they spend on ads.
3. Step 3, deliver results: get access to their GBP profile and connect it to Search Atlas or Claude, which handles weekly posts, review responses, photo optimization and keyword updates.
4. Step 4, stack clients: repeat the same outreach, AI tools and delivery for each client; the post uses $500/month per client, so 20 clients equals $10,000/month.
5. Step 5, upsell existing clients once they rank: website builds, social media management, and Facebook or Google Ads management (listed as a whitelabel upsell at +$1,500/month).
