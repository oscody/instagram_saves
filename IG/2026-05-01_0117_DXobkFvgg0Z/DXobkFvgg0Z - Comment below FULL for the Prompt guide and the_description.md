# Print-on-demand niche research with Claude and Printify

A short reel showing how to use a Claude prompt to find print-on-demand sub-niches and high-margin Printify products, then filter Printify's bestsellers by margin. The caption offers a prompt guide and full tutorial to anyone who comments "FULL".

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Open Claude (Sonnet) and send a prompt asking for a plan to reach $100k in 12 months with print on demand using Printify and Claude, covering what has worked in each niche, current trends, and sub-niches based on Pinterest trends and Etsy bestsellers.
2. Review the top sub-niches Claude returns, such as rising categories like wellness and fitness, digital nomad or work lifestyle, and retro or vintage aesthetics.
3. Reply to Claude to select a niche, then have it list the Printify products with the best margins and delivery speed, such as unisex t-shirts, ceramic mugs and posters.
4. On the Printify site, open the Bestsellers page and use Filters & Sorting to sort the items by best sellers with maximum margins, then show the results.
