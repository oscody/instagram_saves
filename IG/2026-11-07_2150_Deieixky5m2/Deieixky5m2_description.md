# Flip first-date questions from facts to feelings

A dating coach's "Seduction Math #105" reel argues that first dates feel like job interviews when most of the talk is factual. Her whiteboard shows a 70% information and 30% emotion split, which she says should be reversed.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Notice when conversation is mostly information (where are you from, what do you do, how many siblings).
2. Aim for roughly 30% information and 70% emotion: stories, reactions, teasing, and opinions.
3. Use the opinion switch: replace a factual question like "Where do you like traveling?" with an imaginative one like "What's one place you'd drag me to, even if I complained the whole way?"
4. Let the other person imagine something with you and show her personality, so she remembers how she felt rather than the details of your conversation.
