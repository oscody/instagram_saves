# Five LLM prompts for job applications

A carousel that presents five ChatGPT-style prompts for job searching: resume tailoring, cover letters, LinkedIn profile rewrites, networking posts, and interview question prep. The post claims these prompts helped people switch jobs.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Resume tailoring: paste your current resume and the job description, then ask the model to work exact JD keywords into the resume, quantify achievements, keep it to one page in bullet format, and summarize your top 3-5 matches. Ask for the full revised resume plus a list of changes.
2. Cover letter: paste the job description, 3-5 key experiences, and your resume summary. Ask for a 3-paragraph letter with a company-specific hook, fit proven through quantified stories, and a strong interview call to action, in a confident, professional tone with no fluff.
3. LinkedIn profile: paste your current headline and About section, your experience, and the target role. Ask for a first-person, keyword-rich rewrite of the headline and About section that ends with a subtle call to action, plus 3 banner ideas.
4. Networking post: paste a recent achievement and target audience. Ask for a LinkedIn post with a hook in the first line, a short story with lessons, a closing question to drive comments, and 3-5 relevant hashtags.
5. Interview prep: paste the job description and the company and role. Ask for 15 likely questions (5 technical, 5 behavioral, 5 company/role-specific), each with why it is asked and a suggested answer framework.
