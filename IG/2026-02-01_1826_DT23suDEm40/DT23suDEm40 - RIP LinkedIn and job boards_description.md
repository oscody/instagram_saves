# Five LLM prompts for job applications

A seven-slide carousel with five LLM prompt templates for job hunting: resume tailoring, cover letters, LinkedIn profile rewrites, a networking post, and interview question prediction. The final slide is a call to comment for a separate prompt guide.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Resume tailoring: paste your current resume and the job description, then ask the LLM to rewrite the resume with exact keywords from the job description, quantified achievements, a one-page bullet format, and a summary of your top 3–5 matches. Ask for the full revised resume plus a list of changes.
2. Cover letter: paste the job description, 3–5 key experiences, and your resume summary, then ask for a three-paragraph letter with a company-specific hook, fit proven through quantified stories, and a strong interview call to action. Set the tone to confident, professional, and enthusiastic, with no fluff.
3. LinkedIn profile: paste your current headline and About section, your experience, and your target role, then ask for a rewritten headline and About section that is first-person, keyword-rich for recruiter searches, and ends with a subtle call to action. Also ask for three banner ideas.
4. Networking post: give the achievement and target audience, then ask for a post with a hook in the first line, a short story with lessons, a closing question to drive comments, and 3–5 relevant hashtags.
5. Interview prep: paste the job description and company/role, then ask for 15 likely interview questions (5 technical, 5 behavioral, 5 company/role-specific), each with why it is asked and a suggested answer framework.
