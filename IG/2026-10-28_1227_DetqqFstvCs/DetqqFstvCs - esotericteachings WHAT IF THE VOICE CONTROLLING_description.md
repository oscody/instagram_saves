# The Three Voices Within the Mind

A carousel framing the ego, the adversary and the Holy Spirit as three inner voices competing for influence over consciousness. It contrasts how each voice speaks and says the silent observer can stand above them.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Notice the ego's language of identification (I, me, mine): a passing experience such as anger or fear gets attached to who you are.
2. Notice the adversary's voice, which reasons with you and explains why a craving, anger or fear should be acted on or avoided.
3. Look for the Holy Spirit's simple knowing beneath the argument, which points toward truth, conscience and doing what is right.
4. Observe from the silent observer instead of collapsing into what you observe, so you can recognize which voice is speaking.
