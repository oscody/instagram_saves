# Three-prompt ChatGPT chain for a first customer

Shows three ChatGPT prompts in sequence: one that forces a single offer and marketing channel, one that sets the AI to act as a world-class expert in that niche, and one that asks for a 90-day plan to a first paying customer. A screen recording of a ChatGPT chat appears in the reel.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Prompt 1 (constraint): ask what the fastest way is to hit a $1 million goal based on your background and skills, and limit the answer to exactly one offer and one marketing channel.
2. Prompt 2 (mentor): once the path is set, tell the AI to pretend to be the world-class expert in that niche and adopt their mindset and tone.
3. Prompt 3 (90-day sprint): ask for a very simple 90-day action plan that needs under $1,000 upfront, has a 95% chance of success, gets you to your first paying customer as soon as possible, and includes specific outreach actions for Week 1.
