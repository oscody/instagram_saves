# Recall AI knowledge base demo for saved content

Matt Farmer demos Recall AI, a tool that saves YouTube videos, articles, PDFs and podcasts into a searchable knowledge base, with a browser extension summary, chat across saved content, and bookmark import. He promotes it as free to try at getrecall.ai.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## Source

* Link: getrecall.ai

## Research

* Verdict: Valid. Recall is a real product with the features shown (save, summarize, chat). The getrecall.ai link redirects to recall.it. The "free to try" claim holds, but a paid Premium tier exists and I couldn't confirm the free-tier limits or bookmark import.
* Checked: 2026-10-23 by claude-sonnet-5-5

### Resources

* [Recall (getrecall.ai → recall.it) (recall.it)](https://www.recall.it/): product, in the post, verified, high confidence. The page describes an AI knowledge base that saves videos, podcasts, PDFs and articles, with browser extensions, summaries and chat across saved content.

### Key steps

1. Go to getrecall.ai (redirects to recall.it) and sign up free; no credit card is needed.
2. Install the browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) or the iOS/Android app.
3. Save a video, article, PDF or podcast with one click.
4. Read the summary, then chat with a single item or across your saved content.
5. Look for the bookmark import option in the app; the homepage doesn't mention it.

### Not found

Pricing, free-tier limits and bookmark import were not shown on the homepage, so I didn't confirm them. I didn't open the pricing page.
