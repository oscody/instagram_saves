# Seven ChatGPT prompts to find paid skills

A ten-slide carousel presenting seven prompts for ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, using Deep Research for web-based checks and normal chat for planning, to find skills-based income ideas and test them. It says AI can find demand but cannot promise income.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Turn on Deep Research: in ChatGPT, open the tools menu in the message box and pick Deep research. Free accounts get a limited number of runs; Claude (Research) and Gemini (Deep Research) work the same way.
2. Map your money (normal chat): have the AI interview you one question at a time, up to 12 questions, about your job, skills, tools, what people ask you for help with, hobbies, free hours per week, budget and income goal. Then list 15 ways to make money online and offline, score each 1 to 5 on fit, demand, earning potential, speed to first dollar and cost to start, point out where you have an edge, and show a table with the best 3 on top.
3. Find the demand (Deep Research): search Reddit, niche forums, Upwork and LinkedIn job posts from the last 6 months for people who need help with your skill. Count only posts where someone says they would pay, already pay or hired someone. Build a table of 20 examples with link, date, what they wanted, budget if mentioned and their exact words, then name the 3 problems that come up most.
4. Check real prices (Deep Research): for your service and country, pull real prices from Upwork, Fiverr, agency sites and public price lists. Split them into hourly, per project and monthly retainer, note what each price includes, show low, typical and premium with sources, then say where someone with your background should start and why.
5. Find the gap (Deep Research): find the 5 most popular products or services like yours, read their 1 to 3 star reviews on Trustpilot, Reddit, G2 and app stores, and list the 10 complaints that repeat, with a real quote, a link and how often each appears. Pick the 3 you could fix with your skill and write how you'd say that in one line.
6. Build the offer (normal chat): using the research, turn the strongest idea into one offer covering who it's for, the exact result, what's included, delivery time and a guarantee you can afford. Give basic, standard and premium prices, write a one-page sales message in the exact words buyers used (no hype, no fake urgency), then list the skills you still need and the biggest risk.
7. Find your first buyers (Deep Research): find where your ideal customer hangs out online now (subreddits, Facebook groups, Discord servers, hashtags, newsletters), rank them by activity, check each one's self-promotion rules, give the top 5 with links, and write 3 first messages you can send this week that start a conversation instead of pitching.
8. Test it in 7 days (normal chat): ask for a day-by-day 7-day plan to test the offer before building anything, covering what to post, who to message and how many. Set clear numbers for a yes (such as replies, calls booked and paid deposits) and the point where you drop the idea and move to idea #2. At the end, share your results and ask what to change.
9. Rule: AI finds the demand but cannot promise income, so run the Test step before spending money.
