# Seven ChatGPT Deep Research prompts to find income

A 10-slide carousel presenting seven prompts for using ChatGPT's Deep Research mode, alongside normal chat, to map skills, find demand, check prices, and test an offer. It says AI can find demand but cannot promise income.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Open the tools menu in the ChatGPT message box and pick Deep research (free accounts get a limited number of runs; Claude Research and Gemini Deep Research work similarly).
2. Prompt 1 (normal chat): ask the AI to interview you one question at a time, up to 12 questions, about your job, skills, tools, what people ask you for help with, hobbies, free hours per week, budget and income goal.
3. Then have it list 15 ways to make money, online and offline, score each 1 to 5 on fit, demand, earning potential, speed to first dollar and cost to start, point out where you have an edge, and show the best 3 on top in a table.
4. Prompt 2 (Deep Research): search Reddit, niche forums, Upwork and LinkedIn job posts from the last 6 months for people who need your skill; count only posts where someone says they would pay, already pays or hired someone; list 20 examples with link, date, what they wanted, budget and their exact words; then name the 3 problems that come up most.
5. Prompt 3 (Deep Research): find what freelancers, agencies and course creators charge for your service in your country, using Upwork, Fiverr, agency sites and public price lists; split prices into hourly, per project and monthly retainer; show low, typical and premium with sources; then say where someone with your background should start and why.
6. Prompt 4 (Deep Research): find the 5 most popular products or services like yours, read their 1 to 3 star reviews on Trustpilot, Reddit, G2 and app stores, list the 10 repeating complaints with a quote, link and frequency, then pick the 3 you could fix with your skill and write each as a one-line pitch.
7. Prompt 5 (normal chat): turn the strongest idea into one offer covering who it's for, what's included, delivery time, result, a guarantee you can afford, and basic, standard and premium prices, with no hype or fake urgency; then list the skills you still need and the biggest risk.
8. Prompt 6 (normal chat): find first buyers by ranking relevant Discord servers, newsletters and communities by activity, checking each one's rules on self-promotion, then writing first messages that start a conversation instead of pitching.
9. Prompt 7 (normal chat): test the idea in 7 days by setting a clear daily post or message target and tracking numbers such as conversations, calls booked, replies and deposits, then reviewing results and changing the offer based on them.
