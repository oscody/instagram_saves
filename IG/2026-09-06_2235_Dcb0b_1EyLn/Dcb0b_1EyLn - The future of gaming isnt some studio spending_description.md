## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. The product exists and has a free tier. It's mainly a multiplayer backend with a browser Game Builder, and the site doesn't clearly describe the friends-chatting-together flow. The "future of gaming" line is opinion.
* Checked: 2026-10-22 by claude-sonnet-5-5

### Resources

* [Multiplayer Agent (multiplayeragent.com)](https://multiplayeragent.com): product, in the post, verified, medium confidence. The site offers a browser Game Builder and a multiplayer backend with a free tier (1,000 players/month, 3 games, no card). It doesn't explicitly show the shared friend chat session from the reel.

### Key steps

1. Go to multiplayeragent.com and start on the free plan (no card needed).
2. Open the Game Builder and describe your game to the agent.
3. Play the result from a shared link and invite friends.
4. Optionally, connect Claude Code, Cursor or another MCP client to its tool server (12 tools) to build with your own agent.
