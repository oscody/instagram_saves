# A Claude skill that updates your other skills

A creator describes a Claude skill that runs at the end of a session, scans what was corrected, explained twice, or said but never written down, and proposes updates to your other skills. Screen mockups show a chat where a hook was refined over four turns and a skills folder being cleaned up.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. At the end of a session, scan the whole conversation for corrections, things you had to explain twice, and preferences stated out loud that never made it into a skill file.
2. Diagnose what you found in two groups: what the session reveals about how you think and decide, and which patterns or formats kept breaking and need to be locked in.
3. Send learning about how you think and decide to one skill, and recurring patterns and formats to another skill.
4. Only make an edit once a correction has come up twice in sessions; a single correction in one session is not enough.
5. Before any change, show the exact lines that will change and why, and make surgical edits rather than overhauls.
6. Periodically clean house: cut skills that have gone stale and merge skills that have become duplicates.
7. Approve each change yourself before it is written, so you remain the one who decides.
