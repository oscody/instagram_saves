### Key steps

1. You don't need this specific product. Any small home projector that can mirror a phone or tablet screen can show an ebook on a wall.
2. Open the book in an e-reader app (Kindle, Apple Books, Google Play Books) on the phone or tablet.
3. Mirror the screen to the projector by Chromecast or AirPlay, through a Fire TV or Chromecast stick, or with an HDMI adapter.
4. Use a dark room and a light, plain wall, and set the e-reader to sepia or a large font so the text stays readable when blown up.
5. Turn pages from the phone in your hand. The A/B/C ON/OFF remote in the reel is a generic RF outlet-switch remote, not a page turner.
