# Project your book onto the wall

A reel showing a white projector aimed at a bed and a sofa, with a book's pages and a Yule Ball illustration projected onto a living room wall. The caption promotes projecting books onto a wall for reading from bed or couch.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## Caption

Some nights you tell yourself “just one more chapter…” and suddenly it’s way past your bedtime. 📖✨

But after hours of reading, your neck starts hurting, your arms get tired, and finding a comfortable position feels impossible. You keep shifting around instead of actually enjoying the story.

What if you could just sit back and read without holding anything?

✨📚 Project your book onto the wall, get comfortable, and enjoy every page on a much bigger screen. Read from your bed or couch while staying completely relaxed.
It turns your favorite books into a cozy, immersive reading experience — perfect for those nights when you just can’t put the story down. 🤍
