### Key steps

1. Play with 2–4 players. Each has 4 pieces, and the game uses two dice.
2. Roll one die to see who goes first. The highest roll starts.
3. Dice must land on the board. A die that rests off the board ends your turn.
4. You need a 6 to bring a piece onto the track. A 6 also gives you an extra turn.
5. Move pieces clockwise by the number rolled.
6. Two or more of your pieces on the same square form a block. Only you can pass it, and it can't be captured.
7. Landing on an opponent's piece that isn't in a block sends it back to base.
8. Form a block at the entrance to your home section. Then move pieces home using one die or both dice combined. The first player with all four pieces home wins.
