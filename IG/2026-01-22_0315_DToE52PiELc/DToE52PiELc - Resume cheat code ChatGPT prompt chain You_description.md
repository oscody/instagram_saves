# Three-prompt resume chain for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini

A short reel walks through a three-prompt sequence for improving a resume with an AI chatbot, run in one conversation, followed by a bonus prompt for interview prep. On-screen text repeats the "three prompt chains" and "bonus prompt" lines.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Use ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and run all the prompts in the same conversation.
2. Paste your resume and the job description, then ask the AI to act as a senior recruiter, analyze the resume against the job description, give a match score out of 100, and list your top five missing keywords.
3. In the same chat, ask the AI to rewrite your experience section to naturally include those keywords, using the Google XYZ formula: accomplish X, as measured by Y, by doing Z.
4. Still in the same chat, ask the AI to act as an applicant tracking system filter, scan the new resume, and tell you which sections a bot would struggle to read.
5. Bonus: ask the AI to act as the hiring manager for the role, ask you the three hardest technical questions, then give a strong response to each based on your background.
