# Three LinkedIn hacks for finding jobs faster

A former LinkedIn hiring-product builder shares three quick LinkedIn tweaks for job seekers, plus a bonus search tip for finding hiring posts. On screen are a LinkedIn job search with the Past 24 hours filter and the "Who's viewed your profile" analytics page with its company filter.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Hack #1: Search for any job on LinkedIn, open the URL, and change the time-filter value from 86400 (past 24 hours) to 3600 to see only jobs posted in the last hour, so you can apply first.
2. Hack #2: Check your profile photo visibility setting and make sure it is on for all, not turned off or limited to your connections, which the post says can raise recruiter reach-outs.
3. Hack #3: Open the profile analytics (who viewed your profile), set the date range to past 365 days, and filter viewers by company to see if anyone from your dream company viewed your profile.
4. Bonus: Search "I'm hiring + [your job title]", then use Filter and choose Posts to find hiring managers posting roles before they reach job boards.
5. Save the post and tag a friend who is job hunting.
