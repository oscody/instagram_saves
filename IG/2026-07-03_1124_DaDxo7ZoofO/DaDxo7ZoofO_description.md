# Remember: colour pairings that look more polished

A short reel with two people in a living room, each pair in matching outfits, with the on-screen word "REMEMBER" repeated. It shows colour combinations and how adding one accent colour changes the look.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Brown and pink look nice, but adding a pop of red makes the outfit more interesting.
2. Caramel and powder blue look good, but adding olive makes the outfit look elevated.
3. Charcoal, gray and ivory work, but adding yellow makes the outfit more vibrant.
4. Powder blue looks beautiful, and adding more olive makes it look richer.
