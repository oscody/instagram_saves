# Outsourcing thinking to AI in engineering

Two podcast hosts discuss an engineer on their team who posts mostly AI-generated text, which they turn down because it adds no value and anyone can get the same output from a model. They also warn that unchecked AI mistakes can cause serious harm, such as service downtime, and say an upcoming episode will cover the AI software development life cycle.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Don't share AI-generated text as-is when it's copied word for word from the model and adds nothing of your own.
2. Add your own incremental value on top of AI output, since anyone can get the same result from the same model.
3. Follow through on AI output and check it, because unchecked AI mistakes can compound and be catastrophic.
