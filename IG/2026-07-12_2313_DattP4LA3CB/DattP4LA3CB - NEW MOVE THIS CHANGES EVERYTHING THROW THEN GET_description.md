# Boxing drill: throw, step off the line, throw again

Two people practice a boxing drill on a yellow tape line outdoors, with one throwing punches and the other stepping out of the way. The caption calls it a new move: throw, then get off the line and throw again.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Throw the two (cross), then follow with a body shot.
2. When you see the incoming punch, step out of its path and keep your shoulder up to guard.
3. Pivot on the right foot and turn your whole body to get inside the opponent's range.
4. Throw an uppercut right after the turn.
5. Get off the line and throw again, repeating the sequence.
