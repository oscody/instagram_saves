# Technique pour rentrer un t-shirt

A short reel showing a man in a dark tee, a patterned scarf and beige trousers tucking his t-shirt into the waistband. Bilingual French and English captions and on-screen text name the move and show the same action on both sides and at the back.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Tuck the t-shirt into the waistband of the trousers, as the on-screen title 'how to tuck in your tee perfectly' describes.
2. On one side, pinch the hem with both hands and tuck it into the waistband.
3. Repeat the same movement on the other side of the shirt.
4. At the back, grab the hem with both hands and tuck it in so the shirt sits smooth against the trousers.
