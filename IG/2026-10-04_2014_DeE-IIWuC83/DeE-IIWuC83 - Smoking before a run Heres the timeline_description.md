# Timeline of smoking weed before a run

A selfie-style reel in which a man outdoors describes what happens in the body, minute by minute, after smoking weed before a run. It moves from the first puff through the flow-state window to the return to baseline.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Around 9 minutes after the first puff, blood THC peaks.
2. Around 10 minutes in, heart rate rises by about 20 to 30 extra beats per minute as THC pushes it higher.
3. Around 30 minutes, the runner's high builds and you may reach a flow state, where time disappears and miles stop hurting.
4. At 45 minutes you still feel fogged, with the heart still running high.
5. At about an hour the effects are fading, but the heart can still be running high.
6. Two hours in, you are mostly back to baseline, though the heart may stay elevated for up to three hours.
7. Rule: know your body, know your dose, and enjoy the run.
