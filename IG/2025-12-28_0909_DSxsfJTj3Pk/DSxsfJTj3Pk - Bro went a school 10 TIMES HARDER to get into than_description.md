# IIT Bombay founder's street interview on success

A host interviews a man on a waterfront street with a bridge behind them about his IIT Bombay background, his company's earnings, rejecting YC, and his marriage. He shares his views on roles, failure, and negotiating in relationships.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Anticipate where things are going, not where they are right now (his hockey puck analogy).
2. Treat roles such as entrepreneur, student, or son as characters you play, and picture what the best version of you would do in that role.
3. Don't let a role's setbacks affect you personally: if the character fails, you have not failed.
4. Don't try to fit in; stay unique, especially when young.
5. In relationships, negotiate to something in between, since the effort is not 50-50 but 100% from both sides.
