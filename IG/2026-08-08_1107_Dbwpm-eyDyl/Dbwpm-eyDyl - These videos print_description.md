# Making AI videos for online payouts

A creator in a grey t-shirt talks over a clip of a fiery city disaster, claiming AI videos like it can earn around $10,000 a month. The post walks through a short workflow for making and preparing these videos.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Ask ChatGPT for a cinematic prompt matching the style of video you want.
2. Copy the prompt it gives you.
3. Paste the prompt into a video generator, which the post says produces a video in two to five minutes (it names ViewMax.io).
4. Before posting, use the generator's English voiceover feature and add auto captions.
