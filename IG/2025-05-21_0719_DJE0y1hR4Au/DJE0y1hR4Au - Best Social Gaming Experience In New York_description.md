## Research

* Verdict: Valid. Time Mission is a real venue with a West Nyack, NY location. Its site confirms teams of 2–5 and 60/90/120-minute sessions. The room count differs: the post says 30, the site says 25 or more. The post is a promo for a venue and has no downloadable resource.
* Checked: 2026-11-10 by claude-sonnet-5-5

### Resources

* [Time Mission (timemission.com)](https://www.timemission.com): product, found by search, verified, high confidence. Official site lists a West Nyack, NY location, teams of 2–5, 60/90/120-minute sessions and online booking. It says 25+ rooms rather than 30, and it doesn't list prices.

### Key steps

1. Open timemission.com and pick the West Nyack location.
2. Choose a date and time, and book a 60, 90 or 120 minute session for 2–5 players. Online booking is recommended on weekends, and walk-ins depend on availability.
3. Wear sneakers or flat-soled shoes (the post's tip).
4. Minimum age is 6. Ages 6–13 need an adult with them.
