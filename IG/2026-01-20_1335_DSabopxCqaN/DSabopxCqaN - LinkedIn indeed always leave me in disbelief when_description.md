# Free remote job boards to use instead of LinkedIn

A creator explains that LinkedIn and Indeed keep showing hybrid and in-office jobs even when filtered for remote, and names five free job boards she recommends for remote work in 2026. On-screen captures show the Built In and Himalayas sites.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Don't rely on LinkedIn or Indeed for remote jobs, since many listings marked remote are hybrid or in-office.
2. Try NoDesk, which lists positions from around the world, though it suits people with about five or more years of experience better because it has fewer entry-level roles.
3. Try We Work Remotely, which she says has the largest selection of remote jobs.
4. On Built In, use the Fully Remote filter, which is not a remote-specific board but is useful for tech roles that are not necessarily technical positions.
5. Try Truly Remote, a work-from-anywhere board that lists many roles open to non-U.S. citizens.
6. Try Himalayas, a more niche board that mostly posts remote roles from startups and smaller businesses, especially useful for technical positions.
