# Three ChatGPT prompts to monetize résumé skills

A reel showing three ChatGPT prompts for turning résumé skills, experience, and interests into income ideas, plus a bonus follow-up prompt. The prompts appear on screen and are spoken aloud.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Prompt 1: Paste your résumé and ask ChatGPT for three entrepreneurial, freelance, or side hustle opportunities that use your top skills and experience.
2. Prompt 2: Ask how to create a service, product, or content that solves a specific problem and generates income.
3. Prompt 3: Ask for potential income streams or businesses you could build around your top three passions or interests.
4. Bonus: Take the results from the three prompts and ask ChatGPT for the first three steps to make the idea profitable.

### Key steps

1. Prompt 1: "Using my resume, what are 3 entrepreneurial, freelance, or side hustle opportunities I can pursue using my top skills and experience?" Paste your résumé with it.
2. Prompt 2: "How can I create a service, product, or content that solves [X problem] and generates income?"
3. Prompt 3: "What are potential income streams or businesses I can build around [top 3 passions or interests]?"
4. Bonus: for the ideas you get back, ask "What are the first 3 steps in making this idea profitable?"
