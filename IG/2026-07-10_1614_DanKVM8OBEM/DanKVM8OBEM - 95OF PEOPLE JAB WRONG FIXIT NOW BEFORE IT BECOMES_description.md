# Fixing the chicken wing jab

A shirtless boxer in a park shows the common "chicken wing" jab mistake, with the elbow flared out, and then demonstrates the correct straight jab. On-screen text labels the fault as "The Chicken Wing."

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Keep your elbow tucked in instead of flaring it out like a chicken wing.
2. Throw the jab straight from your face, not from a wide or low position.
3. Bring the jab straight back to your face after each punch.
4. Don't pull the hand away to the side; chuck it straight in.
5. Start slowly until you find the rhythm, then build up speed.
6. Practice the motion in a sequence of repeated jabs (boom, boom, boom).
