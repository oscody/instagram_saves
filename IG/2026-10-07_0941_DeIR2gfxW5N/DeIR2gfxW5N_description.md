# Jamaican proverb: "One one coco full basket"

A "phrase of the day" card defining the Jamaican proverb "one one coco full basket" (pronounced wun wun 'koh-koh full bas-kit) as small, consistent efforts adding up over time. It gives the example "Save likkle every week, one one coco full basket."
