# Seven ChatGPT prompts after installing a "mind"

A person at a desk in blue light says they installed a "mind" into ChatGPT, which then started teaching them things they didn't know they needed. The caption lists seven prompts: ask for a custom list of blind-spot skills, a compressed mentor-style learning session, a personal curriculum, the psychology of people who keep levelling up, legacy-level thinking, original mental models, and a 10-years-ahead training blueprint.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## Caption

1| Teach me what l’ve ignored for too long

Prompt:
Based on what most high performers or successful people wish they had learned earlier in life, create a custom list of 10 critical skills, ideas, or mental models I’ve likely been blind to. Then explain why each one matters in ways l’ve probably underestimated.

2| Collapse a year of wisdom into an hour

Prompt:
You are my personal mentor. Compress the most important lessons from psychology, philosophy, and real-world experience into one power-packed learning session. No fluff-just the raw ideas that shift how I see myself and the world.

3| Show me what school never did

Prompt:
Ignore traditional education. Based on where I am in life, give me a personal curriculum that teaches me how to think clearly, act strategically, communicate persuasively, and adapt faster than my environment. Include the real-world “why” behind each lesson.

4| Decode how people become unstoppable

Prompt:
I want to understand how people break past their limits. Walk me through the psychology, belief systems, and behaviors of someone who consistently levels up-even when things get hard, boring, or overwhelming.
Make it practical and uncomfortable if needed.

5| Teach me like I’m building a legacy

Prompt:
Assume I’m not just trying to grow-I’m trying to build something that matters. Teach me the kind of thinking that scales impact, influence, and income. Go beyond tactics and into the mindset shifts required to lead at the highest level.

6| Create mental models I can’t unsee

Prompt:
Design 5 original mental models that simplify how I make decisions, evaluate people, learn from failure, and spot leverage. Each model should be simple enough to remember, but powerful enough to change how I operate.

7| Train me to make more money

Prompt:
Imagine a version of me that’s operating 10 years ahead using Weliton DeOliveira’s mindset. What would that person already know how to do, think, and unlearn? Based on that, create a training blueprint that upgrades my thinking in a way that makes me impossible to replace.

## Research

* Verdict: Partly valid. The post is just seven generic prompts in the caption. Nothing gets "installed" into ChatGPT, so the "mind" claim is clickbait. The prompts still work as ordinary self-development prompts.
* Checked: 2026-11-10 by claude-sonnet-5-5

### Key steps

1. Paste each prompt from the caption into ChatGPT one at a time.
2. Give ChatGPT context about your life, goals and job first. Prompts 3 and 7 depend on it ('where I am in life').
3. In prompt 7, replace 'Weliton DeOliveira's mindset' with a person or mindset you actually want to model, or drop it. It looks like the creator's own name or a plug.
