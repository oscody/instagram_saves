### Key steps

1. Amber Grant: apply once on ambergrantsforwomen.com. It is for women-owned businesses and awards monthly.
2. Galaxy Grant (Hidden Star): short application for women and minority business owners. Amounts and deadlines change each round, so check the current one.
3. Sky's the Limit: complete your profile, based in the US, Canada or the UK. Winners are chosen by community boosts.
4. Skip Instant Grants: $1,000 awards drawn live several times a week for small business owners. Check Skip's membership requirements.
5. Check each grant's own site for current amounts, deadlines and eligibility before applying.
