# Nigerian founders behind the "Tinder for jobs" app

A six-slide Founded carousel profiles three Nigerian founders of Sorce, an AI app that automates job applications and is dubbed the "Tinder for jobs." It covers the app's reported 500,000 users, its YC application, and how investor Sam Parr publicly backed founder Dada.

### Key steps

1. Open sorce.jobs, or find Sorce in the app store.
2. Create a profile with your skills, work history and preferences.
3. Swipe yes or no on matched roles.
4. The AI applies for the roles you accept: it finds listings, fills forms and writes cover letters.

### Not found

The interview claims (OpenAI, Nvidia, Coca-Cola, Samsung) come from the company and I found no independent check. Sources disagree on the co-founders' names (Daniel Ajayi vs "Damilola Ajayi") and on the launch date. The vault notes that matched were unrelated ("founded" in job descriptions).
