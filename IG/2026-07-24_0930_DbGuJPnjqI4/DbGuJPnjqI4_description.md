# Replacing boring texts with flirty questions

A five-slide carousel of text-message lines meant to replace dull small-talk questions when messaging a woman, with photos of women in caps, headscarves and a gym. The last slide adds a general note on delivery.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Swap "How was your day?" for "Rate your day out of 10 and what would've made it a 10?" to show interest without sounding basic.
2. Text "What's something random that happened today that made you laugh?" to break the pattern of small talk.
3. Ask "What were you thinking about before I texted you?" to build tension, since it's flirty and gets her emotionally engaged.
4. Focus on how you say it, not only what you say: confidence + curiosity = connection.
