### Key steps

1. Go to yorby.ai and start the 3-day free trial. It's paid after that ($50/mo Viral Library, $100/mo Posts with Content Studio), so cancel before the trial ends if you don't want to pay.
2. Copy the URL of a viral TikTok or Instagram video.
3. Open 'Your Personal Content', paste the URL to add the video, then click 'Content Studio' on it.
4. Wait for 'AI is Analyzing' (about 90–120 seconds) to get the breakdown of why it went viral.
5. In the chat, type something like 'remix this content for [your niche]'. In the reel he used 'remix this content for studying niche'.
6. Answer the follow-up questions (e.g. 'Studying Angle', 'Your Format'). It then writes a timestamped script with a hook and shot-by-shot lines.
7. Optional: save a prompt with your brand context under 'Saved Prompts' so you don't repeat it in each chat. You can also use 'Get Feedback' on your own videos.
