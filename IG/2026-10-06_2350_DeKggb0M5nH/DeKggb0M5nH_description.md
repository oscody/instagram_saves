# How to Be Happy as a Scorpio

A whiteboard video by a man who lists areas of life tied to the Scorpio sun sign and what to do or avoid in each. Self-awareness, sense of direction and legacy are listed but left blank.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Avoid being neutral: a Scorpio needs to be fully connected to something and either love it or drop it.
2. Habits: dive deep on progressive ideas every day.
3. Health: transform mind and body constantly, since not changing and growing means you are not healthy.
4. Fun: have passion, because fun for a Scorpio comes from connecting to what they are passionate about, even outside their career.
5. Achievement and praise: feel real achievement when it is clear and obvious there is no competition, meaning you are far ahead of everyone.
