## What it teaches

1. Build the palette from neutrals: cream, navy, gray, brown, white, and muted green.
2. Choose timeless pieces such as nipped polos, button-ups, pleated trousers, loafers, and clean structured jackets.
3. Keep the fit relaxed but polished: nothing too tight and nothing sloppy.
4. Keep details simple: clean shoes, a good wash, good grooming, and clothes that look properly ironed.
