# Buying shares in Trans-Jamaica Highway stake

The Jamaican government is selling its remaining 20% stake in the Trans-Jamaica Highway. The post explains how to apply for shares, at $3.60 each with a 1,000-share minimum, in an offer that opens March 4 and closes March 18 (possibly sooner).

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Open an investment, brokerage or securities account (JMMB or Satchip are suggested) by bringing your ID, TRN, proof of address, source of funds and money for the minimum deposit.
2. Once the account is open, keep the JCSD number you receive, since it is needed for the process.
3. If your account is with JMMB, log into its Moneyline platform and go to the buy/sell stocks page to apply.
4. If your account is with another broker, go to NCB's GoIPO platform, fill out and submit the application form.
5. Apply early, since the offer may close before its stated date, and read the prospectus and terms and conditions before applying.
