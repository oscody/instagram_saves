# Self-reflection as the highest-ROI skill

A young man in a dark shirt talks to the camera about self-reflection and creativity, with an on-screen list of fears, flaws, insecurities, and distractions. A screenshot of a comment box shows him drafting the phrase "Write down all your fears, your flaws, your insecurities."

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Write down your fears, flaws, insecurities, guilt, and pressures on a list.
2. Recognize that these items are distractions.
3. Sit down and write about your thoughts from the day, even if it feels harder than going to the gym.
4. Draw something you found interesting.
5. Make a video note to your future self.
6. Put something into the world that did not exist before.

## Caption

I seriously think the highest ROl skill you can learn in today’s modern age is to self reflect.

Write about your thoughts draw something you found interesting. Make a video note to your future self.

The specifics of what you make doesn’t matter, but as long as you put something into the world that didn’t exist before that is the key that is what will allow you to make significant leaps and bounds in your success
