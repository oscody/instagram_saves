# Earning money with remote-controlled car tracks

A presenter pitches a remote-controlled car business: customers drive RC cars with live camera feeds from a driving station. Footage shows a steering-wheel rig with monitors and a row of small cars on a track, plus a dirt-road shot of a car.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Set up a mini-track outside the city.
2. Build a computer workstation with a steering wheel and pedals to control the cars.
3. Equip each car with a camera that streams live footage to the workstation screen.
4. Let customers drive the cars remotely on the real track.
