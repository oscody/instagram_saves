### Key steps

1. Build a story bible (world book) first: the world's rules, culture and the reasons behind them.
2. Hold a long, challenging back-and-forth with a chatbot (Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude). Press on borders, how religion shapes technology, and why people would accept cybernetic parts.
3. Use the saved conversation transcript as the lore and visual-style foundation.
4. Generate concept art from the bible's descriptions with an AI image tool.
5. Feed the images into an image-to-3D world generator to get a walkable environment (the article doesn't name the tool).
6. Optionally view the result on an Apple Vision Pro headset.
