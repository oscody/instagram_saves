# Catching waves by level: whitewater, shoulder, unbroken

A surfer sorts waves into three levels by how far they have broken and gives a technique for each. The clips show surfers riding whitewater, a shoulder, and an unbroken wave face.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Level 1, whitewater wave (already broken, lots of foam): let the wave do the work, lean forward to accelerate and lean back to slow down.
2. Level 2, broken wave with a shoulder: paddle toward the whitewater where the power is, not away from the peak.
3. Level 2: take three or four big paddle strokes and kick your feet at the same time to get into the face of the wave so you can carve.
4. Level 3, unbroken green-faced wave: don't paddle hard.
5. Level 3: dig your ribs into the wave so the board and body drop down the face and accelerate, like dropping in on a skateboard ramp.
