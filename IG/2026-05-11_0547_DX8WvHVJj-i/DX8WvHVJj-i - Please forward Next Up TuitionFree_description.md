### Key steps

1. Check you are eligible: 18 or older, high school diploma or GED, authorized to work in the U.S., household income within the limit, and a computer with internet of at least 20 Mbps.
2. Go to perscholas.org/apply and complete the 7-step eligibility form: contact details, about you, address, work authorization, your computer, education, household income.
3. If you pass, finish the application in the Per Scholas Experience Portal.
4. Admissions reviews applications by hand and responds within about 5 working days.
5. Pick a location or the remote option, and check the course page for the next cohort start date.
