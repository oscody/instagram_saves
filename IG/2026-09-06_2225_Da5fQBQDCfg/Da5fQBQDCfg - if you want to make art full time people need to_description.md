# Show the person behind the art

A creator talks to the camera with a wireless mic in hand, arguing that finished work alone is not enough for a full-time art career. The on-screen text repeats the points: show the person behind the work, and build a career around the craft.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Share more than the final piece: the story, the process, the struggle, your taste, why you made it, and the person behind the work.
2. You don't have to become an influencer to do this; give people more reasons to care about your work.
3. Trust, consistency, and connection are what turn attention into a career.
