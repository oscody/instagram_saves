# Augment people, don't just automate them

A whiteboard talk arguing that technology has always aimed to augment people rather than automate them, and that automation should serve augmentation. It cites the mouse and a 1980s productivity dip as examples.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Treat automation as a means, not the goal: automate only as a step toward augmenting the person.
2. Judge software by whether it gives a human extra capability, not by how much work it removes.
3. Avoid stopping at automating a human's job; build tools that enhance what the person can do.

## Caption

Everything you automate can become just a tool in a larger augmentation
