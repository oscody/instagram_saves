# Mental reframes to stay calm under stress

A reel where the speaker shares mental reframes for staying calm, drawing on Joe Dispenza's book Breaking the Habit of Being Yourself. She argues that stressing about bad outcomes keeps you emotionally living in that future, and that stress also ages you.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Don't stress about bad outcomes, since that puts you emotionally in the negative future and manifests more stress.
2. Practice being happy and stress-free now, rather than waiting for the reality you want.
3. Get into the emotional frequency of the reality you want, and become the version of you who already has it.
4. Accept that there will always be something to stress about, and decide you will no longer let every problem control your emotional state.
5. Use the phrase 'I choose not to let it affect me.'
6. Remember that stress makes you age and shortens your life, so just breathe.
