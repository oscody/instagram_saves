# Buntopia: vegan and non-vegan Asian fusion in Brooklyn

A restaurant review of Buntopia at 994 Broadway, Brooklyn, an Asian fusion spot serving vegan and non-vegan dishes. It recommends the vegan General Tso's chicken, the Raijin Tiger roll, and the non-vegan Thai tom yum shrimp pasta, and highlights a back zen garden with cherry blossoms and wooden wish plaques.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## Caption

This might be one of the best vegan spots in NYC, and non-vegans will actually love it 🌱 

📍Buntopia
994 Broadway, Brooklyn, NY 11221

It’s an Asian fusion spot that’s serving up both vegan and non vegan dishes. 

The vegan General Tso’s ‘chicken’ was insanely good—even the egg on top is completely vegan. I didn’t expect to love vegan sushi, but the Raijin Tiger roll was packed with flavor. 

If you’re not vegan, the Thai tom yum shrimp pasta is a must. It’s spicy, creamy, and it just works

And they’ve got this hidden zen garden in the back that feels like you went to Japan. 

This is one of those spots where everyone finds something they love. Who would you take here?
