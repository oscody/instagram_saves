## What it teaches

1. Find a niche: ask Claude for 10 local business types that don't use AI for basic manual tasks, and for each, the #1 task AI could automate to save 20+ hours a week or thousands a month.
2. Write a short proposal for a business owner in that niche: explain how you can save them 20+ hours a week, cut costs and bring in more revenue with AI tools, and end with an invitation to a quick 15-minute call.
3. Prepare a discovery call: ask Claude for 5 questions that uncover the prospect's biggest time-wasters, then calculate their monthly savings if AI handled those tasks.
4. Pick the tools: ask Claude for the best AI agents for copywriting, customer support, appointment scheduling and email marketing for the chosen niche, each with a one-line explanation a non-technical owner would understand.
