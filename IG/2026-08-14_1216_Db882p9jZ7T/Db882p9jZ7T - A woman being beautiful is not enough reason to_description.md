# Ten questions to ask before a date

A carousel listing ten questions a man should ask a woman before going on a date, with the answers he should hope to hear and the ones that are warning signs. The last slides are an ad for counseling services, which is not included here.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Ask what she is looking for right now. A clear answer that she is genuinely looking for a relationship is positive; 'let's just vibe and see where it goes' is not.
2. Ask what her last relationship taught her. Green flag: she can discuss her past without making her ex the villain in every sentence.
3. Ask what a healthy relationship looks like to her. Listen for respect, loyalty, communication and partnership, not just 'someone who spoils me'.
4. Ask how she handles disagreements. Green flag: she prefers talking things out. If her answer is basically 'I give silent treatment', reconsider.
5. Ask what she expects from a man in a relationship. If her expectations are only what you must provide and she says nothing about what she brings, that is information.
6. Ask her views on loyalty. Green flag: she understands commitment means boundaries with other people too. 'We're not married so it doesn't count' is a red flag.
7. Ask how important family is to her. You don't need the same family structure, but you should know whether your values are fundamentally compatible.
8. Ask what she is currently working towards. She doesn't need everything figured out, but purpose, ambition and willingness to grow are attractive.
9. Ask what she thinks makes someone a good partner. Notice whether she only describes what she wants or also understands her own responsibilities in love.
10. Ask what made her say yes to getting to know you. Her answer can show whether she is interested in you or only in the attention, the lifestyle, or what she thinks you can provide.
11. Keep the goal in mind: not to interrogate her, but to find out whether you are attracted to each other and compatible with each other.
