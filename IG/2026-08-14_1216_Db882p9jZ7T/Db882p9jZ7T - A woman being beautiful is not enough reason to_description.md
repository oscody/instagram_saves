# 10 Questions to Ask Before a First Date

A carousel that lists ten questions a man should ask a woman before taking her on a date, with the answers it treats as green or red flags. It frames the goal as checking attraction and compatibility before investing time, money and emotions.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Ask 'What are you looking for right now?' A good answer is genuinely looking for a relationship; 'let's just vibe and see where it goes' is a warning sign.
2. Ask 'What did your last relationship teach you?' Green flag: she can discuss her past without making her ex the villain in every sentence.
3. Ask 'What does a healthy relationship look like to you?' Listen for respect, loyalty, communication and partnership, not just 'someone who spoils me'.
4. Ask 'How do you handle disagreements?' Green flag: she prefers talking things out. If she basically says she gives the silent treatment, reconsider.
5. Ask 'What do you expect from a man in a relationship?' If she lists only what you must provide and says nothing about what she brings, that is information.
6. Ask 'What are your views on loyalty?' Check that she understands commitment means boundaries with other people too, and that 'we're not married so it doesn't count' is a red flag.
7. Ask 'How important is family to you?' You don't need her family structure to match yours, but you should know whether your core values are fundamentally compatible.
8. Ask 'What are you currently working towards?' Purpose, ambition and willingness to grow are attractive; she doesn't need everything figured out.
9. Ask 'What do you think makes someone a good partner?' Notice whether she only describes what she wants or also acknowledges her own responsibilities in love.
10. Ask 'What made you say yes to getting to know me?' Her answer can show whether she is interested in you or in attention, lifestyle, or what she thinks you can provide.
11. The goal is not to interrogate her. Attraction gets you on the first date; character and compatibility decide whether you go on a second.
