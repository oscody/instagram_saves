# Responding to a Boss Who Yells in Public

A man in a suit speaks to the camera under the label "Boss Yells (Public Humiliation)" about what to do when a boss raises their voice at an employee in front of coworkers. He walks through three escalating responses.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Do not yell back when a boss raises their voice at you in front of peers.
2. Level 1, the pause: stare at the boss for three full seconds and let the room get uncomfortable.
3. Level 2, the volume correction: calmly say you can hear them fine and that there is no need to raise their voice.
4. Level 3, the walk out: step out of the room until you can both speak like professionals, and say you will talk when they are ready.
5. Teach people how to treat you: accepting yelling means accepting their lack of emotional control.
