# Claude for a recruiter job search

A job seeker shows Claude searching Indeed for 10 Chicago recruiter roles and comparing skill gaps with Microsoft Learn courses, starting from an attached resume. The exact prompt is offered separately by comment.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Attach your resume PDF to a new Claude chat.
2. Paste a prompt asking for 10 full-time recruiter jobs in your city with 6+ years of experience, with apply links.
3. Ask Claude to check the skills those jobs require and find free Microsoft Learn courses or certifications that match the gaps.
4. Ask for a side-by-side breakdown of what each job needs, what Microsoft Learn offers, and which roles to apply to first.
5. Click the + button, open Connectors, and turn on Indeed and Microsoft Learn before running the prompt.
6. Review the results table of roles, companies, and salary ranges, then the top-4 comparison with an apply-first recommendation.
