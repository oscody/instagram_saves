# Guardrails for staying faithful in marriage

A man reacts to a clip from a podcast where a newly married man asks how to be a solid husband. He answers with advice on phones, temptation, and guardrails in a committed relationship.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Delete the numbers of other women from your phone, especially once you are married.
2. Expect temptation. Attractive women may show up, but they won't love or protect you the way your spouse does.
3. Work toward being able to leave your phone with your spouse, ideally so you can trade phones freely.
4. Manage temptation by not putting yourself in tempting situations.
5. Men: avoid one-on-one time with women who are not your wife or a blood relative, with very few exceptions.
6. Women: avoid situations where you get lots of validation and attention from other men.
7. Both partners protect the relationship from outside people and from themselves, so set personal guardrails.
