# Ten out of ten men tips

A reel listing four self-improvement tips for becoming a "ten out of ten" man, with book covers and a photo of a man on screen. It closes with a pitch for the BeFreed app, which turns books into short audio lessons.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Master your frame: read The Charisma Myth by Olivia Fox Cabane and actually apply it.
2. Genuinely get good at one skill until people can't ignore it.
3. Learn how to talk to anyone: reach out to older and younger people without changing who you are.
4. Don't skip psychology books; read them, since they give an edge over people who don't.
