# Door-to-door cold pitch for AI restaurant receptionist

A short in-person sales pitch shown inside a restaurant, offering an AI phone receptionist that books reservations. The caption says the cold approach has produced high conversion rates recently.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Walk in and ask for the general manager.
2. Ask whether the restaurant takes reservations and whether it gets a high volume of calls.
3. Explain that the AI receptionist books phone reservations straight into the restaurant's existing booking systems, such as Yelp and the POS.
4. Say pricing is set case by case and leave your card.
5. Offer a test agent built for that specific restaurant and demonstrate a sample reservation call.
