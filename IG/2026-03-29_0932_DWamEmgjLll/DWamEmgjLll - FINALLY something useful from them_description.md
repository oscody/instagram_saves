# Three websites: study tool, photo location, free apps

A reel listing three websites it calls the most dangerous: one that finds where a photo was taken, one that turns textbooks into listenable podcasts with practice questions (shown on turbo.ai), and one said to offer paid apps for free. The study tool gets the most detail.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Upload your textbook to the study tool (turbo.ai is shown on screen) and it turns it into podcast-style audio to listen to.
2. Interrupt the audio at any point and ask a question.
3. Ask it to explain a topic in a chosen style, such as 'like it's Stranger Things'.
4. Work through the practice questions it generates and keep going until the material is in your head.
5. When you get a question wrong, click 'show explanation' to see the right answer and avoid repeating the mistake.
