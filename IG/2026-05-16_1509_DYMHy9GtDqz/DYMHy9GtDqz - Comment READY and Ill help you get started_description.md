# Flexible solar film and removing adoption barriers

## What it teaches

1. Find the barrier that stops people from adopting a product, such as heavy equipment, drilling, or structural changes.
2. Remove that barrier to make setup effortless.
3. Expect adoption to grow well beyond early adopters once setup is easy.
4. Look for surfaces and customer segments that were previously unusable or excluded.
