### Key steps

1. Go to Aqua City Odaiba, West Building 6F, Minato, Tokyo. It's a 1-minute walk from Yurikamome Daiba Station.
2. Opening hours are 11:00–20:00 on weekdays and 11:00–21:00 on weekends and holidays.
3. Public visits need no booking. Walk up to the counter and pay; cashless payment works. Expect waits when it's busy.
4. Choose a pass. General prices are ¥2,000 for 30 minutes, ¥3,000 for 60 and ¥4,000 for 90. Tokyo residents pay ¥1,500, ¥2,500 and ¥3,500. Admission only is ¥700, or ¥500 for Tokyo residents, and each experience then costs ¥700 (¥500 for Tokyo residents).
5. ROBOT FIGHT battles require age 7+ and height 110 cm+. Children aged 3+ can do the Skeletonics ride, Cyber KASSEN and the assembly workshop.
