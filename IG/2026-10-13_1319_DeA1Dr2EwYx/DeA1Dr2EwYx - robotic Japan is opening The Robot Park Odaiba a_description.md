# Robot Park Odaiba opening and robot battles

A carousel announcing The Robot Park Odaiba in Tokyo, a permanent facility opening October 15, 2026, where visitors can pilot large robotic systems and take part in robot battles. The slides show Skeletronics exoskeleton robots fighting in an arena, with a battle recap in the audio.

## Caption

robotic Japan is opening The Robot Park Odaiba, a new experiential facility where visitors can pilot robots and take part in robot battles. 🤖🇯🇵

The park will feature the Skeletnics exoskeleton robot, which became popular in Hachioji, and will officially open as a permanent attraction in Tokyo’s Odaiba from October 15, 2026.

The experience is designed to let visitors step inside large robotic systems, control their movements, and experience robot battles firsthand, bringing futuristic robotics into a real-world entertainment space.
