# Charisma as conversation formulas, and a caution

The creator describes three conversation formulas he made to understand charisma: how to approach someone, how to liven up a dull conversation, and how to build trust quickly. He closes by warning against relying on them too much.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. To approach someone, start with a compliment, then follow up with a question to start a conversation.
2. To turn a boring conversation into a fun, exciting one for both people, reply with questions, assumptions, or jokes.
3. To build trust with someone very quickly, break the touch barrier, use eye contact, mirror their behavior and what they say, and use active listening cues.
4. Don't become too reliant on these formulas, because conversation is a connection, not a math formula.
