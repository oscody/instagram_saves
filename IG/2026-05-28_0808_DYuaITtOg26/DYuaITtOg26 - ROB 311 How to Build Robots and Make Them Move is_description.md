### Resources

* [michiganrobotics/rob311 (github.com)](https://github.com/michiganrobotics/rob311): repo, in the post, verified, high confidence. Repo holds the ROB 311 syllabus, lectures, HWs, labs, resources and ballbot project, as the post describes.

### Key steps

1. Open the repo and read the syllabus PDF and the 25-session course plan.
2. Watch the Fall 2022 lecture playlist on YouTube, linked from the course plan, starting with lecture 1.
3. Check the Ballbot Bill of Materials in Resources: Raspberry Pi Pico, three Pololu 37D gearmotors, omni wheels, acrylic sheets, a basketball.
4. Work through the Labs folder in order, with the HWs and Lecture Files alongside.
5. Plan for access to a 3D printer, laser cutter or waterjet for some labs.
6. Note that lab 1 (soldering) isn't included and lab 5 is skipped.
