# Free U-Michigan ROB 311 Robotics Course

The post announces that the University of Michigan's ROB 311 course, on building and moving real robots, is now free and open source. It lists the course contents, from mechanical design and control to a ballbot capstone, and points to a GitHub repo.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## Source

* Link: https://github.com/michiganrobotics/rob311

## Caption

ROB 311: How to Build Robots and Make Them Move is now completely free.

Ships with:
• Mechanical design, modeling & fabrication
• Rigid-body kinematics, kinetics & dynamics
• Actuation, sensors & mechatronics
• Control systems, stability & multi-task coordination
• Hands-on labs + full ballbot capstone project (balancing robot on a basketball)
• Complete lecture videos, notes, homework, labs & code

👉 https://github.com/michiganrobotics/rob311
