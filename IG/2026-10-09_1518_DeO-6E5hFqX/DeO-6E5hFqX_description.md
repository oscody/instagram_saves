# Cold-emailing into a dream job at Qatar Energy

The creator recounts how she cold-emailed a manager at Qatar Energy (then Qatar Petroleum), proposed a role that did not yet exist, and was hired for a different open position after the conversation. She closes by saying her career has been designed rather than climbed, and that she has used this approach several times.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Pitch a solution, not a request: show how you think and what value you bring.
2. A no can be a yes: a rejected proposal can open a conversation that leads to a role that does exist.
3. Go straight to people: reach out and build relationships, since one thoughtful conversation with the right person can beat 100 unguided applications.
