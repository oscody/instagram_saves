### Key steps

1. Get a supported headset (Meta Quest 3, 3s or Pro, or Pico 4) and install Sketchar from the Meta Horizon or Pico store.
2. Upload the client's design through Google Drive, app.sketchar.io or the mobile app. This needs the Pro plan ($24.99/month or $79.99/year). The free tier has about 1,000 built-in templates.
3. Open the sketch in the headset and set its size on the wall.
4. Use curved-surface mode for columns or other curved surfaces.
5. Trace and paint over the projected lines, and walk around to check the result.
6. Show the client a preview through the headset before you start painting.
