# Indie game clones re-uploaded with ads and AI

A creator describes two indie games, My Own Hero Party and Bills Must Be Paid, whose unfinished demos were copied, decompiled with AI, filled with ads, and re-uploaded in China. He points to a Polygon article about the Bills Must Be Paid clone, which Steam users reportedly preferred because it had more features.
