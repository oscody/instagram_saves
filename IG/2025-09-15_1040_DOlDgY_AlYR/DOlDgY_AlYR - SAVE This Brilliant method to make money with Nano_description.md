# Selling Nano Banana slogan hoodie designs online

A reel showing AI-generated slogan hoodie designs made in Gemini, with mockups of people wearing the hoodie used as ad images. The caption frames it as a side-hustle idea.

Written by claude-haiku-5-5.

## What it teaches

1. Pick a popular item that sells online, such as oversized hoodies.
2. In Gemini, ask for a hoodie design, logo or art meant to promote a clothing item that helps people meet others.
3. Ask Gemini for slogans that get people to approach and talk to the wearer, and choose one that makes sense.
4. Add the chosen slogan to the design and download the image.
5. Find a trending hoodie listing on a print-on-demand site and upload your design to it.
6. Publish the listing.
7. Create several ad variations of the hoodie worn by people in Gemini.
8. Promote the listing with those ads, then repeat with more items.
